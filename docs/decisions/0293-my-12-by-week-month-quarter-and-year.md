# 2026-09-25 — My 12, by week, month, quarter and year

The Cell Groups report counted a month for a whole scope. The owner wanted, from a spreadsheet
the church already keeps, one row for each of their direct 12 counting everyone under that
leader, split into VIP, 2nd timer, 3rd timer, 4th timer and Regular, for a week, a month, a
quarter and a year. Five questions were settled with the owner one at a time, each shown in
the running application before it was decided.

## The ruling

**1. A week is a month's rule on a shorter period.** Weekly shows the people who came to a Cell
in a Monday-to-Sunday week (decision 0054), each at the stage reached by that Sunday. A week
that has passed never changes; weeks are never added up to make a month.

**2. A quarter and a year are the same rule on a longer one.** Quarters are the calendar's.
Each shows the people who came in it, each at the stage reached by its last day; a period
still running shows the stage so far and says it is open, and one that has not begun is not
offered. This settles which month's classification a year in progress carries, which was open.

**3. Leaders may be shown side by side here, under four conditions**: only the reader's own
direct disciples (or those of the leader they opened), in surname order and never sorted by a
figure, with no row numbers, colour, percentages or bars, and each name opening that leader's
report, which shows their 12. Figures for offers the same names. Where the table shows, the
separate stages frame and the two figure cards go, and recording coverage stays first as one
line linking to Filed reports.

**4. A leader's own Cell group is the people who came to any Cell that leader led in the
period**, once each, by the meeting's responsible leader, so a Cell handed over mid-period
splits by meeting. Two Cells are one group, labelled with how many; a leader with none is
labelled so and shows no numbers.

**5. Each row is that leader's true figure.** Somebody at Cells in two branches is in both rows
and once in the total, and a line takes off each count beyond a person's first; another adds
those in the total who are in no row. So the People column adds up in plain sight.

A whole-church reader's rows are the Network roots, where the Network screen starts them
(decision 0268). They are labelled "Men's Network" and "Women's Network", in that order, rather than by the
pastors' names and surname order, because that reader disciples neither, and each opens that
root's 12.

## Why

It is the table leaders already fill in by hand, and every figure in it keeps section 12's one
rule for a stage. The conditions in 3 keep a table of leaders from becoming the leaderboard
section 13 forbids: a leader reads the people they oversee, never a standing.
