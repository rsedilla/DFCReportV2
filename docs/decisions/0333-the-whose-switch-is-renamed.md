# 2026-10-11 — Record's Whose switch is renamed

Owner ruling, 2026-10-11, made from the Primaries' screens. It amends Section 19 and decision
0311, point 4.

## The ruling

**1. The branch view's choice is *Leaders Under Me*** for every account that sees it. It was
*People I oversee*.

**2. The leader's own choice is *Own Cell* under Cell Group and *Own 12* under DCC.** It was
*Mine*, one word for both halves (decision 0311, point 4).

**3. Only the names change.** What each choice lists, and who sees the switch, are unchanged.
Decision 0317's *People you oversee* stays, though its stated ground, matching this switch, no
longer holds.

## Why

The owner chose words the church uses. *My Network* was set aside because Section 4's Network is
the Men's or the Women's Network, which decision 0318 kept off this kind of label, and *My 12*
for the own choice because under Cell Group it lists the reader's own Cell. *144 Leaders* was
considered for a list that stops two levels down and set aside: Section 17 does not let 12, 144
or 1728 stand for actual counts, and such a list rarely holds 144.
