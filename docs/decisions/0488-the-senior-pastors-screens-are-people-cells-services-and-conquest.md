# 2026-11-14 — The Senior Pastors' screens are People, Cells, Services and Conquest

Owner ruling, 2026-11-14, as Bishop Oriel asked, chosen from screenshots of his screens on a phone
and a laptop, today beside the proposal.

## The ruling

**1. The two Senior Pastors' items are *People*, *Cells*, *Services* and *Conquest*, in that
order**, along the bottom on a phone and a tablet and in the sidebar on a laptop, the same four at
every width. `Record` and `Reports` are hidden from them.

- ***People*** is *# of People*, headed *People*, with no tabs.
- ***Cells*** holds three tabs: *CG Attendance*, *# of Cell Groups* and *# of Cell Leaders*, one row
  at every width, headed *Cells*.
- ***Services*** is *DCC Attendance*, headed *Services*, with no tabs.
- ***Conquest*** is unchanged. Its four tabs, one per goal, are a later stage.

Each keeps its address, its *Monthly* · *Yearly* switch, its month and its *Figures for*; nothing
counted changes. A report's *Yearly* view stays under the item that holds it, as do a Cell's
meetings and members, which lead back to *Cells*.

**2. They open on *Services*.** It is the page they opened on before (`/reports/dcc`), now under
its own name.

**3. `Record` is hidden, not removed.** Its address answers the note decision 0379 gives a page
that is not an account's, and so does a Sunday's checklist under it. The Admin, or a Senior
Pastor's Network Admin, keeps the records for both Networks (decision 0480), and Recording
Progress stays the Admin's and a Full view leader's.

## Why

The Bishop and Pastora read; as heads of the church they need the figures and not the recording
screens, which are the administrators' side of the two Networks. Splitting the one `Reports` item
into three names what each holds in the church's own words, and drops the tab rows a reader had to
work through. Four items sit inside the three to five that phone guidance from Google and Apple
gives a bar along the bottom, every item labelled and one tap away.

Considered and rejected: a side drawer opened from a menu button, which hides every item behind a
tap; *People* as a link above the Reports tabs; and keeping *Reports* with fewer tabs.

## What it does not change

No capability, route or record. A Senior Pastor keeps every read and the capability to record;
only the screens change (Section 1, principle 4). The Admin, a Network Admin and every Leader
account are unchanged.
