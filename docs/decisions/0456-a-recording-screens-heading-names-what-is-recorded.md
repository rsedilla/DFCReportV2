# 2026-11-08 — A recording screen's heading names what is recorded

A leader using the installed app could not tell whether they were recording DCC or a Cell Group
meeting: both screens' headings read only the date, *Sunday 8 November*. The owner also marked the
lines on both screens that took up the phone: the DCC checklist's line under the heading and its
four-line box about locked marks, and on a Cell meeting the same kind of line and box. Every change
below was chosen from screenshots of the app on a phone, in dark mode.

## The ruling

**The heading names what is recorded.** *DCC · Sunday 8 November* on a DCC checklist, *Cell Group ·
Sunday 8 November* on a Cell meeting.

**No line under the heading only explains the screen.** On the DCC checklist, the count of who is
listed and left to mark goes: the box, the section headings and the bar at the foot say it. On a
Cell meeting, *Record what happened…* and, once recorded, the note on what a correction does go,
and so does *Every member is listed and every one needs a mark*, beside the count of those marked.
A line that says something about this meeting, such as that its week has closed or that the
Cell's leader records it, stays.

**What is already recorded is one line, with an ⓘ.** *1 of 2 recorded · Locked* on a DCC
checklist, *Already recorded · Locked* on a Cell meeting, and the recorded summary under it. The ⓘ
opens why on a tap, on a phone too: a stray tap cannot change a recorded mark, and changing one
needs permission to correct records, or *Edit* where the reader holds it. The note opens on a line
of its own under the row, so it never runs off a phone's screen. A meeting whose week has closed
still says so in words, not only behind the ⓘ.

## Why

Headings must say what a page is for, and design systems such as GOV.UK's put the task's name with
the page heading. A date alone named neither task.

Unlike `Record`'s ⓘ (decision 0454), this one shows on a phone: it explains why the marks cannot be
tapped, which a leader meets on the phone, and a tap-to-open ⓘ works there.

With the lines gone, a two-person DCC checklist, and a recorded Cell meeting's two members, fit on
one phone screen.

Considered and rejected: a small caption above the date (*DCC attendance*), which is clear but adds
a line of the kind `Record` has just lost.
