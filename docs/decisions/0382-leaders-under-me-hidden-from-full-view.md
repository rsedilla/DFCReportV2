# 2026-10-21 — Leaders Under Me is hidden from a Full view leader

`Record`'s *Update Records* has a *Whose* switch, *Leaders Under Me* beside *Own Cell* or *Own
12* (decisions 0258, 0301, 0311 and 0333): the branch's outstanding work, two levels down,
grouped by leader. A Full view leader also has *Pending CG Records* and *Pending DCC Records*
(decisions 0336 and 0368), which list the same work for their whole branch, every week still
open, with search and *Late only*, and carry the same Record and See actions. On 21 Oct the owner
looked at a Primary's *Update Records* and said the switch was not needed there.

## The ruling

1. **A Full view leader's *Update Records* has no *Whose* switch.** It shows their own work
   alone: their own Cell meetings and their own 12's Sundays, and its count counts those.
   Their branch's work is on *Pending CG Records* and *Pending DCC Records*, unchanged.
2. **The Admin keeps it**, as it is.
3. **A Leader account without Full view is unchanged**: the switch shows while a leader holding
   an account sits directly beneath them (decision 0323), and not otherwise.
4. **Hidden, not withdrawn.** The switch, the branch view and its grouping by leader stay in the
   code and the API, and their routes are unchanged: the owner keeps them for a later feature.

A Full view leader is told apart as every Reports screen tells one apart, by `reports.view_subtree`
held short of Whole Church on screens that are not the Senior Pastors' (decision 0380), never by a
role.

## Why

Two places for the same work invited the question of which one is right. *Pending* is the
complete one for a Full view leader, and *Recording Progress* already links into it (decision
0370). *Update Records* now means one thing for them: what they themselves still have to record.
