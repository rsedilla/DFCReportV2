# 2026-10-13 — The System Administrator is in no count of people

Owner ruling, 2026-10-13, chosen one question at a time. The Person the first Admin account was
created with (Section 6) is the account that runs the system, not a member of the church. It amends
Sections 15, 19, 27 and 28.

## The ruling

**1. The Person left out is one outside the pastoral tree** (owner, Q1): one holding an `ADMIN`
account who has never held a pastoral assignment, the test Section 5 already applies to the
pastoral path (decision 0270). A leader who also holds `ADMIN` is still counted, and so is an
administrator who is later placed in the tree.

**2. They are left out of three places** (owner, Q2):
- the Senior Pastors' # of People, its Whole Church figure and its *Others* line, and Trends;
- *Awaiting CG Assignment*;
- the SUYNL, Training and Conquest tabs, their count cards and their lists.

They stay in the People list, its search and their own page, so an administrator can find and
correct their own record.

**3. Every month, each judged at its own end** (owner, Q3): a month counts a Person who, at its
final millisecond, held `ADMIN` and had held no pastoral assignment as left out. Each past month that
counted them falls by one, and Trends draws no drop that nobody caused. The lists, which name no
period, judge it now.

## Why

Counting the administrator overstated the church by one in every month and left a person on
*Awaiting CG Assignment* whom nobody would ever place in a Cell. The narrow test was chosen over
"any `ADMIN` account" because the planned Network Admins are among the Bishop's and Pastora
Geraldine's 12, and that wider test is recorded as open in `CLAUDE.md`. Judging each month at its
own end, rather than as of today, is what keeps a later change to a person from moving a month
already reported (Section 3).
