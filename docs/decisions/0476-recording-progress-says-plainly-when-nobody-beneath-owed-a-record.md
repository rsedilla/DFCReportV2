# 2026-11-12 — Recording Progress says plainly when nobody beneath owed a record

Owner ruling, 2026-11-12, chosen from screenshots of the installed iPhone app, today beside
the proposal. It amends Section 19's account of a Full view leader's *Recording Progress*
(decision 0334).

## The ruling

**Where nobody beneath the reader owed a Cell group or a DCC record in the week or month
shown, a Full view leader's *Recording Progress* shows one card in place of its two boxes and
its table**: *No leaders to follow up this week* (or *month*), saying that none of the leaders
under them had a Cell group or a DCC Sunday to record, and that their own records are on
*Update Records*. The line *Nobody is under you this week* is gone. Where anybody beneath owed
anything, the boxes and the table are as before.

**The line under Record's *Recording Progress* tab is not shown on a phone**, below `sm`, as
decisions 0454 and 0455 already do for Record's other lines; from `sm` up it stays.

## Why

The owner reported that a leader was confused. With nobody owing, the screen showed two boxes
reading "0 of 0 leaders on time", and then "Nobody is under you this week", which reads as
though the leader has no people at all. On the installed app that last line also sat behind
the bottom menu. Design systems such as Carbon and Atlassian replace an empty element with one
message that says why it is empty and where to go next, and that is what the card does.

Considered and rejected: rewording the closing line alone, which kept two boxes of zeros
above it; and removing the line under the tab everywhere, which the owner did not ask for.

## What is owed

Nothing beyond this change. The Senior Pastors' and the Admin's church-wide *Recording
Progress* is untouched: a church in which nobody owed a record has no sentence of its own today
and is not a case this ruling was asked about.
