# 2026-11-03 — The Cells list shows each Cell's ID under its name

Decision 0306 kept a Cell ID to the Cell's own pages and to the places a Cell is chosen, on
the ground that the leader's name beside a Cell tells two Cells of one name apart. An audit of
every page on 2026-11-02 found the Cells list where that ground is weakest: two Cells both
read "Young Pro · Sat", and three Young Pro Cells differ only by the leader's name, one leader
leading two of the six. The owner was shown the list on a laptop and a phone, as it is and with
each Cell's ID under its name, and chose the second on 2026-11-03.

## The ruling

**1. The Cells page shows each Cell's ID under its name**, in a smaller, muted line, on every
list it carries: Active Cells, Needs Attention and Closed Cells, as a table on a laptop and as
a card on a phone.

**2. Nothing else moves.** Every other list names a Cell as decision 0306 says, by its
category and meeting day, and a person is still shown by name alone everywhere 0306 says so.
This narrows 0306's first point by one screen and changes nothing about the identifiers.

## Why

The Cells page is where somebody looks a Cell up, and its search already finds one by its ID.
A list that is searched by an identifier and does not show it leaves the reader unable to tell
which row they found; on the other lists a Cell is reached from a person or a leader, who
already says which Cell it is.
