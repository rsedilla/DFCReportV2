# 2026-11-04 — Late lasts three weeks

A week is due at 12 noon on the second Monday after it (decisions 0335 and 0387), a record first
made after that is *Late* and still accepted, and the week closes two Mondays after its deadline,
after which only the Admin may record or correct it. On 2026-11-04 the owner was shown that
timeline beside one where *Late* lasts a week longer, with what the longer one costs, and chose
three weeks.

## The ruling

**1. A week closes three Mondays after its deadline, at 12 noon.** The deadline does not move:
the week of 26 October 2026 is still due at 12:00 on Monday 9 November, and now closes at 12:00
on Monday 30 November rather than 23 November. A record first made after the deadline is still
*Late*, and a correction still never makes an on-time record late. After the close only the
Admin may amend it, as before.

**2. It applies to every week of the weekly rule.** No week of it had closed when this was
decided, the first close being 23 November 2026, so it lengthens open weeks and reopens none. A
week before 26 October 2026 keeps the end of the 7th of the following month.

**3. A month closes when its last week closes, 29 to 35 days after its last day, and that can
fall in the month after next.** November 2026 ends on Monday the 30th and closes at 12:00 on
Monday 4 January 2027. So on the first days of a month two earlier months can be open at once,
and every list that looks back for open work looks back two months: Record's *Update Records*
and its note of when each earlier month closes, *Pending CG Records* and *Pending DCC Records*.

**4. It is the same rule for Cell Group meetings and DCC Sundays.**

**5. Unsaved marks kept on the device are offered back for five weeks rather than four**, so they
last as long as the week they were for, which is the reason decision 0345 gave them four weeks
(Section 25).

## Why

The owner wanted leaders who miss the deadline to have more time before a week can only be
changed by the Admin. What it costs was stated before the choice: a month's figures stay open a
week longer and so become final later, the Pending lists can hold five open weeks rather than
four, counting the week in progress, and a longer grace tends to move records later. On time is
still measured against the unchanged deadline, so *Recording Progress* still shows who recorded
on time.
