# 2026-11-07 — Nothing is written under the People search before it is used

The owner marked the line under the People search, *Search by name or Member ID to find someone.*,
as repeating the label above the box, *Search by name or Member ID*, and asked for it to go and for
the page to explain itself with a tooltip instead. The change was chosen from screenshots at phone,
iPad and laptop widths.

## The ruling

**Nothing is written under the search before it is used.** The label above the box stays: it says
what to type.

**From `sm` up, an ⓘ beside the label explains what the label does not**, opened on a tap or a
click and closed as `Record`'s is (decision 0454): that the search finds the people the reader
oversees, and that *Add* searches every branch as the reader types, so to look there before adding
somebody new. The page said that last part only after a search had found nobody. On a phone there
is no ⓘ, as on `Record`.

The People screen is the same for every account that has it, so a Full view leader and the Admin
see this too.

## Why

Search guidance asks for a visible label on a search box and not a placeholder in its place: a
placeholder goes when typing starts and a screen reader does not take it for a label. So the label
stays and the repeat goes. The ⓘ sits beside the label rather than inside it, so it is not read as
part of the box's name.
