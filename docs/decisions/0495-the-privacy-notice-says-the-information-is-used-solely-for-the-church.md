# 2026-11-16 — The Privacy Notice says the information is used solely for the church

Owner ruling, 2026-11-16, from screenshots of the Privacy Notice's *Why we keep it* part on a
phone and a laptop, today beside the change. The owner asked that the notice say the information
is used solely for Doulos For Christ.

## The ruling

**1. *Why we keep it* says the information is used solely by and for the church.** Its second
paragraph opens: "Your information is used **solely by and for Doulos For Christ World Harvest
Ministry**, and for nothing and nobody else." The sentence that followed stays after it: never
sold, never used for advertising, never shared outside the church except where the law requires.

**2. The notice is dated "Last updated: November 16, 2026".** Its wording changed, so its date
does (decision 0412, point 5).

## Why

The Data Privacy Act asks that the purpose of keeping personal information be stated, and the
church's purpose is its own ministry. Saying "solely" in the part a reader looks to for why their
information is kept puts the limit where it will be read, beside the promises it belongs with,
rather than in a paragraph of its own.

It stays true of the three service providers: the notice already says Hostinger, Brevo and Google
Drive handle the information only to keep DREAMS running, which is the church's use and no other.

## What it does not change

What DREAMS keeps, who may see it, who helps run it, or any route. No record or migration changes.
