# 2026-11-11 — A Network Admin places nobody under an administrator outside the tree

Owner ruling, 2026-11-11, on a question `architecture-guardian` raised reviewing the build of
a Network Admin's people powers (decision 0469, point 4). It amends Section 7.

## The ruling

A Network Admin does not add a person under, or move a person to, an administrator who holds
no pastoral assignment. The request is refused `SCOPE_DENIED`. Only a Whole Church holder
does that.

## Why

Whether a Person outside the pastoral structure may be given disciples at all is recorded as
open in `CLAUDE.md`. Until decision 0469 only a Whole Church holder could reach that state. A
Network Admin's `NETWORK` scope covers such an administrator's Person, their Network being set
from their sex, so the new role would have reached it too. The owner kept it with the
whole-church Admin.

The refusal applies only to a leader the Network Admin's own scope covers. A leader outside it
is refused by the ordinary scope check, in the same words whether or not they hold an
assignment, so nothing is said about somebody outside the reader's scope (Section 8).

This does not settle the open question. Whether the whole-church Admin may give such a Person
disciples stays as it was.

Considered and rejected: refusing every leader who holds no open assignment, which would also
refuse a person recorded and not yet assigned, whom Section 5 treats differently.

## What is owed

Nothing. The refusal and its tests were built with the people powers.
