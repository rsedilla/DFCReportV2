# 2026-10-18 — No cards above the Primaries' 12

A Full view leader's CG and DCC Attendance Reports showed the branch's figures twice: as cards
headed *Your Disciples* (decisions 0340 and 0363), and as the Total row of the table of their 12
right below. On 18 Oct the owner asked for the cards to go, and chose from real screenshots the
version that also lays the table out for a phone.

## The ruling

1. **There are no cards above the table.** The table's Total row gives the branch's figures, and
   the cards repeated it. The heading over the cards goes with them; an opened leader is still
   named by *Figures for* and the line with its way back.
2. **Below `lg`, each line of the table is two short lines**: the leader's name, then *Total, VIP,
   2nd Timer, 3rd Timer, 4th Timer, Regular*, ending with a bold *Total* line, as the Senior
   Pastors' *#* tables are on a phone. Without the cards, a table wider than the phone would have
   put the branch's last figures out of sight to one side.
3. Only these two tabs change, and only their layout. People's *# of People* keeps its box, which
   no Total row repeats there.

## Why

The same six figures twice, one above the other, was noise. The phone layout is what keeps them
all readable once the cards are gone.
