# 2026-11-20 — Equipping: the school from the class to the final grade, and SOL 1 to 3

Owner rulings of 2026-11-18, 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20, settled one question at a time from
screenshots of the real app on the demo database, changed for the picture where a screen does
not exist yet. Each answer was recorded on the planning page as it was given, with the date,
and an audit of the whole plan on 2026-11-20 settled nine gaps it found. Decision 0499 ruled
Registration and Enrollment for Life Class, and left Faculty, make-ups, final grades and
posting them to a later ruling. This is that ruling. **Nothing in it is built.** It is to be
built one step at a time, each step with its own pull request.

## The people

**1. A Level Coordinator for each level, for the whole church** (owner, 2026-11-18). Life
Class, SOL 1, SOL 2 and SOL 3 each have one, like a university's program chair: a member of
the pastoral tree, on their own Leader account, looking after the classes of their own level
only. Life Class and the schools are not split by Network, so one Coordinator opens every class
of their level, and a class holds men and women together. The Admin makes somebody a
Coordinator by ticking the level on their Leader account, as *Approves Cells* and *Moves
people* are ticked (Section 7); nobody gets a second login. **The tick is offered only on a
Full view account**, as those two are (owner, 2026-11-20, option A). Only the Life Class Coordinator is
needed until SOL 1 is added to the school.

**2. The Coordinator opens the class and names its Teacher and Guides**, not the Registrar
(owner, 2026-11-18, and again 2026-11-20). Opening a class, built for the Registrar in 0499,
moves to the Coordinator; the Admin keeps every Registrar and Coordinator step. Naming a
Teacher shows whether the class fits that person's week, naming a clash with their own Cell
meeting under the two-hour rule (decision 0392). **The clash warns and the Coordinator may
save** (owner, 2026-11-20, option A), unlike 0392's refusal of one leader's two Cells, because
the Coordinator knows whether the Cell has moved or has another facilitator that night. **The warning names
that Cell's day and time** (owner, 2026-11-20, "Yes, day and time", confirming the screenshot of
2026-11-19: *leads a Cell on Tuesdays at 7:30 pm, less than two hours from this class*), which
discloses to the Coordinator when a Cell outside their scope meets, accepted as the Cell leader
shown on a class's pages is (point 10), so the Coordinator can see why it clashes. **Each check is
recorded and limited as a church-wide search is** (owner, 2026-11-20, "Record and limit it"):
written to the Audit Log under *Lookups*, a clash or not, with the class and who was asked about,
and held to thirty a minute per account (decision 0303), because a Coordinator could otherwise ask
about every Cell Leader in turn and learn when their Cells meet, with nothing recording it.

**3. The Teacher records attendance and make-ups, and nothing else** (owner, 2026-11-19,
dropping weekly grades: the result is Passed or Not passed from attendance, and in SOL from the
checklist in point 17). The Teacher stays a current Cell Leader in the tree when named
(decision 0499, point 10). A Teacher with no account is given one by the Admin.

**4. A Guide follows up their own students** (owner, 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-19), and:

- **sees their own students only**, read only, with a line naming any who missed two weeks in a
  row (owner, 2026-11-20, replacing the whole class of 2026-11-18, after comparing Canvas's
  section-limited teaching assistants and the data-minimisation principle). The Teacher and
  the Coordinator see the whole class. A Guide never records attendance, even when the Teacher
  is away; the Teacher records it on return (owner, 2026-11-19).
- **is the same sex as their students**. The class is mixed and only the Guide groups are split
  (owner, 2026-11-18, and 2026-11-20: "in the long run we can have both men and women in
  SOL").
- **is one level ahead of the class** (owner, 2026-11-20: "SOL 2 grad he can guide SOL 1 and
  Life class"): SOL 1 passed to guide Life Class, SOL 2 passed to guide SOL 1 or Life Class, SOL
  3 passed to guide any of the three below it. **SOL 3 is guided by a SOL graduate** who has
  passed SOL 3 and has at least three Cell Leaders under them (owner, 2026-11-20): at least three
  of their own direct disciples each leading an `ACTIVE` Cell now, not anyone further down their
  tree (owner, 2026-11-20, option A). A pass recorded before the school counts
  (point 19). It is checked when the Coordinator names the Guide, and a Guide keeps the class if
  it stops holding, as a Teacher does. **The Guide search lists only those who qualify, each
  saying why, and naming anybody else is refused** (owner, 2026-11-20, option A), as naming a
  Teacher is (decision 0499, point 10).
- is any member of the tree who meets those, with no approval first (owner, 2026-11-18).

**5. The student's own leader sees their people in the school** (owner, 2026-11-20). A leader
reads, view only, each of their people enrolled this term, at every level of the tree below
them with their own twelve first: the class, its Teacher and Guide, the register and one line
such as *3 of 4 came · L3 not made up yet*. Nothing is sent. An adviser's view of their
advisees in Starfish and EAB Navigate was the comparison shown.

## Classes and Guides

**6. A class holds 30, in Life Class and in SOL 1 to 3; more are allowed with a reason**, which
the enrollment keeps (owner, 2026-11-20). *Choose a class* lists every class of the level with
its places (*28 of 30*) and whether it fits the person's week, their Cell and DCC; a full class
stays listed, reading *Full · going over 30 needs a reason*, with no switch hiding it (owner,
2026-11-20).

**7. Enrollment works both ways** (owner, 2026-11-20, after PeopleSoft's Quick Enroll and
Banner's bulk add): several people ticked and enrolled into the class shown, as built, or
*Choose a class* on one person.

**8. DREAMS shares the students out among the Guides, by sex**, and the Coordinator may swap one
to another Guide of the same sex (owner, 2026-11-18 and 2026-11-20). The class's Edit says how
many Guides each sex needs (*6 men need 2 Guides · 5 women need 2*), and a short class never
stops enrolling. **A student enrolled after the students were shared out joins the Guide of their sex
who has the fewest** (owner, 2026-11-20, "Fewest students, same sex"); with no Guide of their sex
named yet they read *No Guide yet*, and the Coordinator may still swap them. **Naming a Guide
takes nobody from another Guide** (owner, 2026-11-20, "No, swap by hand"): only students with no
Guide are shared out, so a Guide named late starts with none, and the Coordinator moves students
to them with *Swap*.

- **Life Class: 3 or 4 a Guide, except with 1, 2 or 5 students** (owner, 2026-11-18, from a
  table of class sizes). Guides are
  the students of one sex divided by 3, the remainder dropped, at least one, the one or two left
  over each joining a different group, so groups never differ by more than one: 6 is 3, 3; 7 is
  4, 3; 11 is 4, 4, 3. Two cases fall outside that arithmetic and the owner gave each: 1 or 2
  students have one Guide with 1 or 2, and 5 have two Guides, with 3 and 2, never one Guide with
  5. **It is fixed at 3 for now** (owner, 2026-11-20, option A): the owner's "the Admin may
  change it to 4 in Settings; 3 for now" of 2026-11-19 waits until a table for 4 is decided
  from screenshots, and no setting is built until then.
- **SOL 1 to 3: up to 5 a Guide** (owner, 2026-11-20): as few Guides as possible, never more
  than 5 each, the groups as even as they can be. 1 to 5 of one sex have one Guide; 6 is 3, 3;
  11 is 4, 4, 3; 17 is 5, 4, 4, 4.

**9. From enrollment to the class, four steps** (owner, 2026-11-20): the Coordinator edits the
class, naming the Teacher and the Guides; on Save DREAMS shares the students out and the
Coordinator sees each one's Cell leader and Guide; the Teacher records each week; each Guide
sees their own students.

## Attendance and make-ups

**10. The Teacher ticks who came; anyone not ticked is saved Absent** (owner, 2026-11-18 and
2026-11-19), with one control ticking everyone. Each student shows their Cell leader beside
their Guide, and above Save a line names who will be saved absent and their Cell leaders. **The Teacher and the Coordinator see the Cell leader of every student of their own class, and a Guide of each of their own students, whichever branch or Network the student is in** (owner, 2026-11-20, "show it", from screenshots beside a version reading *Another branch*). That discloses a Cell association Section 8 withholds outside a reader's scope, and the owner accepted it so the leader who should hear of an absence is known, as decision 0499 point 10 accepted the Teacher search telling who leads a Cell; it is shown on the class's own pages and nowhere else. A
lesson row opens any lesson, each reading *Saved*, *Late*, *Today* or *Not yet*. The register
below is a row per student and a column per lesson: ✓ came, ✗ absent, M made up, – not yet,
and a total such as *3 of 4*, never colour alone. The Encounter column is the Registrar's
(point 14).

**11. A week follows Cell attendance's timing** (owner, 2026-11-19): due eight days after the
week, late for three weeks, then closed (decisions 0387 and 0434). The Teacher corrects a ✓ or
✗ while the week is open, and the Admin after it closes. The Coordinator sees the weeks not
recorded.

**12. A make-up is recorded as M with its date** (owner, 2026-11-19): the Teacher plays the
recorded teaching of Bishop Oriel for that lesson and records it on the same attendance page,
the ✗ becoming M. DREAMS stores no video. **The Teacher may record a make-up until the term's
final grades are posted, even after the week has closed** (owner, 2026-11-20), because a
make-up is often weeks later; only the ✓ and ✗ follow point 11. After posting, every change
needs a reason and the Admin.

## Life Class: the Encounter and the grade

**13. Life Class has nine lessons, the fifth the Encounter (ENC)** (owner, 2026-11-19;
decision 0295). Every lesson must be completed, a missed one made up. Lessons 1 to 4 decide the
Encounter: one or two missed must be made up before it, and three or more means the student
cannot go and repeats Life Class, made up or not. At most three absences in the whole of Life
Class, every one made up, lessons 1 to 4 keeping their own limit of two. Over that limit after
the Encounter, the student keeps L1 to L4 and the Encounter and joins a later class at Lesson 6.

**14. The Encounter has its own enrollment, by the Registrar** (owner, 2026-11-19). After L4 a
list for each Network's weekend reads *Cleared*, *Needs make-up* or *Can't go*. The Registrar
enrolls those who completed lessons 1 to 4, up to two made up, and are 14 by the weekend's
first day, and records who attended, which is the ENC column. A student who misses the
Encounter keeps attending L6 to L9 and graduates once they attend a later one, repeating no
lesson.

## Final grades

**15. The final grade is Passed or Not passed, with the reason** (owner, 2026-11-19), worked out
by DREAMS, never typed.

**16. The Coordinator checks each class, then the Registrar posts it** (owner, 2026-11-20,
"A, with the own-disciple rule", after universities' chair review). The Coordinator sees the
class's grades read only and presses *Mark as checked*; a wrong mark goes back to the Teacher
or the Admin. **A student who is the Coordinator's own disciple is checked by the Admin
instead**, as a leader may not approve a Cell that counts for themselves (Section 10). The
Registrar's *Final Grades* lists each class as *checked by …, date* or *waiting for the
Coordinator*, and posts only a checked class (owner, 2026-11-19, from screenshots: a term, a
button per class, the register, *Lessons 9 of 9 · 1 made up*, the grade and its reason, a table
on a laptop and a card a student on a phone). Posting updates Training and the next level's
candidates. A change after posting needs a reason and the Admin.

## SOL 1, 2 and 3

**17. Each runs as Life Class does, with ten weekly lessons and nothing else** (owner,
2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20): its own Coordinator, a Teacher, Guides, weekly attendance, make-ups
as M, the Coordinator's check and the Registrar's posting. At most three absences, each made up;
a fourth repeats the level. **The grade is a checklist with no points** (owner, 2026-11-19):
the attendance rule met with every absence made up, all ten memory verses recited, all ten
devotionals handed in, the quiz passed, and the book read. A *Not passed* names what is missing.
The Teacher records all of it, ticking memory verse and devotional with each week's attendance,
entering the quiz score, and ticking the book; the lesson page carries a *Still missing* column
so it is chased during the term. **The quiz** (owner, 2026-11-20): the Teacher enters the number
of items once and each score, and the page shows the percentage; 70% passes, two retakes are
allowed before final grades, every score is kept and a pass on any try counts.

**18. To enroll** (owner, 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20):

| Level | Requires |
| --- | --- |
| SOL 1 | Life Class passed; Win 3, as Conquest derives it (Section 27); *Has SOL 1 manual* ticked by the Registrar with the date |
| SOL 2 | SOL 1 passed; leads an `ACTIVE` Cell now, a started, taken-over or setup Cell, never one closed or created in error; *Has SOL 2 book* ticked |
| SOL 3 | SOL 2 passed; leads an `ACTIVE` Cell now; twelve direct disciples now, twelve open pastoral assignments naming them; *Has SOL 3 book* ticked |

The derived requirements are worked out from the records and never ticked. SOL 3's twelve are
twelve **now** (owner, 2026-11-20: "have 12 in current, in the app"), replacing Conquest's
Completion of 12 once reached, chosen the same day; no order between the Cell and the twelve is
checked. **They are checked once, when the student signs up for the level, and not again during the
term** (owner, 2026-11-20, option A, chosen against checking again before final grades or all
through the term): they are read at registration, as Life Class's are (decision 0499, point 6),
and a Cell closing or a disciple moving during the term takes nobody out and fails nobody. **A requirement not met warns, as Life Class's do** (owner, 2026-11-20, "Warn, like Life Class", decision 0499, point 7): registering asks why they are registered anyway, the registration reads *Registered with exception* with the reason, and the Admin reviews it. Section 28's "the order is displayed and never enforced" gains SOL 1 to 3 beside Life Class as the exceptions that warn. No payment is
recorded: the book is the tuition (owner, 2026-11-19, "not Paid").

## Training

**19. Training's graduations come from the school** (owner, 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20). A
posted Passed files the level's graduation, marked *from the school*, and the Encounter
attended files the Encounter's. Leaders can no longer tick Life Class or the Encounter on the
Training tab, and each of SOL 1, SOL 2 and SOL 3 stops being ticked when the school runs that level
(owner, 2026-11-20, option A: until the first class of a level opens, leaders tick it as now). **A tick made before the school
is kept**, shown as *Before the school* and no longer editable, and counts as that level passed,
for enrolling and for guiding (option A).

## Screens

**20. The Registrar's sidebar is `Registration`, `Enrollment`, `Encounter` and `Final Grades`**
(owner, 2026-11-19), the Admin's `Equipping` item carrying them as tabs.

**21. A leader's `Equipping` item** (owner, 2026-11-20) holds the class they teach or guide and
their people in the school, as tabs *Life Class*, *SOL 1*, *SOL 2* and *SOL 3*, each card
point 5's, rather than the separate *Life Class* item the plan of 2026-11-19 drew. **It shows only while one of the leader's
people is enrolled this term, or they teach or guide a class, and stays until that term's final
grades are posted** (owner, 2026-11-20: "im still in pilot for that, this is for my network
only"); during the pilot that is one branch, with no list of pilot leaders kept. Hiding it is
the menu alone: the server gives a leader only their own people either way. **For a Full view
leader it is a second tab of their `SUYNL` item, which is then named `Growth`** (owner,
2026-11-20, option A), the label following what the item holds as decision 0383 ties it, so a phone's
bottom bar keeps five items, the most phone guidelines allow.

**22. The Coordinator's `Classes`** (owner, 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-20, from screenshots): one
table of the level's classes, Class, Day and time, Teacher, Guides, Students, Attendance and
Status, with *Edit* and *Students*, filtered by term and status, with *Open a class*; a problem
is said in words (*L3 late*, *Not named yet*, *1 more needed*). On a laptop it is its own
sidebar item; on a phone, the installed app included, it is a tab inside `Equipping`, each class
a card (owner, 2026-11-20, option A, replacing "website only", after Google's PWA checklist asks
that all of an installed app's content be reachable at any width). **A Coordinator always has `Equipping` on a phone**, coordinating a level being a third condition beside point 21's two, so `Classes` is never out of reach (owner, 2026-11-20, option A). **For a Full view Coordinator, whose `Equipping` is a tab of `Growth`, `Classes` is a third tab of `Growth`** (*SUYNL* · *Equipping* · *Classes*), not a tab inside a tab, which decision 0481 calls one step too many.

## Why

The school runs on the records it checks against: leaders, Cells, attendance, SUYNL. Each
answer was taken from how a university runs the same step, a registrar's office, a program
chair, faculty and facilitators, changed where the church's own way differs: a Guide is one
level ahead and the same sex, a leader follows up their people, and nothing records money.
