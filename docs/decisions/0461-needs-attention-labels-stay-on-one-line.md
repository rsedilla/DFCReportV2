# 2026-11-09 — Needs Attention's labels stay on one line

Decision 0416 made each segment of a switch inside a tab 8rem from `lg`. On the Cells page's
*Needs Attention* tab that broke *Under 3 Members* and *Inactive 3+ Months* onto two lines, and on
an upright iPad the four lists took two rows. The owner found it tight and chose from screenshots
of today and the proposal.

## The ruling

**From `md`, the four lists under *Needs Attention* stand in one row, sharing it equally up to
46rem, each label on one line.** They remain one size as each other. On a phone they stay two by
two. No other switch changes.

## Why

8rem is narrower than the longest of these labels. Widening all four keeps decision 0416's one size
for every segment and stops the labels wrapping.
