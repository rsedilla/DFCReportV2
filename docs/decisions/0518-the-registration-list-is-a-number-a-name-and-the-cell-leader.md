# 2026-11-28 — The Registration list is a number, the name and the Cell Leader, with short labels

Owner rulings of 2026-11-28, from screenshots of the running list beside proposals, after a
look at how Canvas, Moodle, PeopleSoft, Google Classroom and UP's CRS show a roster's status.

**1. Each row is a number and the name in bold** (owner: "we can just like bold the name … and
put a number before the name"). The name stays in black. The numbers run on across *Show more*.

**2. The requirements not met and the reason leave the list** (owner: "we dont need the regular
in cg and regular in dcc just name", then "no reason pls"). This replaces decision 0514's
sentence that the Registration list names what was not met (*Not met: … Why: …*), and Section
29's clause saying the same. Both stay on the registration, which still refuses one without the
other (`equipping_registrations_exception_has_reason`), and *With exception* still marks the row.
The owner was told before ruling that the reason was the one place an Admin reviewing an
exception would see why it was let in, and chose to leave it off: no screen now shows it, and it
is kept on the registration. **So the Admin's review of an exception is withdrawn** (owner,
"Withdraw the review", after `architecture-guardian` raised that Section 29 and decision 0501,
point 18, still asked for one). It withdraws decision 0499's too, where it began (points 3 and
7), so it holds for Life Class and SOL alike: a requirement not met still warns and still needs a reason, and
nobody reviews it afterwards.

**3. The leader column is *Cell Leader*, the leader of the Cell the person attends now**
(owner, "Cell Leader", choosing it over today's name under a clearer heading). The column had
shown the person's leader in the tree, who is often their Cell Leader and not always. Somebody
who is a member of a Cell shows that Cell's leader; somebody who is a member of none and leads
one reads *Leads a Cell*; anybody else reads *No Cell yet*. Nothing here says whether a leader is
a member of their own Cell, which stays open. It is read when the list is read, as the Encounter
list reads a student's Cell leader (decision 0507). It shows the Registrar a Cell association and
whether somebody leads a Cell, which Section 8 otherwise withholds from a reader without
`cell.view_subtree`, accepted by the owner here as for the Teacher and Coordinator (Section 29).

**4. The birthday and the status are short labels**: *! Missing* or *✓ Recorded*, and
*✓ Registered*, *! With exception* or *✓ Enrolled*, *Cancelled* in plain words. On this list
*Registered with exception* is shortened to *With exception*, a screen reader still hearing the
whole of it. Each says what
it means in its words and a tick or an exclamation mark, never in its colour alone (1.4.1).
*Cancel* is the outlined button, at the one size every button takes (decision 0416).
