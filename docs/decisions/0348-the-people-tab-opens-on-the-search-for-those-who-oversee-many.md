# 2026-10-14 — The People tab opens on the search for those who oversee many

Decision 0259 opens the People tab on everyone the reader oversees, ten a page. For an Admin
that is the whole church, and for a Senior Pastor or a Full view leader near the top of a
Network it is hundreds or thousands: nobody browses that ten at a time, and they come to the
tab to find one person. The owner chose this on 14 Oct from real screenshots.

## The ruling

**For an account on the Full view or Senior Pastor screens, the People tab opens on the search
alone**, with "Search by name or Member ID to find someone."
Nothing is listed, and nothing is asked of the server, until a search. The table then shows
what the search finds, as before. That is an Admin, a Senior Pastor, and a Leader with Full
view, Bishop Oriel's direct 12 among them.

**An account on the Recording screens keeps decision 0259's list**: a leader without Full view
oversees few enough people to browse them.

Which screens an account has is the server's answer (decision 0323, point 5); the screen does
not work it out from a role.

## Why

The list is the quickest way to reach one's people while there are few of them, and noise
when there are many. A Full view leader already browses their branch by its structure on the
Family Tree tab (decision 0341). Rejected: a threshold on the size of the branch, which would
be arbitrary, and removing the list for everyone, which takes it from the leaders it serves.
