# 2026-09-25 — Record opens on four lists

The Record screen showed what awaits a record beside four figures for the month, then the
reader's requests, then three short lists side by side: Cells with meetings still to record,
people needing a leader, and people without a Cell. The two lists of people showed five names
each and linked to a screen of their own for the rest.

The owner chose this answer by editing the running screen, one change at a time.

## The ruling

**Record opens on four lists, one at a time, chosen by four equal buttons each carrying its
count:** Awaiting a record, Cells behind, Needs a new leader, Not in a Cell. A list read a page
at a time shows a count such as "50+" rather than a figure it has not read. Awaiting a record
is itself split into Cell Group and Doulos Cell Celebration, Cell Group first.

**Each list is a table, and a card for each row on a narrower screen.** The two lists of
people show everybody, fifty at a time with Show more, and an empty list of Cells or people
keeps its table and says so in one row. The screen that held the rest of the people needing a
leader is retired, because this list now shows everybody.

**The month's four figures are a row at the foot**, still carrying their scope and period, with
the reader's requests between the lists and the figures.

**Shorter headings elsewhere.** People, Cells, Growth and Network put one short line beside
their heading, and the Network screen's direct-reports headcount is labelled Direct disciples.

## Why

A leader acts on one list at a time, and the counts on the buttons say which lists have
anything in them without opening each. The figures stay on the screen, below the work, which
is the order Section 19 asks for.
