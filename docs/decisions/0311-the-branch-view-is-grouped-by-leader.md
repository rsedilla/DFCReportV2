# 2026-10-04 — The branch view is grouped by leader

Record's *People I oversee* lists one row per outstanding meeting or Sunday (decisions 0258 and
0301). A leader overseeing about a hundred Cells, such as a Network root's direct 12, would see
up to about four hundred rows near a month's close. The owner compared their Claude Design
handoff, which lists one row per meeting, with a version grouped by leader, and chose the
grouped one.

## The ruling

**1. In *People I oversee*, rows are grouped under the leader each names.** One line per leader
with something outstanding, saying how many and the oldest date, closed until opened. Opened, it
shows that leader's rows as today, each carrying its action: Record, or See checklist (decision
0301). This applies to the Cell and DCC lists alike.

**2. Leaders are listed by name**, in the order names are sorted elsewhere. Not by how much they
owe or how long it has waited, which would read as a ranking (decision 0009).

**3. The close of last month's open work is stated once, above the list**, rather than on each
row.

**4. *My own Cells* is unchanged**, one row per meeting, because a leader's own list is short.

**5. The screen applies this, and the API does not.** The route, who is listed and what each row
carries are unchanged (decision 0258, points 2 and 3; decision 0301).

## Why

A list of hundreds of rows is read by scrolling, and the person reading it wants to know which of
their leaders still owe something. Grouping answers that in one line per leader, and keeps every
meeting one tap away.

The deadline was considered and kept. A month closes at the end of the 7th of the following
month (sections 9 and 13), so a leader has from about a week to about five weeks depending on
the date. A deadline counted from each meeting would not shorten the list, would take time from
leaders whose meeting falls early in the month, and would change when every report is final.
