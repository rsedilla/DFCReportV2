# 2026-11-14 — The Senior Pastors' *Cells* and *Services* open on their own Network, and every Network switch reads *Men* · *Women* · *Both*

Owner rulings, 2026-11-14, each chosen from screenshots of Bishop Oriel's screens on a phone and a
laptop, today beside the change, one question at a time. Decision 0490 gave the Senior Pastors'
*People* these; the owner asked for the same on the three *Cells* tabs, and Bishop Oriel asked for
a tab on *Services*.

## The ruling

**1. *# of Cell Groups* and *# of Cell Leaders* work as *People* does** (decision 0490), for the
two Senior Pastors: a *Men* · *Women* · *Both* switch, the reader's own Network first and chosen on
opening; a Primary's name opens that Primary's 12 on the same page, with *Back to Cells*; a name
there with people beneath it opens that leader's 12 in turn. Each root's table ends with **the
root's own row and a Total**. One person is not one Cell, so `GET /api/v1/reports/church-counts`
now carries each root's own figures with their table, as `own`, counted in the same transaction as
the rows and the box (section 20's single-snapshot rule), so the table adds up to the box. *Others*
is the API's line less each root and is shown only where it is not 0; were it ever below 0 the
tables would not add up, and the line is shown as the API answered it, with no root rows. *People*
reads its root rows the same way. This replaces decision 0490's sentence that the two Cell counts keep *Others* as it was,
and its point 3's last sentence: on these two a name opens that Primary's 12, not their page.

**2. *CG Attendance* and *Services* read *Men* · *Women* · *Both*, the reader's own Network first
and chosen on opening**, in place of decision 0486's *All*, *Men's* and *Women's*, and with no
*Figures for* label. The three buttons take the control bar's width on a phone and sit side by
side; before this they stood in a narrow column, one under another. *Men* or *Women* on *CG
Attendance* is laid out as *Both* is: the Network's boxes, then its root's table of Primaries with
short names on a phone, then the lines under the table, the root among those *Under none of these
leaders*.

**3. *Services* opening on *Men* or *Women* lists that root's Primaries.** `GET
/api/v1/reports/dcc/twelve` with a `NETWORK` selector now answers the Network's root's direct
disciples as rows, with that root as `roots`, as the Cell table's `NETWORK` selector already did
(decision 0469), each row holding only the Network's own people, as the total does. The total is
unchanged: the Network's membership (decision 0219), so the rows,
their overlap and *elsewhere* add up to it, *elsewhere* holding the root and anybody in the Network
under no root. A Network Admin's screens still show their Network's total alone.

**4. The way back goes up one level.** On *Services* the way back keeps the Network the reader was
on. Two levels down, on *People*, the two Cell counts and *CG
Attendance*, the link reads *Back to* the leader above, by name, and returns to
that leader's 12; one level down it reads *Back to People*, *Back to Cells* or *Back to your
report*, as before. The leaders opened on the way are kept in the address as `trail`, so a reload
or a shared link keeps the way back. This matters most in the installed app on an iPhone, which has
no back button of its own; Android's back gesture keeps working either way, each level having its
own address.

**5. *Services* has a tab row, one tab, *DCC***, sized as the *Cells* tabs are. Bishop Oriel asked
for two, *DCC* and *Crossover*, a young professionals' gathering; *Crossover* is parked and owes
rulings of its own before it is built: what it records, who attends, who records it, and how it
is counted.

**6. Every switch that chooses a Network reads *Men* · *Women* · *Both*.** Besides the above, that
is the Admin's *Network* filter on *CG Records Due* and *DCC Records Due*, which read *All*,
*Men's* and *Women's*; it opens on *Both* as it opened on *All*, the Admin having no Network of
their own. The Admin's *Accounts* filter keeps *All*: it chooses account states, not Networks.

## Why

The Bishop reads his own Network first and Pastora hers on every report, not only on *People*; one
set of words for one choice, everywhere it is offered, is what a leader does not have to work out.
A switch whose buttons stand in a column on a phone reads as a list. A way back that jumps to the
top from two levels down loses the reader's place, and in the installed app on an iPhone it is the
only way back there is.

## What it does not change

Who may read what. A Whole Church holder could already read either Network's figures, any leader's
branch and either Network's DCC total; the rows point 3 adds are each a leader that holder may
already name (decision 0254's naming, shared by both tables). A Network Admin still reads only
their own Network. The totals are unchanged: a Network's DCC total is its membership and a Cell
figure is counted by the Cell's Network (section 20). The Full view leaders' screens are unchanged.
