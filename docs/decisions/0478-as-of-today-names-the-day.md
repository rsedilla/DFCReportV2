# 2026-11-12 — *As of today* names the day

Owner ruling, 2026-11-12, chosen from screenshots of Reports at iPhone width, Weekly and Monthly,
today beside the proposal. It dates the note decision 0451 set and changes no rule.

## The ruling

**Under `Reports`, an open period's note names the day: "As of today, 12 Nov"**, in place of "As
of today". It is the same note in the same places: on *Attendance*, Cell Group and DCC, Weekly
and Monthly, a Full view leader's note under the controls and the Senior Pastors' and the Admin's
beside the period; and in the first tile of each *#* tab for the current month. The year is left
out, today being in the current one. On DCC the note keeps any Sunday with no service after it
("As of today, 12 Nov · No service on Sunday 14 June"). A period whose records have closed still
says nothing, and a *#* tab's finished month still says *As of 31 October 2026*.

**The day is read again when the app comes back to the front**, so an installed app brought back
after midnight says the new day.

## Why

The owner asked how an installed app should show it. Google's Material guidance prefers a
specific date where an approximate one is not enough ("Today, 10:00 AM") and drops the year in
the current one; offline-first and dashboard guidance prefers a concrete "as of" to a vague one.
An installed app often stays open in the background, and a screenshot sent on Messenger is read
days later: "today" alone says neither which day nor that it has passed. The date comes from the
phone's clock in Asia/Manila, as every other "today" in this client does, and asks nothing more
of the server.

Considered and rejected: removing the note, which Section 17 requires for an open period; and a
time of day as well, which the figures do not need, a leader reading them by the day.

## What is owed

Nothing.
