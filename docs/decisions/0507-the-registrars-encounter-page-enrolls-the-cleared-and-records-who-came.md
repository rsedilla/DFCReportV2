# 2026-11-22 — The Registrar's Encounter page enrolls the cleared and records who came

Owner ruling of 2026-11-22, made from real-app screenshots ("Yes, as drawn"), building decision
0501, point 14: the Encounter has its own enrollment, by the Registrar; after lesson 4 a list for
each Network's weekend reads *Cleared*, *Needs make-up* or *Can't go*; the Registrar enrolls
those who completed lessons 1 to 4, up to two made up, and are 14 by its first day, and records
who attended, which is the ENC column.

**1. The screen** (owner, from screenshots). The Registrar's sidebar gains *Encounter* after
*Enrollment*, and the Admin's *Equipping* the same tab, as 0501 point 20 names it. One weekend at a
time on a *Men's* · *Women's* switch, with a season to choose where more than one is recorded.
Before the weekend: *Cleared*, a tick box each, all ticked to start, and one button, *Enroll 4 in
the Men's Encounter*; *Needs make-up* and *Can't go*, with no tick box and the reason in words;
the classes *Waiting for lesson 4*; and *Enrolled*. Each student shows their class, Cell leader,
age on the weekend's first day and lessons 1 to 4 as the register marks them. From the weekend's
first day: *Who came*, a tick box each, and *Save who came*, anyone not ticked saved as not
attended.

**2. Where a student stands** is worked out from the class's register as the class's own page
reads it (decision 0503), once the class has saved lesson 4; a class that has not lists nobody and
is named under *Waiting for lesson 4* with lesson 4's expected day. *Can't go*: three or more of
lessons 1 to 4 missed, made up or not (0501, point 13), no birthday recorded, or under 14 on the
weekend's first day. *Needs make-up*: one or two missed and not yet made up, a lesson held before
they joined and not yet made up, or a saved lesson with no mark for them. *Cleared*: the rest.
**A lesson held before a student joined is not one of the three missed** (owner, 2026-11-22, "No,
they make up"): it is owed as a make-up, as the register owes it (decision 0503), so a student
who joined after lesson 3 reads *Needs make-up* and goes once the three are made up, rather than
*Can't go*.
Only *Cleared* may be enrolled, and an enrollment of several is all or nobody, each rechecked
under the class's lock; a make-up recorded later moves a student to *Cleared* by itself.

**3. Enrolling closes when the weekend begins, and recording opens.** A student is enrolled in
one weekend a season, their own Network's. Who came is recorded from the weekend's first day,
and saving again corrects it, the earlier row kept and closed (principle 12), with no window yet:
the step that posts final grades owes one. A save writes only the students the page lists, so
one archived, withdrawn, moved or of the other Network since is left as recorded. A person
attends one Encounter: ticking one already recorded at another weekend is refused in words, the
service locking every weekend row of the people ticked, and the database refuses a second as a
backstop. It is the register's ENC column: ✓ came, ✗ missed, – otherwise, on the class's
page and a leader's cards.

**4. A student who missed it is listed for a later weekend** (0501, point 14), with *Missed the
August Encounter*; so is one of an earlier season who was never enrolled, once their own weekend
has begun, with *From the August season*. A student enrolled in a weekend whose attendance is not
yet recorded is on no other list while they are still on that weekend's. **One who leaves it
before who came is saved is carried** (owner, 2026-11-22, "carry them"): withdrawn and enrolled
in a later season's class, moved to one, or of the other Network after a sex correction, they
are listed for a later weekend, the unrecorded row holding nobody back and never written. One
weekend a season being the rule, a student with a row in a season, of either Network, is carried
to a later season, not to that season's other weekend.

**5. The page opens on the earliest season whose weekend is no more than two weeks past**, so
who came can be recorded after it, else on the latest.

**What the owner ruled here is the screen** (point 1) **the late joiner** (point 2) **and the student who leaves a weekend's list** (point 4). The rest beyond 0501's own words is this
step's reading and not an owner ruling: waiting for lesson 4, no birthday meaning *Can't go*, a
saved lesson with no mark meaning *Needs make-up*, the two weeks of point 5, corrections with no
window until final grades, and carrying an earlier season's student who was never enrolled.

Every route is `equipping.manage` at Whole Church, the Registrar's and the Admin's (Section 7).
Filing the Encounter's Training graduation from who came is not part of this step: it is the
later step that brings Training's graduations from the school (0501, point 19).
