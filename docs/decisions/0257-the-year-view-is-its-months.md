# 2026-09-19 — The year view is its months, and its year row adds up two figures

Section 18 offers a year of reports and decision 0222 says a yearly report returns the months
that have begun and names them. No ruling said what the Reports screen shows for a year. The
owner's Claude Design handoff draws two tables: a person-by-month grid and a month-by-month
table of the report's own figures.

## The ruling

**1. The year view is one table, one row per month of the selected year that has begun.**
The table names the months it covers. A month that has not begun is not shown.

**2. Each row is that month's own report**, requested and authorized exactly as the monthly
report is, for the same scope: Owed and Filed from its coverage line, never divided (decision
0224), the unique people it counts, and whether the month is still open (Section 17).

**3. The year row adds up Owed and Filed across the rows shown, and nothing else.** It shows no
people count and no classification for the year.

**4. A month the screen cannot read is said so on its row** and left out of the year row.

## Why

**The year row is limited to two figures because only they add up.** Coverage is counted by
obligation, and each obligation falls in exactly one month, so adding the months counts none
twice. Unique people and classification do not: somebody who came in March and in June is
one person for the year and would be counted twice by a sum, and Section 20 already records
that a year's classification cannot be taken from its months. The owner chose to show neither.

**Building on the monthly report was chosen over the yearly routes Section 22 lists.** Each
month keeps the authorization the monthly report already has, so the year view needs no rule
about how a reader's reach is measured across a year. The yearly routes stay listed and unbuilt.

**The person-by-month grid is not built.** It would be the first report naming individual
people, which `CLAUDE.md` records as open, together with which graph grants a per-person view
and which leaders a Network's drill-down offers. It waits for those rulings, after the pilot.
