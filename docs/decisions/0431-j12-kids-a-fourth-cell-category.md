# 2026-11-03 — J12 Kids, a fourth Cell category, first in order

Section 10 gave a Cell exactly three categories, Youth, Young Pro and Couple, and Section 25's
rule 7 forbade a fourth unless requirements changed. On 2026-11-03 the owner changed them: the
church is introducing Cells for children, called J12 Kids. The owner was shown the six screens a
category reaches, on a laptop and a phone, with J12 Kids added, and chose to build it as shown.

## The ruling

**1. A Cell's category is one of four: J12 Kids, Youth, Young Pro and Couple.** It is stored as
`J12_KIDS` and shown as *J12 Kids*, written so.

**2. The four are always in that order, youngest to oldest**, on every screen that offers a choice
of category and in every report that counts by one: *Total* first, then *J12 Kids*, *Youth*,
*Young Pro* and *Couple*.

**3. A J12 Kids Cell is a Cell like the others in every rule** (owner, 2026-11-03): its members
share their leader's Network, so it is boys with boys or girls with girls, and it is created,
handed over, recategorised and closed as any Cell is.

**4. *# of Cell Groups* counts it in its own figure**, for the Senior Pastors and for a Full view
leader's branch (decision 0380), beside the other three, and the four add up to the total. On a
laptop the five figures sit on one line; on a phone *Total* runs across the top and the four
categories sit two by two. This widens the categories decisions 0326 and 0327 listed, and the
identity 0327 stated over three of them now holds over four.

## Why

The church's ministries run by stage of life, and a list read youngest to oldest is the order a
reader already expects. The Network rule is kept because the owner wants the children's Cells
divided as the adults' are.

Nothing here changes who may be a member or how a member is recorded: a child in a J12 Kids Cell
is a member under the rules that govern every member.
