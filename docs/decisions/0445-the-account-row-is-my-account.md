# 2026-11-05 — The sidebar's account row: an initial, the name, and *My Account*

At the foot of the sidebar the signed-in person's first name stood alone, with an underlined
*Account and session* link under it (decision 0245's account, outside the items). Once decision
0444 gave every item an icon, that corner was the one place with none, and its label was the
longest in the sidebar. On 2026-11-05 the owner asked for an icon and a shorter label, and chose
from screenshots of a Full view leader's and the Admin's sidebar today, with a white circle
holding the person's initial, and with a person icon.

## The ruling

**1. The account is one row at the foot of the sidebar, and the whole row is the link**: the
person's first initial, emerald in a white circle, then their first name in white and *My
Account* under it in the items' mint. When the account page is open the row is marked as a
current item is, by the darker emerald and the white bar down its left. It stays apart from the
items, below its rule, as decision 0245 placed it.

**2. *My Account* replaces *Account and session*** as the link's words, the account page's
title and the name of the phone's account button, which keeps its person icon. The page itself
is unchanged: signing out and the other session controls stay on it.

## Why

Apps with a sidebar keep the person at its foot as a picture or initial beside their name, the
whole row leading to their account. The initial makes the row the reader's own where a person
icon would look the same for everyone, and the whole row as the link is a larger target than an
underlined line of small words. *My Account* is shorter, is what such links are usually called,
and matches this application's own words, *My Cell* and *My 12*.
