# 2026-09-19 — The recording queue has a branch view beside the leader's own

Decision 0251 made the recording queue the actor's own work and nobody else's, restating the
owner's choice of 2026-09-15. On 2026-09-19 the owner compared their Claude Design handoff with
the built queue and chose its "Whose" switch — *People I oversee · My own Cells* — knowing it
reverses that choice.

## The ruling

**1. The queue has two views.** *My own Cells* is decision 0251's population, unchanged. *People
I oversee* adds the same outstanding work for everyone beneath the actor in the pastoral tree as
it stands now.

**2. In the branch view a Cell meeting is listed when the leader Section 7 authorizes to file it
(decision 0251, item 4) is the actor or beneath them, and `cell.view_subtree` covers that leader.** The tree sets
who is in the branch; a capability authorizes each row (Section 7). Each row names that leader.

**3. A row is listed only where the actor may record it**: their own meeting, or one they may
record on the filing leader's behalf under `cell.take_attendance` and `cell.submit_on_behalf`,
measured against that leader
(decision 0192). The API decides this per row, and every row carries Record, because every queue entry carries the action that resolves it (Sections 15 and 19). A meeting the actor may see but not record stays on the attention list below the queue (owner's choice of 2026-09-19).

**4. The branch view lists Cell meetings only.** DCC stays the actor's own checklist in both
views, because a DCC checklist is always its leader's own and no screen yet records on another
leader's behalf; a Sunday row would have offered no act (owner's choice of 2026-09-19). Who
still owes a DCC record stays on each Sunday's coverage gaps (decision 0228), reached from the
DCC calendar.

**5. The route stays the one route**, guarded by `cell.take_attendance` against the actor; the
view is a parameter, and *My own Cells* is its default.

## Why

**The same comparison settled three more things on this screen**, all display rather than rule:
the month's figures sit beside the queue with last month's under each and no bar (decision 0224
forbids a fraction), each row reads the Cell's category, meeting time, leader, size and how long
it has waited, and the reader's own DCC checklist is shown across the month's Sundays (decision
0194). The filter says DCC rather than Sundays.

**Section 14 already lets a leader record for someone beneath them.** What the 2026-09-15 choice guarded against, a
leader's list filling with other people's work, is kept by making the actor's own view the
default and the branch view something they choose.

**The branch is the pastoral tree, not the actor's grant.** A Whole Church grant would make the
branch view every outstanding meeting in the church, the error decision 0251 records catching
once already. An administrator outside the tree sees an empty branch view and keeps the
attention lists Section 15 gives them.
