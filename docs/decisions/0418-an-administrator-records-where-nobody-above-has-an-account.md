# 2026-11-01 — An administrator records a DCC line where nobody above has an account

A leader's people are recorded by the leader, or, where the leader holds no account, by the
nearest leader above who does (Section 9; decisions 0042, 0336 and 0403). Bishop Oriel and
Pastora Geraldine are the two Network roots and neither holds an account, so the walk up from
their 12 reaches nobody: Pending DCC Records showed *See Checklist* alone, the checklist it
opened was empty, and nobody could record those Sundays at all. Cell Groups were never
affected, an administrator records any Cell's meeting. The owner chose this from screenshots
on 2026-11-01, together with giving both roots their accounts.

## The ruling

**1. Where nobody above a leader held an account on the Sunday, and nobody above holds one
now, an administrator records that leader's people.** An administrator here is an account
holding `dcc.take_attendance` and `dcc.submit_on_behalf` at Whole Church, which by default is
the `ADMIN` role alone (decision 0322). It is decision 0403's walk taken one step further: that
walk first reads the accounts held on the Sunday, then the accounts held now, and only when
both reach nobody does this apply.

**2. On Pending DCC Records such a row carries Record for an administrator**, and reads *Has
no account · Nobody above has one, so an administrator records it*. Record opens the checklist
of the leader at the top of that chain, a Network root, with the root's people on it as though
the root held an account: their 12, then the people of any leader beneath them holding none.
Every other reader sees the same words and *See Checklist* alone.

**3. The record is saved as any record made on behalf is** (decision 0313): under the
administrator's name, the line staying the leader's obligation in every report (Section 20),
and a mark already recorded still needing `dcc.correct_subtree` to change.

**4. It ends by itself.** The day a leader in that chain is given an account, decision 0403
gives them the Sundays from that moment, and the walk reaches them rather than nobody. The
Sundays before it stay with the administrator until their week closes.

**5. Nothing else moves.** *Recording Progress* still counts such a line against nobody
(decision 0325), and the *Leaders Under Me* view is unchanged.

## Why

A Sunday nobody may record is a Sunday nobody records, and the two roots' 12 are the people
the church most needs counted. The administrator is the one account already trusted to record
across the whole church, and the arrangement lasts only until the roots hold their own
accounts.
