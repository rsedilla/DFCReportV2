# 2026-10-20 — Add a Person states the Sex as already decided for a leader under a leader

Decision 0305 started Add a Person's Sex choice on the adder's own sex when the adder holds a
pastoral assignment under a leader, and kept it a choice the encoder could change. On 19 and
20 Oct the owner asked for it to be already decided for those leaders rather than pre-selected,
and chose it from real screenshots of the form.

## The ruling

1. **For an adder who holds a pastoral assignment under a leader, Sex is stated, not offered.**
   The form shows the adder's own sex and its Network as a line, for example *Male · Men's
   Network*, with *Set from your Network. A woman is added by a leader in the Women's
   Network.* (or the same the other way round), and saves that sex. In practice this is every
   Primary and Cell leader.
2. **A Network root and anybody holding no pastoral assignment keep the choice**, with nothing
   pre-selected, as decision 0305 had it: they add people to both Networks. In practice these
   are Bishop Oriel, Pastora Geraldine and the Admin.
3. **It reads the adder's place in the tree, never their account's role**, as decision 0305
   did.

Nothing changes about what the server accepts or refuses.

## Why

Add a Person requires a pastoral leader, the server accepts only one the adder oversees, and
everyone such an adder oversees is in their own Network (Section 5). So for them the other sex
was never a save that could succeed: the choice decision 0305 kept open could only be refused.
Stating it removes a control that does nothing, and the line says what to do instead when a
person of the other sex needs adding.

Decision 0305 kept the choice so that an encoder could correct a slip before saving. For these
adders there was no other value to correct to, so nothing is lost. A sex wrong on the adder's
own record shows here wrongly, and is corrected on that record, as any recorded sex is.
