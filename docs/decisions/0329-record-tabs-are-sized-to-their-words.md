# 2026-10-10 — Record's tabs are sized to their words

Owner ruling, 2026-10-10, made while tidying the leader screens. It amends decision 0290's
"chosen by four equal buttons" and the sentence in Section 19 that carries it.

## The ruling

**1. Record's four lists are chosen by four buttons sized to their words, each still carrying
its count**, like the tabs of People, Growth and Cells. From a small screen up they sit in one
row on the left. On a phone they stay a grid of two by two, so four long labels do not wrap
into a ragged row. The same applies to the Senior Pastors' two Record tabs, which use the same
row.

**2. Reports keeps its equal tabs.** Decision 0292 gave them "equal buttons in the Record
screen's style", and the owner chose on 2026-10-09 that its five shorter labels fill one row.
That phrase now describes the style Record had then rather than the one it has now.

## Why

The owner saw the tab rows side by side and wanted them alike.
