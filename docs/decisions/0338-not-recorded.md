# 2026-10-13 — Not recorded

Owner ruling, 2026-10-13, made one place at a time from drawings and then real screenshots. The
words are the owner's Claude Design. It amends Section 13 and decision 0332, point 1.

## The ruling

**1. A Cell meeting or a DCC Sunday with no record reads *Not recorded*.** It was *Awaiting a
record*. It stays a word, never a colour.

**2. On the DCC month calendar and on a DCC Sunday's screen it reads *Not recorded · 3 to mark*,**
and a closed Cell's meeting *Not recorded · Cell closed*. *Not recorded · week closed*, added with
decision 0335's build, is unchanged.

**3. After a save, the link back reads *Back to Update Record*** and opens Update Record on the
half just recorded: Cell Group after a meeting, DCC after a Sunday. It was *Back to what's
awaiting a record*, and opened Record as it first opens, which for a Senior Pastor is Recording
Status (decision 0325).

**4. An empty Update Record reads *Nothing to record.*** for all four choices: Cell Group or DCC,
each with its own choice (*Own Cell*, *Own 12*) or *Leaders Under Me*.

**5. Only the words and the link change.** What is listed, and who sees it, are unchanged.

## Why

The tab has been *Update Record* since decision 0332, so the back link named a list that no
longer existed. *Not recorded* also begins the closed-week label, so a missing
record reads the same way everywhere and the words after it say whether it can still be
recorded.
