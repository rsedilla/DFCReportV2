# 2026-10-06 — Cells behind carries last month while it is open

On Record, *Awaiting a record* has a Whose switch (*Mine* / *People I oversee*, decision 0258) and
lists last month's work until the 7th, while *Cells behind* had no switch and listed this month
only. The owner chose this version from screenshots of both lists, beside their Claude Design,
which puts one Whose switch and one month at the top of Record over every list and figure.

## The ruling

**1. Cells behind carries last month's Cells behind while that month is open**, through the 7th,
each marked with its month. Section 15 already asks this: its list covers meetings "whose month is
still open".

**2. Cells behind keeps the reader's whole scope and has no Whose switch.** It is Section 15's list
for the leader who oversees the Cells, and decision 0314 made Reports' *Show only Cells behind* the
same list, so each month's link opens on that month's Cells. The Whose switch stays on Awaiting a record.

## Why

Option 2, the owner's design, would have shown only the Cells the reader leads under *Mine*, so
Record's "See every Cell behind" link would again open a different set of Cells, and it would have
settled the month figures' scope as well, which is a separate question.
