# 2026-11-13 — The Senior Pastors' *Figures for* is All, Men's and Women's

Owner ruling, 2026-11-13, chosen from screenshots of Bishop Oriel's CG Attendance on a phone and a
laptop, today beside the proposal.

## The ruling

**On the two Senior Pastors' CG Attendance and DCC Attendance, *Figures for* is three buttons,
*All*, *Men's* and *Women's*, in the style of the Monthly · Yearly switch, with no list of
names.** *All* is the whole church and is pressed when the tab opens. A Primary's figures open by
clicking the Primary's name in the table, and *Back to your report* returns; while a Primary is
open no button is pressed, and choosing one returns to the church or a Network.

*Men's* and *Women's* narrow CG Attendance by the Cell's Network (Section 20, decision 0469), which
that tab gains here, and DCC Attendance by membership (decision 0219), as its list already did. The
API is unchanged: both reports already answer a Network.

Every other reader with *Figures for*, the Admin and a Network Admin, keeps the list.

## Why

The owner noticed that choosing a Primary in the list and clicking the Primary's name led to the
same figures, and checked in the demo: both open the same address. The list also held the only way
to a single Network, so it could not simply go. Dashboards commonly drill into a row by clicking it,
with a visible way back, and keep a filter only for the choices the table cannot offer.

Considered and rejected: *Whole Church · Men's Network · Women's Network*, whose words wrapped onto
two lines on a phone and on a laptop; *All* fits, and the heading under it still says *Whole
Church*.

## What is owed

Nothing.
