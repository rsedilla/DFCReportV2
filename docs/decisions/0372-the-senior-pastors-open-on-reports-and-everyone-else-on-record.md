# 2026-10-19 — The Senior Pastors open on Reports, and every other account on Record

Decision 0245 landed a whole-church reader on `Reports` and everyone else on `Record`, read off
the reach of `reports.view_subtree`. By the role defaults that is the two Senior Pastors and the
Admin. On 19 Oct the owner, reading a table of what each account sees, asked whether the Admin
should open on `Record`, and chose it from real screenshots of both.

## The ruling

1. **The Senior Pastors' screens open on `Reports`; every other account opens on `Record`**,
   the Admin included. Primaries, Cell leaders and every other Leader account already opened
   on `Record`, and still do.
2. **It is read off the screens the server names** (decision 0323, point 5), not off a role and
   no longer off a capability's reach. The client derives nothing from a role.
3. **Nothing else moves.** The sidebar, each screen, and what any account may read or do are as
   they were. Every item stays one tap away, so this decides only the first screen after
   signing in.

## Why

The Senior Pastors read the whole church, and their `Record` holds Recording Progress alone
(decision 0325), so `Reports` is their work. The Admin reads the whole church too, but acts:
stepping in on Pending CG Records and Pending DCC Records (decision 0336), moving people on
*Awaiting Leader Assignment*, approving and closing Cells. Those start on `Record` and `Cells`,
so landing on `Reports` cost the Admin a tap every time. Reach of `reports.view_subtree` was a
proxy for "reads rather than records", and the Admin is the account it got wrong.

Rejected: an exception for the Admin role, which would have the client decide from a role.
