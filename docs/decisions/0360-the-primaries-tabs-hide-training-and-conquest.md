# 2026-10-17 — A Full view leader's tabs hide Training and Conquest

A Full view leader, the Primaries, had four `Reports` tabs, CG Attendance, DCC Attendance, SUYNL
and Conquest (decision 0340), and the three `Growth` tabs, SUYNL, Training and Conquest. On
17 Oct the owner asked for the Conquest tab to go from their Reports and the Training and
Conquest tabs from their Growth. They chose from real screenshots, and said the two will be used
again later.

## The ruling

1. **A Full view leader's `Reports` has three tabs**: CG Attendance, DCC Attendance and SUYNL.
2. **A Full view leader's `Growth` has one tab, SUYNL.** The sidebar item stays `Growth` and the
   tab row stays (the owner's option A), so the other two come back beside it without a rename.
   The Encounter seasons page, which the SUYNL report links to, marks SUYNL and links back to it
   for such a reader, rather than to Training.
3. **A person's page shows such a reader its SUYNL box and no Training box.**
4. **Only the screens change.** The account keeps every capability Full view gives (Section 7,
   decision 0323), Training and Conquest included, and the API answers their routes as before.
   Their pages stay reachable by address and show only the SUYNL tab. Nothing recorded is
   changed, and the Admin keeps every tab. The Senior Pastors had neither tab already
   (decision 0326).
5. **Who is a Full view leader here is as decision 0340 says it**: a reader holding
   `reports.view_subtree` short of Whole Church who is not a Senior Pastor.

## Why

The owner will use Training and Conquest with the Primaries later. Hiding the tabs is undone by
a screen change. Withdrawing the capabilities would be an authorization change now and another
to give them back. Option A keeps the sidebar and the tab row as the Primaries will meet them
again, rather than renaming `Growth` to `SUYNL` and back. The person page follows the tabs, so
a Primary is not shown Training in one place and not the other.
