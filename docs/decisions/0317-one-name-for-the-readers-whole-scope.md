# 2026-10-06 — One name for the reader's whole scope

The reader's whole scope was named three ways for a leader ("People you oversee" on Record,
"Everyone you oversee" in the Cell Groups and DCC *Figures for* pickers) and three for a reader
holding Whole Church ("Whole Church" on Record, "Everyone in your scope" and "The whole church" in
those pickers and the report headings). The owner chose this version from screenshots of every
place, beside their Claude Design, whose most common words are "people you oversee" and
"Whole Church".

## The ruling

**1. Wherever a screen names the reader's whole scope, it says *People you oversee*, or *Whole
Church* for a reader holding the capability at Whole Church.** That covers Record's month figures,
the *Figures for* pickers, and the report headings that named the church as a whole.

**2. Only the labels change.** What each covers, and every figure, is unchanged.

## Why

They match the switch *People I oversee* on Record, and *Whole Church* is the name Section 7 gives
that scope. Sentences that mention a scope in passing ("No Cell in your scope is behind") are not
names of it and are not covered.
