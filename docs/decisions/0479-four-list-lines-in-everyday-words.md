# 2026-11-12 — Four list lines in everyday words

Owner ruling, 2026-11-12, chosen from screenshots of the installed iPhone app, today beside the
proposal. It rewords the line under four lists, one of them set by decision 0432 and one by
decision 0436, and one row label, and changes no rule.

## The ruling

1. ***No Leader Yet*** says "People in your care who have no leader now, because their leader
   left or was archived.", in place of "People you oversee whose pastoral leader is not in the
   pastoral tree." A reader holding the list church-wide reads *in the church* (decision 0436).
   "A new leader is chosen by your Primary." stays (decision 0356).
2. ***Absent 3+ Months*** says "People marked Absent every month, and never Present, at Cell
   meetings and DCC Sundays in August, September, and October 2026. Anyone who joined during
   those months is not listed.", in place of decision 0432's longer sentence. The months and the
   year are still named as decision 0432 has them.
3. ***Cells to Approve*** says "Waiting for your approval: a new Cell, a new leader for a Cell,
   or a closed Cell reopened. Oldest first.", and a whole-church reader "Waiting for your
   approval across the church: …", in place of "Requests for a new Cell, a handover or a restart
   in your branch, oldest first."
4. ***SUYNL*** says what it is before the count: "Start Up Your New Life (SUYNL): ten lessons,
   one to one. 183 people in your care.", in place of decision 0436's count first.
5. **A Growth row the reader may not file for says "Another leader records these"**, in place of
   "Not yours to record": who does it rather than who does not. "Another" rather than "their
   leader", because a Network root has no leader; a reader's own row keeps "You · another leader
   records these" (decision 0280).

## Why

The owner found leaders reading these lines and not understanding them. "Pastoral tree",
"handover" and "restart" are this application's own words, not a leader's; the Absent line took
two sentences to say one rule; SUYNL gave a number before saying what it counts; and "Not yours
to record" said who does not record without saying who does.

Considered and rejected: "Their leader records these" for the row label, shown to the owner,
which is false of a Network root; and keeping "in your branch" on *Cells to Approve*, which the
owner's chosen line leaves out, the queue being the reader's own.

## What is owed

Nothing.
