# 2026-11-12 — The Senior Pastors' Record is Recording Progress alone

Owner ruling, 2026-11-12, chosen from screenshots of the Senior Pastors' Record on an installed
phone and on a laptop, today beside the proposal. It amends decision 0325, which gave the two
Senior Pastors' `Record` two tabs.

## The ruling

**1. The Senior Pastors' `Record` is *Recording Progress* alone.** *Update Records*, their own
work, is withdrawn from their screens. With one list left there is no tab row: the panel is
headed *Recording Progress*, with "How many leaders recorded on time, week by week or month by
month." under the heading from `sm` up and the heading alone on a phone, as Record's other lines
(decisions 0454, 0455 and 0476). The line beside the welcome reads "Whether the church has
recorded." An address naming *Update Records* opens *Recording Progress*.

**2. The Admin, or the Senior Pastor's Network Admin, records a Senior Pastor's records on their
behalf.** Both already can: an Admin's *DCC Records Due* rows carry *Record* wherever a recorder
exists, a Senior Pastor's 12 included (decision 0336), and a Network Admin records for a root of
their own Network who holds an account (decision 0469). Nothing new is built for it.

**3. The Senior Pastor stays the leader those records belong to.** Their 12 still report under
them, on time and late are judged as before, and no figure moves. A record made for them is
recorded as on behalf.

**4. Their capability to record is kept.** Only the tab is withdrawn: nothing in the API refuses a
Senior Pastor who records, so a direct link still works in a week the Admin is away. Making it
impossible would be an authorization change and was not asked for.

## Why

The owner's policy is that the Senior Pastors read the reports and the Admin records their
records. A list of their own outstanding work on their landing screen asked them to do work the
church does not expect of them. Design systems advise against a tab row holding one tab and
suggest a heading in its place, which also names the screen to a screen reader.

Considered and rejected: making the Admin the leader those records belong to, which would move
past figures and the attribution Section 20 fixes; and refusing the capability, which the owner
did not ask for.

## What is owed

Nothing. *Recording Progress* names a Senior Pastor as the leader behind when a record made on
their behalf is late, as it does today.
