# 2026-10-12 — A week is due the Monday after it, and closes two Mondays later

Owner ruling, 2026-10-12, made from the Tuesday Primaries meeting with Bishop Oriel and Pastora
Geraldine, its three open points settled the same day. It replaces Section 13's monthly
submission window (decisions 0007 and 0170) and Section 9's DCC window (decision 0021) with a
weekly one, from the week of Monday 26 October 2026.

## The ruling

**1. Every week is due at 12 noon on the Monday after it ends.** A week runs Monday to Sunday,
Asia/Manila (decision 0054). A Cell meeting belongs to the week of its **scheduled** date, as a
reschedule never moves its reporting month (Section 13); a DCC Sunday to the week it ends. The
week of Monday 26 October to Sunday 1 November 2026 is due at 12:00 on Monday 2 November.

**2. A record first made after the deadline is Late, and is still accepted for two weeks.** A
Cell meeting is first made when it is first reported, in any status; a DCC line when its first
version is written. A correction never makes an on-time record late.

**3. Then the week closes, two Mondays after its deadline at 12 noon,** and no leader may record
or correct it. The week above closes at 12:00 on Monday 16 November. An Admin may still amend it,
with a reason, audit logged, as an Admin amends a closed month today (Section 13, decision 0182).

**4. It starts with the week of Monday 26 October 2026.** A record dated before that week keeps
the old close, the end of the 7th of the following month, and is never Late: there was no
deadline. So October's meetings and Sundays of 1 to 25 October may be recorded until the end of
Saturday 7 November.

**5. A month is open while any of its weeks is, and final when its last week closes.** Its last
week is the one holding its last day, which always closes last, so October 2026 is final at
12:00 on Monday 16 November and a month after it between the 15th and the 21st of the next. Every
rule that reads "the month is open" or "closed" for a figure (Sections 17 and 20, the stored
figures of decision 0320, Pending CG Reports carrying last month of decision 0315) reads this.
**Every rule that reads it to allow a recording reads the record's week** (points 1 to 4): a
closed Cell's meetings (Section 7), which also bounds where they are listed (Sections 15 and
19), a reschedule's target date (decision 0195), and the DCC calendar, which neither adds nor
removes a Sunday whose week has closed.

**6. Recording Status counts on time first, late beside it** (decisions 0325 and 0334). A
leader is on time in a column when everything they owed in it was first made by its deadline,
late when all of it was made and some after. "3 of 5 leaders on time · 60%", with "1 more
recorded late" under the box; a row reads "2 of 3 on time, 1 late". What counts as recorded is
unchanged, so a DCC Sunday is on time when the first record that counts it was. A period with no
deadline in it says recorded rather than on time. It opens on **the week whose deadline last
passed**, labelled with that deadline.

**7. An Admin's Record gains *Recording Status*, the Senior Pastors' church-wide one, as its last
tab.** An Admin is the one who amends a closed week, so the one who needs to see what went late.

**8. Update Record shows when each row is due** ("Due Mon 2 Nov, 12NN") or, once late, when it
closes ("Late · closes Mon 16 Nov, 12NN"); a row from before 26 October, which has no deadline,
says when it closes, the end of Saturday 7 November. A closed week drops off the list. Late is always a
word, never only a colour (Sections 13, 17 and 19).

**9. No reminder is sent.** The due date on Update Record is the reminder. Section 13's
notifications stay specified and unbuilt.

## Why

The goal is to train leaders to record quickly. A deadline the day after each week, reviewed at
the Tuesday meeting, makes recording a weekly habit; two weeks of late recording forgive a missed
Monday without leaving a week open for a month; and an Admin amendment keeps genuine mistakes
fixable. The owner chose this over keeping the monthly close, over a deadline that only marked
lateness, and over closing to an Admin as well. It starts on 26 October so it can be announced at
the Primaries meeting of 20 October, before any deadline exists, and so no record already made
becomes Late under a rule nobody knew.

## What this supersedes

Decisions 0007, 0021 and 0170 in full; 0182, 0195, 0315, 0325 and 0334 where they read the
monthly window. Sections 7, 9, 13, 15, 17, 19, 20, 21 and 24 are amended.
