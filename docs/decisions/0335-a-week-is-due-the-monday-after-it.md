# 2026-10-12 — A week is due the Monday after it, and closes two Mondays later

> **DRAFT for the owner to read. Not yet amended into `SKILL.md`, not indexed, not built.**
> The owner's decisions of 2026-10-12 are settled; the points under *To confirm* are not.

Owner ruling, 2026-10-12, made from the Tuesday Primaries meeting with Bishop Oriel and Pastora
Geraldine. It replaces Section 13's monthly submission window (decisions 0007 and 0170) and
Section 9's DCC window (decision 0021) with a weekly one, and touches every rule that reads
"the month's window is open".

## The ruling

**1. Every week is due at 12 noon on the Monday after it ends.** A week runs Monday to Sunday,
Asia/Manila (decision 0054). The week of Monday 28 September to Sunday 4 October 2026 is due at
12:00 on Monday 5 October. The Tuesday Primaries meeting reviews the week that has just ended.

**2. After the deadline a record is still accepted for two weeks, and is marked Late.** The week
above may still be recorded until 12:00 on Monday 19 October; anything first recorded after
12:00 on 5 October is Late. A correction made inside those two weeks does not make an on-time
record late: lateness is decided by when a record was **first** made.

**3. Then the week closes, and no leader may record or correct it.** From 12:00 on Monday
19 October the week above is closed to every leader. **An Admin may still correct it**, with a
reason, audit logged, as an Admin amends a closed month today (Section 13, decision 0182).

**4. Which week a record belongs to.**
- A Cell meeting belongs to the week of its **scheduled** date, as a reschedule never moves its
  reporting month today (Section 13).
- A DCC line belongs to the week that ends on its Sunday, so a Sunday is due the next day at noon.
- A leader has recorded their DCC checklist for a Sunday on time only when every line they owe
  for it was recorded by the deadline; one line late makes that Sunday late for them.

**5. A month's figures become final when its last week closes.** The last week of a month is the
one holding its last day, so September 2026 (last week 28 Sep – 4 Oct) is final at 12:00 on
19 October, and October 2026 (last week 26 Oct – 1 Nov) at 12:00 on 16 November. Until then the
month is **open**: reports, Recording Status and the stored month figures (Section 20) treat it
exactly as they treat an open month today. "Open until 7 November" becomes "open until
16 November, 12NN".

**6. Everywhere a rule asks whether "the month's window is open" to allow a recording, it now
asks whether the record's week is open** (points 1 to 3): a closed Cell's meetings (Section 7's
exception), the recording queue, Pending CG Reports carrying last month's Cells, and the DCC
calendar command's refusal to touch a closed month (which becomes: a month with any closed week).

**7. Recording Status** (decisions 0325 and 0334)
- It counts **on time first**, with late beside it: "3 of 5 leaders on time · 60%", and under
  the box "1 more recorded late". Each row reads, for example, "2 of 3 on time, 1 late".
- It opens on **the week whose deadline last passed**, labelled "Due Mon 12 Oct, 12NN"; ›
  moves to the week still being recorded, which reads "so far".

**8. Update Record** shows, under each meeting or Sunday, when it is due ("Due Mon 19 Oct,
12NN") or, once late, when it closes ("Late · closes Mon 26 Oct, 12NN"). A closed week drops
off the list. "Late" is always a word, never only a colour (Sections 13, 17 and 19).

## Why

The goal is to train leaders to record quickly. A deadline the day after each week, reviewed at
the Tuesday meeting, makes recording a weekly habit; two weeks of late recording forgive a
missed Monday without leaving a week open for a month; and an Admin correction keeps genuine
mistakes fixable, so reports and Growth and Conquest figures stay right. The owner chose this
over keeping the monthly close, over a deadline that only marked lateness, and over closing to
an Admin as well.

## To confirm before building

1. **From when it applies.** Proposed: from the first full week after it is deployed. Every
   earlier week keeps today's rule, closing with its month on the 7th.
2. **Whether an Admin sees Recording Status.** Today an Admin has no Recording Status tab, so
   would not see on-time and late counts. Optional.
3. **Notifications.** Nothing reminds a leader before Monday noon today. Out of scope unless
   asked for.
