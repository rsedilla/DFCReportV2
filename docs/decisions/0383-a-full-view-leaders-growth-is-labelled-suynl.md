# 2026-10-22 — A Full view leader's Growth is labelled SUYNL

Decision 0360 left a Full view leader's `Growth` holding the `SUYNL` tab alone, Training and
Conquest hidden. The item kept the name `Growth`, so a Primary clicked a word that names three
things and found one, while the leaders under them reach the same screen by an item named
`SUYNL` (decision 0323). On 22 Oct the owner looked at real screenshots of the sidebar, a
person's page and the Full view tick, and ruled.

## The ruling

1. **A Full view leader's fifth sidebar item reads `SUYNL`**, as a Leader account without Full
   view's does, and opens the same page. Its heading reads `SUYNL`. The tab row with its one
   tab stays.
2. **A person's page heads its Growth frame `SUYNL` for a reader who is never shown Training**,
   a Leader account with Full view or without, and the row under it no longer repeats the word,
   which stays for a screen reader. A reader shown Training, such as the Admin, keeps `Growth`
   over the two halves.
3. **The Full view tick says so**: "… sees Record, Reports, People, Cells and SUYNL over their
   branch."
4. **The Admin keeps `Growth`** with its three tabs, SUYNL, Training and Conquest, and the
   Senior Pastors are unchanged.

A Full view leader is told apart as every other screen tells one apart, by `reports.view_subtree`
held short of Whole Church on screens that are not the Senior Pastors' (decision 0380), never by
a role. Nothing the API answers changes.

## Why

A label names what is behind it. Under decision 0360 nothing behind a Full view leader's
`Growth` is anything but SUYNL, and the two kinds of leader calling one screen by two names
made the Primaries the only readers who had to learn that they were the same.

**Point 2 reaches a Leader account without Full view too**, whose frame also held SUYNL under
`Growth`, because the rule is about what the frame holds rather than who reads it.

**If Training and Conquest return to a Full view leader, `Growth` returns with them**: the
label follows what the item holds. The owner also raised naming the item *Destiny Training*;
that is left to be settled with Equipping, which is where Life Class, the Encounter and the
Schools of Leaders are planned to live, since SUYNL is consolidation and Conquest is goals.
