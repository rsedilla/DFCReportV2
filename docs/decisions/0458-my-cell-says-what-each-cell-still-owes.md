# 2026-11-08 — My Cell says what each Cell still has to record

On the recording screens, the *My Cell* tab listed each Cell with its schedule and two equal
buttons, stacked on a phone, and *Your requests* as boxed rows under a sentence. A leader could not
tell from the tab whether anything was left to do. The owner chose this layout from screenshots of
the tab, today beside the proposal, at phone and laptop widths.

## The ruling

1. **The tab reads in order**: how many Cell Groups are in the reader's care, then *You lead*, then
   *Your requests*. While the month is open, *You lead* carries the open tag (Section 17), because a partial line such as *1 of 4* has to say the month is still running.
2. **A Cell's card** carries its name with its member count beside it, its schedule and Cell ID, and
   this month's coverage line: *3 of 4 recorded this month*, followed by *· 1 to record* when the
   Cell is behind. Both figures, and what counts as behind, are the server's (Section 12, decisions
   0225 and 0267). *0 of 0* is shown as it stands.
3. **Its buttons are *Meetings* and *Members***, side by side at every width. *Meetings* is filled on
   every Cell, whatever its coverage, so that a Cell behind is told by the words alone (Section 13).
4. **On this tab, *Your requests* is a plain list**: the leader's name, under it what was asked and
   where it stands (*New Cell · approved 19 Oct*), and the state beside it. The sentence about the
   list is behind an ⓘ, on a phone too. A Full view leader's *Your requests* on `Record` is unchanged.

What the reader may see, and every figure, are unchanged. The coverage line is read from the same
Cells list this tab already asked for.

## Why

A leader opens this tab to see whether anything is waiting. The line answers that on the card, and
the button beside it is the way to act.

The line is two figures and never a percentage, and nothing is sorted or coloured by it: a leader
sees only the Cells they lead, and Section 13's prohibition on ranking and on value-laden encoding
is untouched.

Considered and rejected: filling *Meetings* only on a Cell with something to record, which the first
mock-up showed, because that encodes a Cell's state in colour.
