# 2026-11-13 — The Senior Pastors read Attendance by month, with no Weekly

Owner ruling, 2026-11-13, chosen from screenshots of Bishop Oriel's CG Attendance on a phone and a
laptop, today beside the proposal. It narrows decision 0443 for the two Senior Pastors alone.

## The ruling

**The two Senior Pastors' CG Attendance and DCC Attendance offer *Monthly* and *Yearly*, with no
*Weekly*.** The switch is two buttons of one width, and the tabs open on Monthly. An address naming
a week, kept from before, opens the month for them, as an address naming a quarter does for every
reader. Their three *#* tabs already had Monthly and Yearly alone, so all five of their tabs now
offer the same two.

The Admin, a Network Admin and a Full view leader keep *Weekly*, *Monthly* and *Yearly*. The API is
unchanged and still answers a week.

## Why

The Bishop wants a monthly report. A week is the unit a leader records and is followed up on, due
the Monday after; the Senior Pastors record nothing of their own (decision 0480) and read the church
by month and its trend, and the follow-up of a slow week is on their Record's *Recording Progress*.
Church tools built for pastors, and cell-church practice, put weekly reports with the leaders and
monthly trends with the senior pastor.

Considered and rejected: hiding *Weekly* from a Full view leader too, since a Primary follows their
12 week by week (the pilot will show whether they use it); and adding *Quarterly* back for the
Senior Pastors, which decision 0443 withdrew a week earlier and *Yearly*'s twelve months already
show.

## What is owed

Nothing.
