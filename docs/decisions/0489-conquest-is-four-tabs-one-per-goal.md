# 2026-11-14 — Conquest is four tabs, one per goal

Owner ruling, 2026-11-14, as Bishop Oriel asked, chosen from screenshots of his Conquest page on a
phone and a laptop, today beside the proposal. The fourth stage of the Senior Pastors' screens
(decision 0488).

## The ruling

**1. Conquest has four tabs, one per goal, in ladder order: *Win 3*, *Open a Cell*, *Completion
of 12* and *Raise 12 Leaders*.** They sit two by two on a phone and in one row from a laptop.
The page opens on *Win 3*; the address's `goal` names the tab, and one it does not know opens
*Win 3*.

**2. Each tab shows how many have reached its goal, then everyone.** One box carries the goal's
count of people who have reached it, read and not pressed. Under it every person the reader
oversees is listed, with their progress on that goal alone: the month it was first reached with
today's count beneath, or how far it is, as before (Section 27). The four cards that narrowed
the list to those who had reached a goal are withdrawn, and so is each row's *N of 4*.

**3. Unchanged:** the search, *Only my disciples*, the first-name order (decision 0359), no
ordering by progress (decision 0009), no colour, and nothing to tick. A tab keeps the search and
*Only my disciples*.

**4. The page is the same for every reader of it**: the two Senior Pastors, the Admin and a
Network Admin.

## Why

The table carried four goals side by side, and on a phone each person was a tall card of four
lines. One goal at a time reads at a glance, and listing everyone rather than only those who
reached it shows who is close as well as who is there.

Considered and rejected: listing only the people who reached the tab's goal, which hides who is
close; and keeping the four cards as filters above the full table.

## What it does not change

No capability, route or record. The list route still accepts `goal`, which the page no longer
sends.
