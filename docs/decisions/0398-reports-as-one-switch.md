# 2026-10-28 — Reports as one switch

Owner ruling, 2026-10-27, chosen from real screenshots of four layouts (option C), and the
button sizes agreed on 2026-10-28 from screenshots of the switch as built.

Decision 0377 put a row reading *By period* and *Last 12 months* under the Senior Pastors' five
`Reports` tabs, and decision 0380 gave a Full view leader the same row. Under it sat a second
switch for the length, Weekly to Year, then the month. Two rows of buttons chose one thing: how
far back to look.

## The ruling

1. **One switch replaces the two.** On the Senior Pastors' and a Full view leader's five tabs,
   the *By period* / *Last 12 months* row goes. In the report's own bar, one switch reads the
   tab's lengths and then *Last 12 months*:
   - a Full view leader's CG and DCC Attendance: **Weekly | Monthly | Last 12 months**;
   - the Senior Pastors' CG and DCC Attendance: **Weekly | Monthly | Quarterly | Year | Last 12
     months**, keeping the four lengths they have;
   - every *#* tab: **Monthly | Last 12 months**, a count being taken a month at a time.
2. **The month stepper sits beside the switch, and *Last 12 months* has none.** A Full view
   leader's note under the bar, whether the period is still open, stays. The Senior Pastors' open
   period still says *so far*, now just after the stepper. *Figures for* and *Show* stay in the
   bar after the switch.
3. **One size for every button.** Every button of the switch is as wide as *Last 12 months*, in
   one row from a small screen up. On a phone they sit two a row across the bar, and where an odd
   one is left, *Last 12 months* takes the whole last row, so no label breaks. Every month stepper
   is the joined box, ‹ month ›, with one width for every month so the arrows do not move as it
   steps; this includes the *#* tabs, where an Admin's *# of People* has the same stepper.
4. **Nothing a figure shows changes**, and nothing else moves: the tabs keep their addresses,
   *Last 12 months* keeps `/trends`, and choosing a length or *Last 12 months* keeps the tab, as
   decision 0377 had it.

This replaces decision 0377's point 2 as to where the switch sits and what it reads; what each
view shows stands.

## Why

A pastor or a leader was choosing *By period* in one row and then a length in the next, where the
two are one choice. One switch saves a row on every one of these tabs, and the lengths and the
twelve months sit side by side.
