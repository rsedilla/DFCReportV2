# 2026-11-08 — Asking for a new Cell is a tab beside My Cell

On the recording screens, a Leader without Full view reaches their own Cells from the `Cells`
item (decision 0371). The page was titled *My Cell*, its one tab read *My Cell* too, and asking
for a new Cell was a *Request New Cell* button in the header that opened a dialog. In the
installed app the owner marked three things: the title and the tab said the same words one
under the other; the button pushed the Cells down a row; and the dialog, a long form, opened as
a tall box scrolled inside itself over a greyed page. Every change below was chosen from
screenshots of the app at phone, iPad and laptop widths.

## The ruling

1. **The page is titled *Cells***, as the sidebar and the phone's bottom bar name it. The line
   beside the title, *The Cells you lead, in November 2026*, goes: the count under the tabs says
   how many the reader leads. This replaces the part of decision 0371 that kept the title *My
   Cell*. The Cell pages reached from it name their section *Cells* too.
2. **Asking for a new Cell is the second tab, *Request New Cell***, beside *My Cell*, at an
   address of its own, `/my-cell/new`. Its form fills the tab's pane rather than a dialog. The
   header button goes.
3. **Sent, the form returns to *My Cell***, where the request heads *Your requests*. The form
   has no *Cancel*: the *My Cell* tab is the way out. If something is filled in, leaving by that
   tab asks *Discard this request?* first, with *Keep editing* as the safe answer, and closing or
   reloading the page asks in the browser's own words.
4. **When the reader leads no Cell**, *My Cell* says so and its *Request one* links to the tab.
5. **The form's words are shorter.** The sentence above the form goes. *Who will lead it* reads
   *Leader*, with *Someone you oversee, not you.* under it. *Three members* reads *Members*,
   with *3 people from the leader's Network.* under it; each member's box no longer repeats a
   hint. *Which day* and *What time* read *Day* and *Time*, the time's hint *Manila time,
   24-hour.* Every search box reads *Search by name*, and a screen reader hears which one: *for
   the leader*, *for member 1*.
6. **What happens to somebody already in a Cell is behind an ⓘ beside *Members***, on a phone
   too: *Someone already in a Cell moves to the new one once it is approved. Someone new is
   added first, under Add People.*

What a request may name, who decides it and what is refused are unchanged (Section 10). The
members' wording is the shared component's, so the Admin's *New Cell* page and the restart
dialog read the same.

## Why

A screen's title should be the place the reader tapped to reach it. *Cells* is that word, and
the tabs then say which part of it the reader is on.

Installed apps open a long form as a screen of its own with a way back, not as a box over the
page, and Apple's and Google's guidance keep dialogs for short questions. An address of its own
lets the phone's Back return to *My Cell*.

A form's labels name the field and a hint stays under it only where the reader needs it to fill
the field in. *Not you* and *the leader's Network* are rules that stop a request, so they stay
in view. What happens to somebody already in a Cell, and where somebody new is added, is
background, so it is behind the ⓘ. It shows on a phone because, without it, a leader cannot tell
why somebody new is not found.

Considered and rejected: moving the header button into the tab row as a button, which still
opened the dialog; and two columns of choices for Category and Day, left for another day.
