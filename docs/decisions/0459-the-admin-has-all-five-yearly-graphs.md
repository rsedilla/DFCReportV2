# 2026-11-08 — The Admin has all five Yearly graphs

Decision 0443 gave the Admin *Yearly* on *Attendance* and kept *Counts* to its month. The screen
rule of decision 0379, that the twelve months do not open for the Admin, was never changed with it,
so the Admin's *Yearly* button opened "This page isn't part of your screens". The owner asked for
the Admin to have the twelve-month graph on all five figures, as the Senior Pastors do, and chose
from screenshots at laptop, iPad and phone widths.

## The ruling

1. **The Admin reads the last 12 months of all five figures**: CG Attendance, DCC Attendance,
   # of Cell Groups, # of Cell Leaders and # of People. This replaces point 4 of decision 0443, so
   far as it kept the Admin's *Counts* to its month, and the part of decision 0379 that kept the Admin
   off the twelve months.
2. **The graph is the Senior Pastors'**: Whole Church and each root's branch, or one leader's chosen
   under *Show*, one leader at a time so that it cannot rank them, and its colours, which Section 23's
   one exception now names on all five figures.
3. ***Counts* keeps its tab and its switch.** *Monthly* / *Yearly* sits beside *Cell Groups* / *Cell
   Leaders* / *People*, the month following on the next row where the three do not fit, and choosing
   another count on the graph stays on the graph. *Encounter
   Readiness* and *Training* have no graph.

What the Admin may read is unchanged: the route serves a Whole Church holder of
`reports.view_subtree` for every figure, as it already did.

## Why

The Admin already held the capability and the route already answered, so the only thing keeping the
graphs from them was the screens, and for *Attendance* that was a defect rather than a decision.
