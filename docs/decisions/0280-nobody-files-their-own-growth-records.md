# 2026-09-23 — Nobody files their own Growth records

Sections 27 and 28 give a Network root's records to any actor whose confirming grant is Whole
Church, and a Senior Pastor is a root holding that grant, so each root could file their own.
`CLAUDE.md` carried whether they may as a Stop Condition owed by the write endpoint, which is
now being built.

The owner chose this answer from a drawing of a Senior Pastor's Training tab.

## The ruling

**Nobody files or corrects a SUYNL lesson, a graduation or a Conquest confirmation about
themselves.** A write naming the actor's own Person is refused, whatever their grant. Their own
row is shown and cannot be ticked.

**A root's records are filed by another actor whose confirming grant is Whole Church**, which is
the other Senior Pastor or an Admin, and still name no confirming leader.

## Why

**Every one of these rows is a leader's statement about somebody they disciple.**

**It is the stricter of the two answers**, and a rule that later admits self-filing loosens
nothing already recorded.

## What it does not decide

**Whether a person may record their own DCC attendance**, which Section 9 answers the other way
for a root and `CLAUDE.md` still records as open. Growth and DCC are allowed to differ: a DCC
record is a leader counting who was in the room, and a Growth row is a statement about a
disciple.
