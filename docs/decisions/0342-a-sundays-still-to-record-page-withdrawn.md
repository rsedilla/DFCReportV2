# 2026-10-14 — A Sunday's still-to-record page is withdrawn

Owner ruling, 2026-10-14, chosen from real screenshots of the page beside Record's Pending DCC
Reports on the demo. It amends Section 9 and decision 0228.

## The ruling

**The page naming the leaders who still owe a record for one Sunday (`/dcc/{id}/gaps`) is
withdrawn.** Record's Pending DCC Reports (decision 0336) names those leaders for every open
Sunday, with the action that resolves each. `GET /api/v1/dcc/events/{id}/coverage-gaps` remains
and no screen reads it.

## Why

Its only link was on Filed Reports, which decision 0340 withdrew, so the page could be reached
only by typing its address. A second screen showing the same leaders, one Sunday at a time and
without the action, adds nothing Pending DCC Reports does not already give.
