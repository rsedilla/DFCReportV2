# 2026-09-19 — A person's page carries their Sundays of the month, counted out of N

The owner's Claude Design handoff gives a person's page a card, *Sundays this month*, with last
month's figure under it. The first build counted the month's Sundays held so far, which is not
Section 9's N: mid-month the card said "1 of 2" while the monthly report measured the same person
against N = 4. On 2026-09-19 the owner chose the card counted as the report counts it.

## The ruling

**1. A person's page shows their Sundays attended in the current month and in the month before,
each as "attended of N"**, where attended is their present, live records on Sundays not removed,
and N is Section 9's: the month's Sundays that carry a DCC event, including those still to come.
It is the figure the monthly report puts them in a bucket by, stated as two numbers and never
divided.

**2. Each figure names its month, and says so while that month is open** (Section 17), until the
7th of the month after.

**3. It is read under `dcc.view_subtree`**, as the rest of that section is (decision 0247). The
month's Sundays are read against the reader, so a reader whose grant reaches the person and not
themselves is told the figure is not available rather than shown one.

**4. Section 19 gains the person's page as a home for that person's DCC figures**.

## Why

**One figure, one definition.** Held-so-far reads naturally mid-month, and it would be a second
answer to a question the report already answers. A leader who compares the page with the report
must see the same numbers.

**No Cell card.** Nothing reads one person's Cell attendance: a Cell record is read one meeting at
a time (decision 0246).
