# 2026-11-12 — The Senior Pastors' Attendance is two tabs

Owner ruling, 2026-11-12, chosen from screenshots of the Senior Pastors' Reports on a laptop and a
phone, today beside the proposal. It amends decision 0420 for the two Senior Pastors alone.

## The ruling

**The two Senior Pastors' `Reports` has five tabs: *CG Attendance*, *DCC Attendance*, *# of Cell
Groups*, *# of Cell Leaders* and *# of People*.** The *Attendance* tab with a *Cell Group* / *DCC*
switch inside it (decision 0420) is not theirs any more: each report is a tab of its own, and the
switch is gone, Weekly, Monthly and Yearly taking its place on the line. The five sit on one row
from `lg` up; on a phone they are two to a row, *# of People* filling the third. Each tab opens the
same report at the same address, with the same figures, and the month and the twelve-month view
travel from tab to tab as before.

**Every other reader keeps decision 0420's *Attendance* tab and its switch**: the Admin, a Network
Admin and a Full view leader.

## Why

The owner was concerned the Bishop and Pastora Geraldine would be confused. Reaching DCC took a
tab and then a switch inside it, a tab within a tab, which design systems advise against (Pega:
never tabs within tabs; American Express: nesting doubles the complexity). The Senior Pastors only
read these reports, so the shortest way to each is a tab of its own.

Considered and rejected: shorter labels (*CG*, *DCC*) to keep a phone at two rows, which the owner
did not choose, the full names reading more plainly; and changing every reader's tabs, which was
not asked.

## What is owed

Nothing.
