# 2026-10-25 — A week is due eight days after it ends

Owner ruling, 2026-10-25, a week before the first deadline of decision 0335 (12 noon, Monday 2
November 2026). The owner was worried that leaders have not yet mastered the app and would be
marked Late on the first Monday. Three ideas were weighed: dropping the word Late from a
leader's own Update Records, moving the start of the weekly rule to a later week, and giving
every week one more week before it is due. The owner chose the third, with the Late period kept
at two weeks.

## The ruling

**1. Every week is due at 12 noon on the second Monday after it ends**, eight days after its
Sunday, not on the Monday straight after it. The week of Monday 26 October to Sunday 1 November
2026 is due at 12:00 on Monday 9 November, so a meeting of Thursday 29 October recorded on
Wednesday 4 November is on time.

**2. The week still closes two Mondays after its deadline**, so a record first made after the
deadline is Late and accepted for two weeks, as before. The week above closes at 12:00 on Monday
23 November; after that only an Admin may amend it, with a reason, as decision 0335 says.

**3. A month therefore becomes final a week later**: when its last week closes, between the 22nd
and the 28th of the following month. October 2026 is final at 12:00 on 23 November, November at
12:00 on 28 December. Every rule that reads a month as open or closed reads this, as decision
0335 point 5 says.

**4. Recording Progress opens on the week whose deadline last passed**, which is now the week
before last. Nothing has passed before 12:00 on 9 November 2026, so until then it opens on the
current week as it did.

**Nothing else in decision 0335 changes.** The rule still starts with the week of 26 October
2026; a record dated before it keeps the end of the 7th of the following month and is never
Late; a correction never makes an on-time record late; Late is a word and never only a colour;
no reminder is sent.

## Why

A leader who records on the Wednesday or Thursday after their week is still on time, so the
first weeks of the rule mark fewer people Late while they learn the app. The cost is that the
Tuesday Primaries meeting reviews the week before last rather than last week, and a month's
figures stay open a week longer. The owner preferred this to moving the start, which would have
kept the one-day deadline once the rule began, and to keeping the start and only softening the
word Late.

## What this supersedes

(due 2 November, closed 16 November) and the 15th-to-21st range, point 6's opening week, and
point 8's example labels.
Sections 13 and 19 are amended.
