# 2026-11-07 — A person is archived, never removed

The security audit of 2026-11-02 asked what the church does when somebody asks for their details
to be removed. The owner was shown screenshots of a *Remove their details* action on the person's
page, which would have blanked the name, birthday and mobile number while keeping the counts. On
2026-11-06 the owner decided against it: "in the church we always redeem people." A person who
leaves may come back, and their record should be waiting for them.

## The ruling

**A Person's record is never deleted or blanked, including at the person's own request.** Somebody
who leaves is archived (Section 3, decision 0397), which takes them out of every current count from
that moment and keeps their name, Member ID, attendance, pastoral and Cell history, and audit
entries exactly as they were. The application offers no removal, and no screen or route is built
for one.

**Restore brings the record back.** It returns the Person to `CURRENT` with the same Member ID and
the whole of their history. As decision 0397 already says, a Cell, a pastoral leader and an account
do not come back with them: the Admin or a leader gives those again, as for anybody else.

**This replaces the "normally" in Section 3.** Section 3 said a Person "must not normally be
hard-deleted merely to reduce a leader's current People count", which left room for a deletion for
some other reason. There is none: archiving is the only way a person leaves the records.

**A formal request under the Data Privacy Act is the church's to decide**, with its legal adviser,
outside the application. The privacy page still lists the right to ask for removal, because the Act
gives it; what the application does is archive. Its *How long we keep it* paragraph, which said
"we will tell you what can be removed", now says a record can be restored if the person comes back
and that a request to remove it is decided by the church under the Act (owner, 2026-11-07, chosen
from screenshots of the page before and after).

## Why

A church record is a relationship, not an account to close. People drift away and return years
later, and the leader who welcomes them back should see their Member ID, the Cell they were in and
the Sundays they came, not a blank. Blanking the details would also leave past reports naming
nobody, and Section 3 keeps a merged or archived person's history so that a past month's figures
can always be explained.
