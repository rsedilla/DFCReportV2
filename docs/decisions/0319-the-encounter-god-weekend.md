# 2026-10-06 — The Encounter God Weekend, and Graduated on the readiness table

Two names Section 28 states differed from what the church says and from the screen beside
them. The owner settled both while the UI label audit was reviewed, from real screenshots.

## The ruling

**1. The weekend is the Encounter God Weekend.** Each season has a **Men's Encounter
Weekend** and a **Women's Encounter Weekend**, which is what the church calls them. *The
Encounter*, on its own, stays the name of the graduation: the record that somebody attended.

**2. The readiness table's first column is Graduated (ten of ten),** because ten lessons of
ten is graduation, and the card beside it already says Graduated.

**3. Only names change.** Seasons, their dates, the graduation and every count are as they
were (decisions 0295 to 0297).
