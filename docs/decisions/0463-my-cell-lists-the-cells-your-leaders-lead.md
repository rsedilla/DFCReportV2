# 2026-11-10 — My Cell lists the Cells your leaders lead

A Leader account without Full view saw, on *My Cell*, how many Cell Groups were in its care and
*M you lead · N − M led by your leaders* (decision 0438), and a card only for the Cells it led
(decisions 0323 and 0458). A leader overseeing a Cell whose leader has an account could count it
and not open it, except through the approved request that started it. On 2026-11-10 the owner
was shown the tab today beside the proposal, on a laptop and a phone, then the pages its buttons
open, and chose it.

## The ruling

1. **Under *You lead*, *Led by your leaders* lists every running Cell in the reader's care that
   the reader does not lead**, at every level of their branch, so the cards answer the count
   above them. Ten are shown, and *Show more* reads ten more.
2. **Each card is the reader's own card with its leader named**: *Led by* and the leader's full
   name under the schedule and Cell ID, and this month's coverage line as on the reader's own
   (Section 12, decisions 0225 and 0267), the open tag on *You lead* covering both.
3. **Both its buttons, *Meetings* and *Members*, are plain**, on every such card alike. The reader's
   own *Meetings* stays the one filled, because a Cell is recorded by its own leader (decision
   0322); a leader's card is for following up. Nothing on it is coloured by its coverage
   (Section 13).
4. **The pages behind the buttons are the ones every account uses and are unchanged.** A
   meeting of a leader's Cell opens read only, as it already did for anybody who may not record
   it, and what the Members page offers is what the server already admits.

It reads the list the Cells page reads, under `cell.view_subtree` over the reader's scope, which
puts the reader's own Cells first (decision 0400): the first page asks for as many more as the
reader leads and the screen leaves those out. It counts and shows no Cell the reader could not
already open, and adds no figure. It is read only where the count says the reader's care holds a
Cell they do not lead.

## Why

A leader who oversees others could see that their Cell Groups were growing and not how each one
was doing. The coverage line on a card answers that, and the read-only meeting is where they
follow up.

Considered and rejected: listing only the Cells of the reader's direct leaders, which would
leave the cards disagreeing with the count above them; and a filled *Meetings* on these cards,
which would ask the reader to record what is not theirs.

Not part of it: whether a leader may add members to a Cell they do not lead, which the owner
has been asked separately; and keeping *Cells* highlighted on a meeting page reached from
here, which a 2026-09-14 ruling places under *Record* and will be shown separately.
