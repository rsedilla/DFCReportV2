# 2026-11-04 — My Cell counts the Cell Groups in the leader's care

A Leader account without Full view sees its own Cells on the page titled My Cell (decisions 0323
and 0371), and nothing said how many Cell Groups its branch holds, while My People opens on a count
of its people (decisions 0422 and 0437). On 2026-11-04 the owner asked for a glimpse of it, was
shown one line in the manner of My People and three boxes, on the real screen, and chose the line.
It was built that day, parked behind decisions 0436 and 0437, and worded in their words when it
was resumed.

## The ruling

**1. My Cell opens on how many Cell Groups are in the reader's care as of today**, above their own
Cells: "*N* Cell Groups in your care", and under it "*M* you lead · *N − M* led by your leaders ·
as of today", the words decision 0436 gives the Cells page, which, as there, reads *People you
oversee · as of today* where the reader leads none (decision 0317). It is shown where there is at
least one, and a failed read says so.

**2. It counts the running Cell Groups whose leader is in the reader's branch now**, at every level,
as My People now lists every level of their people (decision 0437). It reads the counts the Cells
page's totals read (decision 0309), under `cell.view_subtree` over the reader's scope, so it counts no Cell the
reader could not open, and adds nothing anybody may see.

**3. It is one more small read beside the list**, and the page's links, *Members* and *This
month's meetings*, still load nothing until they are followed.

## Why

A leader who leads others had no sight of the Cell Groups growing under them without Full view's
reports. One line in My People's manner keeps the two pages alike, and keeps the leader's own Cells
on the first screen of a phone.
