# 2026-09-25 — Reports has six tabs

Reports carried two reports, Cells and DCC, behind a two-button switch, and each closed with a
long "Row by row" table of who had filed. Growth's counts lived only on the Growth tabs, and
Sections 27 and 28 said they did not enter the reporting surface.

The owner chose this answer from the running screen.

## The ruling

**Reports carries six tabs: Cell Groups, DCC, SUYNL, Training, Conquest and Filed reports**,
each its own address, equal buttons in the Record screen's style.

**Cell Groups and DCC keep their recording coverage first**, as Section 12 and decision 0202
require. Their Row by row table moves to **Filed reports**, which shows the same coverage figure
with By Cell or By Sunday, and By leader, beneath it, for a month and for Cell Groups or DCC.

**SUYNL, Training and Conquest show their Growth tab's counts, read only and as of now**: the
same routes, capabilities and populations. They are never counted by period, which is the line
Sections 27 and 28 hold. Ticking and recording stay under Growth, which keeps its sidebar item.
This reverses the part of decisions 0249 and 0250 that kept these counts out of Reports.

**Conquest's counts stay separate from Section 16's metrics** (owner's choice): on a tab of
their own, never beside *New Cell Leaders* or *Leaders with 12+ Direct Leaders*, which measure
something close to Open a cell and Raise 12 leaders and are not the same. Whether one replaces
the other or both sit together is decided again when Network Summary is built.

**Filed reports keeps the report's own scope**: one leader, one Cell or one Network where the
report had narrowed to it.

## Why

Reports is where a leader reads, so every figure worth reading is reachable there, and the
long list of who filed what no longer sits under the attendance figures. Coverage stays first
because it is the one figure recording less cannot improve.
