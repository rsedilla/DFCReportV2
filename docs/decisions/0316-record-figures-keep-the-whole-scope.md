# 2026-10-06 — Record's month figures keep the reader's whole scope

On Record, *Awaiting a record*'s Whose switch (*Mine* / *People I oversee*, decision 0258) narrows
that list, while the month's figures at the foot always covered the reader's whole scope, so a
reader on *Mine* saw their own list above figures for everyone they oversee. The owner chose this
version from both drawn in the app's own style, beside their Claude Design, whose one switch at the
top of Record narrows the figures to the reader's own Cell and the Sundays they file.

## The ruling

**1. The month's figures cover the reader's whole scope whichever list is shown**: everyone they
oversee, or the Whole Church for a reader holding `reports.view_subtree` there. The Whose switch
changes Awaiting a record and nothing else.

**2. The line above the figures says so in words**, for example *People you oversee, whichever
list is shown above* (*Whole Church, …* for a church-wide reader).

## Why

Every figure stays one Section 20 already defines. Figures for "my own Cells and the Sundays I file"
would be a new report scope needing its own definition first; the design's bars on those figures
are also refused for coverage (decisions 0224 and 0262).
