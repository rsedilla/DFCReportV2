# 2026-11-12 — A Network Admin records for a Person in the tree who holds an Admin account

Owner ruling, 2026-11-12, on a Stop Condition `architecture-guardian` raised while a Network
Admin's Growth powers were built (decision 0469, point 5). It amends Section 7.

## The ruling

A Person who has a pastoral leader and also holds an `ADMIN` account is, for attendance and
Growth, recorded like anybody else in that leader's care. **A Network Admin records and
corrects their DCC line, their SUYNL lessons and their Training graduations on that leader's
behalf.** Editing, archiving, restoring and moving that Person stay refused, as does adding
them to or removing them from a Cell directly (decision 0473).

A Network root is untouched by this, whatever account the root holds.

## Why

Section 7 says such a Person "is read and never changed" and lists edit, archive, restore and
move. Decision 0469 says a Network Admin does not change the whole-church Admin's record. Neither
said whether a DCC line or a Growth record *about* that Person is one of their records.

The owner was shown both answers. These are records the Person's leader owes, and stepping in
for a leader is what the role is for; refusing them would leave one line of that leader's
checklist to the whole-church Admin alone.

Considered and rejected: refusing them through the same check that refuses an edit, which
keeps one rule for every act and splits a leader's checklist between two administrators.

## What is owed

Nothing. The recording and Growth powers were built this way, and a test now pins recording
a DCC line, a lesson and a graduation for such a Person.
