# 2026-11-08 — A journey table heads its stages short at every width

Decision 0451 shortened a journey table's headings on a phone, *2nd · 3rd · 4th · Reg*, and kept
*2nd Timer*, *3rd Timer*, *4th Timer* and *Regular* from `lg`. The owner asked for shorter headings
on a laptop, considered *2T / 3T / 4T*, and chose the words the phone already uses.

## The ruling

**Every table with a column per journey stage heads them *VIP · 2nd · 3rd · 4th · Reg*, at every
width.** A screen reader still hears the full name. *Total* and *People* stay as they are. A stage
named outside a column heading, on a person's page, on a count box or in a sentence, keeps its full
name.

## Why

*2nd* can be read without being learned, where *2T* needs a key under the table, and one set of
headings serves every screen.
