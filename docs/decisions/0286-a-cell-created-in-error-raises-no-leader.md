# 2026-09-24 — A Cell created in error raises no leader

Decision 0283 took a Cell closed `CREATED_IN_ERROR` out of its leader's Open a cell. It said
nothing of Raise 12 leaders, where leading that Cell made the leader qualify (Section 11)
while it lasted, and so counted toward their own pastoral leader's twelve.

The owner chose this answer from a drawing of both.

## The ruling

**Leading a Cell closed `CREATED_IN_ERROR` does not count toward Raise 12 leaders.** The
periods `cells` returns for decision 0285 leave such a Cell out, so a derived goal reached
through it moves to the first instant twelve counted without it, or is not reached.

## Why

The closure says the Cell should never have existed. Counting it for the upline while not
counting it for the leader would have two goals disagree about one Cell.
