# 2026-09-26 — The Encounter is Life Class lesson 5

Section 28 set the pathway as SUYNL, then the Encounter, then Life Class, and called the
Encounter the first of five schools with Life Class running to ten lessons. The owner corrected
it, and named each step.

## The ruling

**The pathway is SUYNL (Start Up Your New Life), then the LC Party (Life Class Party), then
Life Class, then SOL 1, SOL 2 and SOL 3 (School of Leaders 1 to 3).** The LC Party comes before
Life Class lesson 1 and is not itself a lesson.

**The Encounter is lesson 5 of Life Class**: a three-day weekend held in the first week of
April, August and December. Life Class has nine lessons, the fifth being the Encounter. So
there are four schools, Life Class and SOL 1 to 3, with the Encounter inside Life Class.

**Somebody may join at the LC Party before finishing SUYNL**, where their leader judges them
ready. The order stays displayed and never enforced.

**Nothing recorded changes.** The Encounter is still recorded as its own graduation, and it
still comes first of the five, because it is attended before Life Class is completed.

## Not settled here

Which dates "the first week" of those months means. A view counting who is ready for an
Encounter needs it, and this ruling does not.
