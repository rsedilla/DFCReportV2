# 2026-11-11 — A Network Admin moves people into and out of a root's 12

Owner ruling, 2026-11-11, on a reading made while building a Network Admin's people powers
(decision 0469, point 4). It amends Section 7.

## The ruling

A Network Admin moves a person to their Network's root, and moves a person away from that
root to another leader of the Network. Section 7's "no move reaches" a root means the root
is never the person moved.

## Why

Section 7 says a Network root "is read and never changed" and that no move reaches them. A
move names three people: the person moved, the leader they leave and the leader they join.
The sentence did not say which of the three it meant.

The owner was shown both readings. Under the other, every move where either leader is the
root would go to the whole-church Admin. The owner chose this one, knowing that moving one of
the root's 12 carries their branch with them and that the application does not record the
root's agreement, as it records nobody's (decision 0469, point 4).

## What is owed

Nothing. The people powers were built on this reading, with a test that moves a person to
the root and away again.
