# 2026-10-11 — Every Senior Pastor tab lists the same Primaries

Owner ruling, 2026-10-11, on an item `architecture-guardian` raised on decision 0327's build. It
amends Section 19.

## The ruling

**On all five of the Senior Pastors' `Reports` tabs, a root's table lists that root's direct
disciples on Section 20's placement graph for the period.** The # tabs already did; CG attendance
and DCC attendance took the assignments in force at the period's end instead. They differed
for a Primary whose assignment ended within the period and who held none at its end, for example
one archived mid-month: that Primary was a row on the # tabs and not on the attendance tables,
whose figures for the people under them fell into the line under both tables. Now that Primary
keeps a row on every tab for that period, carrying the people they led. Totals are unchanged.

## Why

The owner compared the two on the real screen and chose one list of Primaries for every tab, so a
month never shows a Primary on one tab and not on the next. Decision 0326 already said every tab
placed the rows this way; the attendance tables now do what it said.
