# 2026-09-23 — Growth records only current people

Sections 27 and 28 specify three write surfaces and three per-person tables and never
mention lifecycle. `CLAUDE.md` carried that as a Stop Condition owed by the write endpoint,
which is now being built.

The owner chose this answer from a drawing of the SUYNL tab with a person who has left.

## The ruling

**A Person who is archived, or whose record was absorbed by a merge, is not listed on the
`SUYNL`, `Training` or `Conquest` tab and is given no row.** A write naming one is refused,
a correction included. An archived Person is restored to `CURRENT` first, as Section 5
requires before a reassignment; a merge is irreversible, so a new record names the surviving
Person (Section 5). Lifecycle is read as things stand now, as the tabs are (decision 0278).

**What they already have is kept.** Their rows stay on their own record, and a goal a leader
reached with them stays reached (Section 27).

**The counts at the head of each tab count current people only**, since each counts exactly
the people the tab lists.

## Why

**It is the answer every neighbouring write already gives.** Section 5 refuses an archived
Person as an assignment target, Section 10 as a Cell member, Section 13 as a facilitator, and
DCC recording drops both from its roster and refuses both on submission. An archived Person
does not acquire new live relationships (Section 10), and a lesson ticked for somebody who
has left is a record of nothing.

## What it does not decide

**Whether an absorbed record's rows count toward the survivor.** An absorbed record is no
longer listed, so a merged pair is never counted twice; whether the survivor inherits the
absorbed record's lessons, graduations and confirmations is Person Merge's question, still
recorded as open in `CLAUDE.md`.
