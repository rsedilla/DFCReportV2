# 2026-10-14 — Three things shown twice are removed

Owner ruling, 2026-10-14, from screenshots of the live screens. Each removes something the
application already shows elsewhere. It amends Section 19 and decisions 0290 and 0316.

## The ruling

**1. Record carries no month figures.** The row at the foot of Record (Cell meetings
recorded, DCC records filed, People at a Cell, People at DCC, each with last month's) is
removed. Reports' CG Attendance and DCC Attendance carry the same figures, last month by its
arrow. This withdraws decision 0316's rule that those figures keep the reader's whole scope.

**2. Reports has no "How these are counted" dialog.** Its terms stay defined in this
specification.

**3. The Cells page has no "People without a Cell" button, and its page is withdrawn.**
Record's *Awaiting CG Assignment* reads the same list from the same route. A person is added to
a Cell from their own page.

## Why

The owner judged each a second copy of something a leader already has, and fewer things on a
screen are easier to read. What goes with them, accepted: the in-app explanation of the terms,
and adding somebody to a Cell straight from the list.
