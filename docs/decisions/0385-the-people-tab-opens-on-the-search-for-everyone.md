# 2026-10-23 — The People tab opens on the search for everyone

Decision 0348 opened the People tab on the search alone for an account on the Full view or
Senior Pastor screens, and left a leader on the recording screens decision 0259's list of
everyone they oversee, ten a page. On 23 Oct the owner looked at that list on a leader's account
and said it served no purpose on a tab called *Add People*, then chose this from real
screenshots.

## The ruling

**The People tab opens on the search alone, for every account**, with "Search by name or
Member ID to find someone." and the heading line "Find someone by name or Member ID." Nothing
is listed, and nothing is asked of the server, until a search; the table then shows what the
search finds, limited to the searcher's scope as before. *Add* is offered as decision 0350
says.

This extends decision 0348 to the recording screens and replaces the opening list of decision
0259. The route is unchanged: with no term it still lists the actor's own scope, and nothing on
the screen asks it to.

## Why

The tab is for finding or adding one person. A leader reaches their own people on the Cells,
SUYNL and Record screens, and finds anyone else in their branch by name or Member ID, so the list
duplicated those screens and put personal details on the screen before anyone asked for them.
One behaviour for every account is also one fewer thing to explain at the pilot. Decision
0348 had rejected removing the list for everyone, because it served the leaders with few
people; the owner has now judged that it does not.

Who may see whom is unchanged.
