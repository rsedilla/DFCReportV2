# 2026-10-29 — Conquest is a link above the tabs on Reports and Growth

Owner ruling, 2026-10-29, chosen from screenshots of the Admin's Reports and Growth on the demo
database, today beside the proposal.

## The ruling

**1. Conquest is no longer a tab of `Reports` or of `Growth`.** Each tab row loses it: `Reports`
carries five tabs for a whole-church reader (Cell Groups, DCC, *Encounter Readiness*, Training and
*# of People*), equal, three across on a phone and one row from a small screen up, and `Growth`
carries SUYNL and Training.

**2. A link reading "Conquest goals →" sits at the right, just above each tab row**, and opens the
same page it did: `/reports/conquest` from Reports, `/growth/conquest` from Growth. On the Conquest
page the link reads "Conquest goals", is marked as the current page, and no tab is; each tab leads
back.

**3. It is shown to whoever the tab was shown to**: on Reports a whole-church reader, the Admin; on
Growth an account whose Growth carries Training. The Senior Pastors, a Full view leader and a
Leader account without Full view had no Conquest tab and are shown no link (decisions 0326, 0360,
0379 and 0380).

## What it does not change

The two Conquest pages, their addresses, their content and who may open them. No capability,
route or record changes. A Senior Pastor still has no Conquest, which decision 0326 settled.
