# 2026-10-17 — Update Records and Recording Progress

Record's first tab was *Update Record*, and its pane opened with a heading repeating that name and
the line *Still to record this month, oldest first*. Its last tab, for a Full view leader and the
Senior Pastors, was *Recording Status* (decisions 0325 and 0334). On 17 Oct the owner, from real
screenshots, asked for the heading to go as redundant and for the tab to say *Records*, as the
two Pending tabs do (decision 0358), and chose *Recording Progress* to sound positive.

## The ruling

1. **Record's first tab is *Update Records*.** Every place that names it, such as a recording
   screen's *Back to Update Records*, says the same.
2. **Where the tabs name it, its pane carries no heading** repeating the name and no line under
   it. A Leader account without Full view has no tab row (decision 0323), so its list keeps the
   heading, renamed *Update Records*, and its line.
3. **The tab that was *Recording Status* is *Recording Progress***, for a Full view leader and
   for the Senior Pastors. What it shows is unchanged.
4. ***Awaiting Leader Assignment* and *Awaiting CG Assignment* keep their names.** Those lists
   hold people waiting to be placed rather than records, so *Records* would misname them. The
   other four panes keep their headings.

## Why

The tab already names the list, so the heading said it twice. The rows still show when each
one closes, so dropping the line loses nothing. *Records* matches the two Pending tabs.
*Progress* reads as how far recording has come rather than as a verdict.
