# 2026-10-23 — SUYNL's Graduated card is hidden

The SUYNL tab carried three cards, Not started, In progress and Graduated, adding up to everyone
listed (decision 0281), and its list already opened on the people still to finish (decision
0287). On 23 Oct the owner said SUYNL's purpose is getting people through the ten lessons; once
someone graduates they go on to Equipping, Life Class to SOL 3, so the Graduated card had no
purpose on the tab. From real screenshots the same day the owner also removed the line counting
those who have finished.

## The ruling

1. **SUYNL shows two cards, Not started and In progress**, which add up to everyone still to
   finish. Each narrows the list to its people, as before.
2. **The list says nothing of those who have finished.** Decision 0287's line ("1 who has
   finished all ten is not shown") and its Show everyone button are gone from SUYNL, so the tab
   is about who still has lessons to take.
3. **Graduated is hidden, not withdrawn.** A graduate is found by a search, which lists them
   with their lessons, so a lesson ticked in error can still be corrected; an address naming
   the graduated step, or everyone, still answers.
4. **Nothing else changes.** The counts route still returns how many have graduated;
   graduates' lessons stay recorded, and Encounter Readiness and Conquest's Win 3 read them as
   before. Training keeps its cards, its line and its Show everyone.

## Why

A card is a filter, and the one filter a leader using SUYNL needs is who still has lessons to
take. A correction is rare, and the leader making one knows whose, so a search serves it. The graduates' records matter elsewhere, which is why they stay; hiding the card saves the
server nothing, the three counts being one request either way.
