# 2026-10-28 — One Cells total, and your Cells first

Owner ruling, 2026-10-28, chosen from real screenshots of the Cells page: the two totals today,
three ways to show one total (a tinted band, a green bar, a headline over the table), and the
table's columns as they are and tidied.

## The ruling

**1. One total, information only.** *Active Cells* is headed by how many Cells the reader
oversees, as of today, as the title of the list (*26 Cells*), with how many of them they lead
beside it (*1 you lead · 25 under your leaders · as of today*). It is a title, not a button, and
filters nothing. It replaces decision 0289's two cards, *Cells you lead* and *Cells you oversee*,
each of which was also a filter. The two cards read as though they added up (1 and 26 making 27)
when the second already counted the first. A whole-church reader's reads *others in the church*
where a leader's reads *under your leaders*, and *Whole Church* where they lead none.

**2. The reader's own Cells first, and marked.** The running Cells come with the reader's own
first, then the rest, each part in Cell ID order, so they head the first page however many Cells
the reader oversees. Each is tinted with a green bar beside it and marked *You lead* in words,
so the tint is never the only sign (Section 23). This is what pressing *Cells you lead* was for,
and it needs no press. Putting the reader's own first ranks nobody (decision 0009). The *No
meeting in 3 months* list and the closed view keep Cell ID order, and *Show only Cells I led*
stays on *Closed Cells*.

**3. The table's columns.** Each column has a fixed share (Cell and Leader 27% each, Members 11%,
Meets 19%, Recorded 16%), and *Members* and *Recorded* are right-aligned under right-aligned
headings so their figures line up. The page keeps its width (option A); using more of a wide
screen is a separate question, for every page at once.

**4. Not changed.** The SUYNL, Training and Conquest cards stay as they are: each filters its
list, and there that is the only way to narrow it. The API keeps `?led_by=me` and the totals
route, which the screen now reads for the headline (Section 22 keeps `/api/v1` additive).
