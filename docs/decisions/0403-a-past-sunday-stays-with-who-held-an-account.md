# 2026-10-28 — A past Sunday stays with whoever held an account that Sunday

Owner ruling, 2026-10-28, chosen from screenshots on the demo database of a leader's checklist for
Sunday 25 October today beside the proposal, after the leader beneath was given an account on
Wednesday 28 October.

## The ruling

**1. Who holds an account is read as of the Sunday, everywhere a DCC checklist is decided.** A
person's DCC line belongs to their nearest leader above who held an account on that Sunday (Section
9). Recording, correcting, Record's *Update Records* and *Pending DCC Records*, and the Senior
Pastors' *Recording Progress* all read it that way. *Recording Progress* already did (decision
0325); recording and Record's lists read it now (decision 0336), and this replaces that.

**2. A new account starts with the next Sunday.** A leader given an account owes the Sundays from
the moment the account was created, whether or not it has been activated yet. A Sunday before it
stays with the leader above who covered it, who records and corrects it until its week closes. The
new account holder's checklist for that Sunday is empty, and it is not on their *Update Records*.

**3. Why.** The leader who covered a Sunday is the one who knows who came. Read now, giving a
leader an account moved every open Sunday from before it onto them, so the covering leader could no
longer record what they knew, and the new account holder was asked about Sundays from before they
could sign in. And it makes the checklist agree with *Recording Progress*, which already counted
those Sundays against the covering leader.

**4. The screen.** The section of a checklist holding a leader's people now reads "had no account
on this Sunday, so you record them".

**5. What is unchanged.** The tree is still walked as of the Sunday, a closed week is still
amended only at Whole Church, and SUYNL, which is not per Sunday, still follows whoever records a
person's DCC now (decision 0322).
