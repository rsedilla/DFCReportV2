# 2026-10-22 — "Only my disciples" is offered where it narrows the list

The SUYNL and Training lists show everyone the reader's viewing grant reaches, at every depth,
and "Only my disciples" narrows them to the reader's own direct disciples (decision 0287). For a
leader whose people are all direct disciples, the toggle shows the same list it was pressed on.
On 22 Oct the owner looked at real screenshots of a Cell Leader's SUYNL page and ruled that it
should not be offered there.

## The ruling

1. **The SUYNL and Training counts also give `beyond_direct_disciples`**: how many of the
   people the tab lists are other than the reader, whom their own grant lists, and the
   reader's direct disciples, as things stand now. It is counted as the tab's `people` is, over
   the same population, so it never exceeds it. It is not a card and filters nothing.
2. **The screen leaves the toggle out where `beyond_direct_disciples` is zero**, since the toggle
   would then take away nobody but the reader. It shows it where someone listed is neither the
   reader nor a direct disciple, where the counts could not be read, and while the toggle is on,
   so it can be turned off.
3. **The list's own `mine` filter is unchanged**, and an address asking for it still answers.

Conquest's toggle is unchanged.

## Why

A control that changes nothing tells the reader there is something to narrow. The list is paged,
so the screen cannot tell from the rows it holds whether someone other than a direct disciple is
on a later page or
among those already finished; the server, which counts the whole list, can. A number beside the
total is one more figure in an answer the page already reads, and a client that does not know it
ignores it (Section 22).

**It grants nothing.** The figure counts people the reader already sees on the same tab, under the
same capability and scope, and the toggle is a filter over that list. No capability, scope or
population changes.
