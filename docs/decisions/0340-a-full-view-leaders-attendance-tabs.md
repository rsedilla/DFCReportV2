# 2026-10-13 — A Full view leader's attendance tabs, and Filed Reports withdrawn

Owner ruling, 2026-10-13, chosen one question at a time from drawings and real previews of a
Primary's screens on the demo. It is for the direct 12 of the two Senior Pastors and everyone
else given Full view (decision 0323). It amends Sections 15 and 19 and decisions 0254, 0292, 0293,
0298 and 0314.

## The ruling

**1. A Full view leader's `Reports` has four tabs: CG Attendance, DCC Attendance, SUYNL and
Conquest.** Training is not among them. The Senior Pastors' # of Cell Groups, # of Cell Leaders
and # of People are not given to them. A reader holding `reports.view_subtree` short of Whole
Church whose screens are not the Senior Pastors' is treated as a Full view leader here, keyed on
the capability and never on a role (decision 0323, point 5).

**2. CG Attendance and DCC Attendance are laid out as the Senior Pastors' (decision 0326), for
the leader's own branch.** They show:
- the coverage line, first, as Section 12 requires of a leader's figures;
- the branch's figures as cards: Total, VIP, 2nd Timer, 3rd Timer, 4th Timer, Regular;
- the leader's 12, Total first, then their own row, every other row shaded as theirs is;
- decision 0293's two lines under the rows, so Total adds up in plain sight.

Figures for, the periods and the Year table are as before.

**3. Filed Reports is withdrawn for every account** (owner): who is behind is on Record's
Recording Status (decisions 0325, 0334 and 0335) and its Pending lists. Every way to it goes
with it:
- the coverage line's link;
- the Branch screen's Still to record figures (decision 0298), which become plain numbers;
- Record's "See every Cell behind in Reports" (decision 0314).

A whole-church reader keeps Cell Groups, DCC, SUYNL, Training and Conquest. The Senior Pastors'
five tabs do not change.

**4. Record's Pending CG Reports reads the whole branch, every page** (owner), so it names every
Cell behind and its count is exact. This settles the open question of what completeness that
list owes: it is paged until it is exhausted.

## Why

The owner wants a Full view leader to read their branch the way the Senior Pastors read the
church. Filed Reports showed, Cell by Cell and Sunday by Sunday, what Recording Status now shows
by leader and by week. What goes with it is a look back at a closed month Cell by Cell. The coverage line stays for a leader because
Section 12 makes it lead; only the Senior Pastors were exempted, by decision 0327.
