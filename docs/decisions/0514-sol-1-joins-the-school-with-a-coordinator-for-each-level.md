# 2026-11-24 — SOL 1 joins the school, with a Coordinator for each level

Owner rulings of 2026-11-24, each made from screenshots of the real app, today's
beside the proposal, and most after a comparison with Google Classroom, Canvas, Blackboard,
Moodle, PeopleSoft, Banner, Workday and UP's CRS that the owner asked for. Decision 0501 ruled
SOL 1 to 3 (points 17 to 19) and the open question of how a level is bound to
`equipping.coordinate` waited for SOL 1. This settles that question and every SOL 1 screen. **It
is built in four steps**, each its own pull request: points 1 to 7 first, then the register
(points 8 to 10), then final grades and Training (points 11 to 13), then the leader's tab (point
14).

## The Coordinator

**1. One tick per level, and a level may have several Coordinators** (owner, 2026-11-24, "A, yes"
and "several allowed", on the Stop Condition `architecture-guardian` raised on decision 0501).
The Account view carries *Life Class Coordinator* and *SOL 1 Coordinator*, each offered only with
Full view, each the Admin's to give, each audited. One person may hold both. Decision 0501's
"each has one" is the usual case, not a limit. **The level is carried on the tick**: the tick
grants the one `equipping.coordinate`, and the school refuses a class of a level the account does
not tick. Of the two answers the open question named, this is the second; the first, a capability
per level, would need the guard to know a class's level before reading it. The Admin keeps every
Coordinator step at every level (decision 0501, point 2). A class of another level answers
`NOT_FOUND`, as one that does not exist does, so the refusal says nothing of another level's
classes; a list or an opening that names another level answers `SCOPE_DENIED`.

**2. Who coordinates is shown** (owner, 2026-11-24, "Yes, both", from screenshots, after Google
Admin's roles, Canvas's sub-account admins and Moodle). A *Coordinators* list heads the Admin's
Accounts tab, above the search: one row a level the school runs, each name linking to that
person's Account view, *None yet* where nobody is ticked, and the line *Who opens each level's
classes. Change it on the person's Account view.* A Coordinator's *Classes* names the level's
others: *Life Class, which you coordinate with Teofilo Ramos.*

## Classes, registration and enrollment

**3. A Level picker beside the Term** (owner, 2026-11-24, after Primer, eBay and Trimble's
guidance that two to four choices switching a page are a row of buttons). *Registration*,
*Enrollment* and *Classes* carry the same picker. On *Registration* each level shows how many are
registered (*Life Class · 14*) and the button reads *Register for SOL 1*; on *Classes* it lists
only the levels the reader coordinates and is not drawn for one.

**4. A SOL 1 class names no Encounter weekend.** SOL has no Encounter (decision 0501, point 17), so
its class line reads *10 lessons*, and nobody joins one at Lesson 6, which is Life Class's alone
(decision 0512).

**5. Registering for SOL 1 reads three requirements** (owner, 2026-11-24, after Banner's and
Workday's prerequisite overrides). *Life Class passed*, a posted pass of the school or one
recorded before it, is read from the records, and so is the second.

**The second is Win 3, or three disciples** (owner rulings of 2026-11-25, "OR", from screenshots,
amending decision 0501 point 18, which named Win 3 alone). It is met by *Win 3* as Conquest derives
it (Section 27), **or** by three direct disciples under the person in the pastoral tree now, counted
on open pastoral assignments, so a disciple archived, merged away or moved to another leader is not
one. Like every requirement it is read once, at registration. A person with three people placed
under them, whose lessons nobody has recorded yet, is met without the Registrar writing a reason.
**Win 3 itself is unchanged**: Conquest's goal keeps its meaning, and the second arm belongs to SOL
1's entry alone. The row reads *Win 3, or 3 disciples*: met by Win 3, *Win 3 reached*; met by the
disciples, *3 disciples under them in DREAMS*; neither, *Not met · needs Win 3, or 3 disciples under
them in DREAMS*.

**Neither count is shown** (owner ruling of 2026-11-25). The Registrar is never told how many of the
person's disciples carry three SUYNL lessons, a Conquest figure shown to those who hold
`conquest.view_subtree` (Section 27), nor how many disciples the person has: the form needs only
whether the requirement is met, so it is given nothing more. Conquest hands the school whether Win
3 is reached and nothing else. Win 3's words say any three lessons, as Sections 27 and 28 count them
(owner, 2026-11-25, "Any 3 lessons", over a first draft naming lessons 1 to 3, which would have
stated a rule those sections do not).

*Has the SOL 1 Manual* is the third requirement, ticked by the Registrar in its own row and kept
with the day it was ticked; left unticked it is a requirement not met, so the header counts it
(*1 of 3 not met*). There is no Bible tick. A requirement not met warns and the reason is required,
as decision 0501 point 18 rules, and the Registration list names it: *Not met: Win 3, or 3
disciples. Why: …*. The top line reads *the SOL 1 manual is the tuition, paid outside DREAMS*.

**6. A birthday stops nobody enrolling in SOL 1** (owner, 2026-11-24, approving the screenshot
that drops it). Life Class needs it for the age at the Encounter; SOL has no Encounter, so a SOL 1
class's list has no *Age at the Encounter* column and no *Can't enroll yet: No birthday
recorded*. Each class reads *1 of 30 enrolled*, after UP's CRS showing slots against total slots,
and *Ready to enroll* says how many are waiting.

**7. A SOL 1 Guide has passed SOL 2, up to 5 a Guide** (decision 0501, points 4 and 8, built here):
the Guide search lists only those who qualify, each saying which level they passed.

## The register

**8. The SOL 1 Teacher's register** (owner, 2026-11-24, "Yes, r4 approved", after Google
Classroom, Canvas Roll Call, Blackboard and Moodle). Tabs *L1* to *L10* and a tab *Quiz and book*
after them. Each lesson's list ticks *Came*, *Memory verse* and *Devotional*, each with a box that
ticks everyone, the line reading *Tick who came, who recited the memory verse, and who handed in
the devotional. Anyone not ticked as came is saved Absent.* On a phone each student's card carries
the two ticks under the name, and the save bar says only who came and who is absent. *Quiz and
book* takes the quiz's number of items once, each try's score with every score kept, the result in
words (*✓ Passed*, *Not yet passed · 2 retakes left*), *Book read*, and *Add a retake*. *Record so
far* has *L1* to *L10*, *Verses* and *Devotionals* counted out of ten, *Quiz*, *Book* and *Still
missing*.

**The Teacher records and corrects the quiz and the book until final grades are posted** (owner,
2026-11-26, "Until posting, fixable"), as make-ups are; a corrected score keeps the score it
replaces, and after posting only the Admin changes anything. **A retake follows a failed try**
(owner, 2026-11-26, "Only after a failed try"): *Add a retake* is offered only while the last try
is under 70%, never past the third try, and none after a pass. **Any try's score may be
corrected** (owner, 2026-11-26, "Yes, all three", from screenshots, as Canvas adjusts any single
attempt): each try carries its own *Correct*, and the corrected try shows the score it was
corrected from, by whom and when. A try corrected to a pass leaves every later try **kept and not
counted**, struck through with *Not counted: try N passed. Kept in history.*, so no later try
rests on a retake the corrected score never allowed; a later correction back to a fail leaves
those tries as they are, and the Teacher enters the retake again. **The number of items is
entered once and fixed once the first score is recorded** (owner, 2026-11-26, the same answer, as
Moodle locks a quiz's questions once it has attempts): until then it may be put right, and after
it every score is read against it.

**9. An absent student's verse and devotional are ticked at the make-up** (owner, 2026-11-24,
"A"). The lesson's list reads *At make-up* in their place, and *Record make-up* carries the two
ticks for the lesson made up.

**10. A verse or devotional brought late is ticked on a card** (owner, 2026-11-24, "C still owed
card", and the revision approved the same day). *Verses and devotionals still owed* sits under
*Make-ups still owed*: one row a student, what they owe, *Cell leader … · owed since …*, and *Tick
what is brought*, which asks what was brought and the day. The Teacher ticks there until final
grades are posted. An absent student is listed only once their make-up is recorded. A late tick
counts as an on-time one and carries no *late* mark, a checklist grade having no points to take
off.
**A late tick made by mistake is unticked** (owner, 2026-11-26, "Yes, all three", from
screenshots, as Google Classroom returns a turned-in piece of work): the card carries *Brought
late*, one row a lesson's verse or devotional ticked there, with the day it was brought and who
ticked it, and *Untick*. The Teacher unticks until final grades are posted; the tick is closed and
kept, and what it covered is owed again. The card shows while anything is owed or brought late.

## Final grades and Training

**11. SOL 1's final grades show the checklist** (owner, 2026-11-24, "B, five checks", after
PeopleSoft's grade roster, UP's CRS, Canvas and Moodle's completion report). Beside the grade and
*Why*: *Attendance* (*10 of 10 · 1 made up*, or *4 absences*), *Verses* and *Devotionals* out of
ten, *Quiz* (*75% ✓*, or *65% · 3 tries*) and *Book*, a failing value named in *Why* as well. The
lesson-by-lesson register stays on the Teacher's page, and there is no *Not yet* filter.

**12. Anything owed when the class is posted is Not passed** (owner, 2026-11-24, "Keep Not
passed", declining a grade like UP's *INC*). *Why* names it and the level is taken again as a 2nd
try (decision 0511); a tick wrongly missed is the Admin's change after posting.

**Every check not met is named in *Why*, the absences first** (owner, 2026-11-26, "B, name everything",
from screenshots compared with Google Classroom's missing work, Canvas's module progress,
Moodle's completion report, PeopleSoft's grade roster and degree audits): a student over the
absence limit reads, for example, *4 absences, over the 3 allowed. L3 and L8 not made up. L9
verse and the book missing.*, so each value Final Grades shows in red has its reason in *Why*.
Life Class keeps its single reason.

**The Admin changes a SOL grade after posting, as Life Class's is changed** (owner, 2026-11-26,
"Yes, build it", from screenshots compared with Google Classroom's grade history, Moodle's grade
override, Canvas's final grade override, PeopleSoft's grade change request and UP's Form 13-D):
the same dialog, with *What to change* first (a lesson's attendance, its memory verse, its
devotional, a quiz score, or *Book read*), what the grade becomes shown before saving, then a
summary to confirm, and each change kept in the grade's history. **Recorded wrongly is its only
reason** (owner, 2026-11-26, "Recorded wrongly only"): a change after posting corrects what was
done on or before the day the class was posted and missed in the record, so a make-up or a day
handed in falls on or before that day, and what was still owed when the class was posted is a
retake, as above. **Only a quiz try already recorded is corrected** (owner, 2026-11-26, "No,
correct only", compared with Canvas and Moodle, where a missing attempt is a new attempt the
student takes): a try not recorded before posting carries no day to check against it and is a
retake. **A correction to a lesson's attendance keeps its verse and devotional as recorded**
(owner, 2026-11-26, "keep them", as Moodle leaves other grade items as they are when one is
changed): a mistake in attendance does not make them wrong too, and one that was is its own
change. Only *Absent* clears them, a lesson not done counting neither. A grade made Passed files SOL 1 *From the school*, dated that day; one changed away from
Passed withdraws it.

**13. Once SOL 1's first class opens, only the Admin ticks SOL 1 before the school** (owner,
2026-11-24, "Admin only"), as decision 0509 gave the Encounter and Life Class, with a reason; a
posted Passed files SOL 1 *From the school*. The note above the Training table names the levels the
reader still ticks. **A class opens when a Coordinator opens it** (owner, 2026-11-26, "When a class
opens", asked between that and its first lesson): the moment a SOL 1 class exists, before any
lesson, leaders stop ticking SOL 1, as a university's registrar alone records credit for a course
it teaches. A class cancelled before it began (decision 0510) never opened. And as 0509 says of
the Encounter and Life Class, **nobody changes a SOL 1 the school filed**, the Admin included.

## The leader's tab

**14. A leader's SOL 1 card** (owner, 2026-11-24, after Canvas Parent, Khan Academy's parent
dashboard and Google Classroom's guardian summaries). Under the name, in bold, *Still missing: …*
or *Nothing missing so far*; then the class, Teacher and Guide, the *L1* to *L10* strip, and
*Verses N of 10 · Devotionals N of 10 · Quiz passed* (or *not yet passed*) *· Book read*. **The
quiz score is never shown to the leader**, as Google leaves grades out of a guardian's summary.

## What this step builds

Points 1 to 7: migration `0060_equipping_sol_1.sql` (the SOL 1 tick, needing Full view; a class or
registration of SOL 1; a SOL 1 class naming no Encounter weekend; each level's own requirement
codes, and a SOL 1 manual left unticked named in them, all database constraints); the tick, the
Coordinators list and the levels a Coordinator holds; the level gate on every Coordinator route and
on the Coordinator's view of a register; SOL 1's requirements, read through Conquest and Training;
and the three screens.

Points 8 to 10, the second step: migration `0061_equipping_sol_register.sql` (a SOL class's ten
lessons, held to its level by a trigger; the memory verse and devotional on a lesson's mark for
whoever came and on a make-up, held to the level by a trigger; a verse or devotional brought late;
the quiz's number of items, each try's score and *Book read*, each kept when corrected and never
deleted); the register's tabs, ticks, make-up ticks, *Verses and devotionals still owed* with
*Brought late* and *Untick*, *Quiz and book* with a *Correct* on every try, and *Record so far*; a SOL class among a Teacher's and a Guide's classes; and the lesson line
on the Coordinator's *Classes*. The card lists what is owed from a lesson whose week has closed, or
from a make-up recorded without it; while a lesson's week is open its own tab is where the two are
ticked.

Points 11 to 13, the third step: migration `0062_equipping_sol_final_grades.sql` (a posted grade
counting up to ten lessons, Life Class held to nine and a SOL grade never *Not yet* nor joining at
Lesson 6, by a trigger); a SOL grade worked out from its checklist, *Why* naming each check not
met; checking and posting a SOL class as Life Class's are; a posted *Passed* filing SOL 1 *From the
school*; SOL 1 the school's in Training once a SOL 1 class opens; and *Final Grades* with a *Level*
picker and the five checks, with *Grades* on a SOL class on the Coordinator's *Classes*.

**The Admin's change of a SOL grade after posting** (point 12, as ruled on 2026-11-26): migration
`0063_equipping_sol_grade_changes.sql` (a grade change names its item and the value before and
after, a lesson up to 10, each level held to its shape by a trigger, SOL's to *Recorded wrongly*;
a posted SOL class's late ticks, quiz tries and *Book read* written only beside a change of the
grade); the change and its preview reaching each item; and the dialog with *What to change*.

Point 14 is ruled and not built; until it is, what Section 29 says of it describes no route, and a
SOL 1 class is left out of a leader's people in the school.
