# 2026-11-22 — A leader reads their people in the school through their People access

Owner rulings of 2026-11-22, made from real-app screenshots while the cards of decision 0501
(points 5 and 21) were being built. Point 1 settles the last open half of which capabilities
carry the school's people, a Stop Condition. Point 2 is the screen the owner chose, for a Full
view leader and for a leader without Full view alike ("Yes, build this").

**1. A leader reads their people in the school through `people.view_subtree`** (owner,
2026-11-22, "Their People access"). `GET /api/v1/equipping/my-people` declares that capability
against the actor, so a leader sees in Equipping exactly the people their People access
reaches, narrowed to those enrolled. **Enrolled is read as an enrollment that has not ended**,
which is this step's reading and not the owner's words: Section 29 says *enrolled this term*, and
the two agree only while no term's final grades have been posted, since nothing yet ends an
enrollment when a term does. The step that posts final grades owes it, by ending those
enrollments or by this read filtering by term. The reader themself is
left out, being nobody's student here. Every Leader holds the capability over their own branch,
with or without Full view, so no new capability is added and none is granted. Each person comes
with their class, its Teacher, their Guide and their register, read the way the class's own
register reads it (decision 0503), and is placed under the one of the reader's twelve they are
reached through, or marked one of the twelve. A person a wider grant reaches whom the reader's
own branch does not, which no Leader holds by default, is placed under nobody and listed under
*Others*. The other answer was a new capability, `equipping.view_subtree`, which would have let
the Admin switch the cards off for one account apart from their People access, at the cost of a
capability to keep and manage.

**2. The screen** (owner, 2026-11-22, from screenshots). A leader's *Equipping* is one level at
a time, a switch of *Life Class*, *SOL 1*, *SOL 2* and *SOL 3*, the app's view switch. Under
*Life Class* come the classes the reader teaches and guides, as decision 0503 has them, and
then *Your people in Life Class*, a card each: the name, the class and its Teacher, the Guide or
*No Guide yet*, the nine lessons with the Encounter in lesson 5's place, and one line such as
*1 of 2 came · L2 not made up yet*. The cards are grouped *Your 12* first and then each of the
twelve's people under that leader's name, each group in first-name order (decision 0359). A
level with nobody says so in words. The school runs Life Class alone today (decision 0499), so
*SOL 1* to *SOL 3* read empty until it runs them. **The item is drawn while the reader teaches
or guides a class or one of their people is enrolled**, as 0501 point 21 has it: for a leader
without Full view an *Equipping* item of its own after *SUYNL*, five on a phone's bar; for a
Full view leader a tab of *Growth*. A Senior Pastor's screens keep *Equipping* only while they
teach a class (decision 0503, point 6). Hiding it is the menu alone.
