# 2026-11-01 — One size for every control, with shorter names

Tabs, buttons, switches, radio choices, tables and search boxes had grown one screen at a
time: each tab sized to its words (decision 0329), a switch here as wide as its longest label
and there filling its row, a button as long as the sentence it carried, and a search box as
wide as whatever was left. The owner chose one look for each from screenshots on 2026-11-01.

## The ruling

**1. Every tab row has one tab size**, 11.5rem from a small screen up and two equal tabs to a
row on a phone, where a label may wrap only on the narrowest. It holds on every screen and for
every kind of account. This replaces decision 0329. Five tabs take shorter names, wherever the
list is named: *Awaiting Leader Assignment* is *No Leader Yet*, *Awaiting CG Assignment* is
*No CG Yet*, *Inactive (3+ Months) Cells* is *Inactive 3+ Months*, *Same Leader, Same Time* is
*Time Clashes*, and *Under 3 Member Cells* is *Under 3 Members*.

**2. A choice inside a tab is a pill**, so the two levels never look alike: a grey rounded
track, the chosen view a white pill with its words bold in the accent. The tabs above stay
boxed, the chosen one filled. Every segment is 8rem from a small screen up; on a phone the
segments share the row equally. *Last 12 months* reads *12 Months*, so it stays on one line.

**3. Every stacked radio choice is 11rem**, wrapping where the column is narrow, and full
width, one to a line, on a phone. A roster's Present and Absent rows are unchanged.

**4. Every button is at least 9rem wide from a small screen up and the full width on a
phone**, 44px tall as before. Each action has one short name, at most about three words, in
Title Case: *Cancel* for leaving without changing anything (*Keep*, *Keep them*, *Keep it
waiting*), *Back* for going back a step, *Save* for saving (*Save changes*, *Save username*,
*Save new password*), and *Add Person*, *Add Member*, *Add to Cell*, *Create Account*,
*Request New Cell*, *Change Leader*, *Change Cell*, *Change Schedule*, *Change*, *Edit*,
*Close Cell*, *Remove*, *Decline*, *Send Link*, *Send Reset Link*, *Sign Out Everywhere*,
*Reset Second Step*, *Save and Send* and *Show More* in place of the longer names, and the
email change's four (decision 0415) *Send Link*, *Confirm Email*, *Change Email* and *Start Over*. The second
sign-in step's last button reads *Continue to DREAMS*, the application's name since 2026-10-20.

**5. Every table is bold**: its header row solid accent with white words, and every other body
row the pale accent tint. On a phone most lists are cards, which do not change.

**6. There is one search bar**, after the USWDS search component: about thirty characters
wide from a small screen up and the full width on a phone, a magnifying glass inside the box
on the left, and the Search button joined to the box. Any other button, such as People's
*Add*, stays apart. The label stays above the box.

## Why

One size makes a screen read as one product, and a control the same size everywhere is
found where it was last time. Short names fit those sizes on a phone without wrapping, and
the two levels of choice, a tab and a view inside it, are told apart by shape rather than
by position alone. Colour still marks no meeting status, coverage figure or leader
(sections 13, 17 and 19): the tint on a table row alternates and grades nothing.
