# 2026-10-30 — A form is as wide as a list, and is set out in sections

Owner ruling, 2026-10-30, chosen from real screenshots of Add a person and of recording a Cell
meeting at 1920px, today beside the proposal, after the owner asked for the laptop's width to be
used and for the fields, buttons and labels to be easier to read. **It replaces point 2 of
decision 0402** for a form; one person's page stays at 1024px.

## The ruling

**1. A page that is filled in is up to 1280px wide**, as a list is. That is Add a person, Edit
details, New Cell, recording a Cell meeting and a DCC Sunday. It still starts 40px from the
sidebar, and below 1024px every page fills the screen as before.

**2. A form is set out in sections.** Each has a short title, and where it helps a line saying
what it is for, on the left, and its fields on the right; a rule divides one section from the
next. Two related fields sit side by side: sex beside civil status, birthday beside mobile
number, the leader beside the Cell Group, the day beside the time. The button sits under the
fields. On a phone each section is one column, its title above its fields.

**3. On a recording screen the two questions sit side by side in one box, and the people to mark
are cards in two columns**, on a Cell meeting and on a DCC Sunday.

**4. On these pages a label is in sentence case, in the text colour**, such as "Birthday
(required)", instead of small green capitals. Everywhere else keeps the label as it was.

**5. Account and Where they sit keep 768px.** Each is read from top to bottom and holds no form of
many fields, so the width would only make its lines longer.

## What it does not change

No field, rule, wording of a refusal or order of what is asked. The screens hold what they held,
in the same order within each section.
