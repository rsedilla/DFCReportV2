# 2026-11-01 — Tabs are 13rem, and a list's filters are pills and chips

Decision 0416 went live on 2026-11-01. A check of every main screen, as an administrator, a
Senior Pastor, a Primary and a recording-only leader on a laptop and a phone, found three
things it had made worse. The owner chose these fixes from screenshots the same day.

## The ruling

**1. Every tab is 13rem from a small screen up**, in place of decision 0416's 11.5rem. A
tab carrying a count, such as *Pending DCC Records* with its number, did not fit on one line
at 11.5rem and pushed the whole row to two. Every tab is still one size, two to a row on a
phone.

**2. A list's filter bar puts the search on a line of its own, and the filters on one row
under it.** That is the pattern of Shopify Polaris's index filters and Google Material's
filter chips. A choice of one from a few, such as Pending CG Records' and Pending DCC
Records' *Network* (All, Men's, Women's), is the same pill as decision 0416's choice inside a
tab. A filter that is on or off, such as *Late only* and SUYNL's *Only my disciples*, is a
chip: rounded, keeping the same name whether on or off, with a tick when on. On a phone each
takes the full width.

**3. Radio choices sit side by side where there is room** and wrap where there is not, as
decision 0416 said. Inside a control bar they had stacked one to a line.

**4. A search box sits beside the filters that follow it**, its box at most 30rem beside its
9rem button, so a chip such as *Only my disciples* stays on the same row.

## Why

These follow decision 0416's intent and correct where its numbers and its layout did not hold
on the real screens. A chip that keeps its name and shows a tick says what it filters whether
it is on or off. A button that renamed itself to *Showing late only* did not.
