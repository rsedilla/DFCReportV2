# 2026-09-25 — The Cells page carries its totals

The Record screen ended with two tiles, "Cells you lead" and "Cells in your scope", dated as
of today. Record is where a leader fills things in, and these are figures to read. Section
19 put figures on the Dashboard and in the reporting modules only.

The owner chose this answer from a clickable drawing of four places for them.

## The ruling

**The two totals sit at the top of the Cells page, and leave the Record screen.** Each is
dated as of today, and each is also the filter that shows those Cells: "Cells you lead" shows
your own, "Cells in your scope" shows them all. They count every page of running Cells,
since the list returns no total.

## Why

A total is easiest to read above the list it counts, and pressing it shows the Cells behind
it. Reports was considered and not taken, because these are counts of things that exist
today rather than figures for a period.
