# 2026-10-18 — The Primaries' report controls, and no coverage line

A Full view leader's CG and DCC Attendance Reports had their period buttons above a grey bar, the
month's arrows and an *Open until* tag in it, *Figures for* at its right, then a coverage line
(*5 of 30 records filed in the month*) and *4 Sundays counted*, then *Your branch* above the cards
and *Your 12* above the table (decisions 0293, 0294 and 0340). On 17 Oct the owner said these
looked detached and could confuse a leader, and on 18 Oct chose the layout below from mocks and then real
screenshots.

## The ruling

1. **Every control is in one panel.** *Show* labels the four periods. The period sits between its
   two arrows, labelled *Week*, *Month*, *Quarter* or *Year*, with *Figures for* beside it at the
   same height.
2. **One plain note sits under the controls** instead of the tag. On DCC it gives the Sundays
   counted, any Sunday with no service (Section 9), and whether the period is still open, for
   example *4 Sundays. Still open, until Mon 16 Nov, 12 noon.* On Cell Groups it says only
   whether the period is open.
3. **There is no coverage line.** The owner ruled this, extending decision 0327 from the Senior
   Pastors to a Full view leader, on the same ground: they read whether their people have
   recorded on Record's *Recording Progress*, which they have had since decision 0334. Section
   12's coverage-first rule keeps every other reader's line.
4. **The cards are headed *Your Disciples***, or *[name]'s Disciples* for an opened leader, which
   still says whose figures they are. **The table has no visible label**: its Leader column says
   what the rows are, and its caption keeps its name for a screen reader (Section 23).
5. Every figure, every period's reach (decision 0310) and every route are unchanged. A whole-church
   reader and the Senior Pastors keep their layouts.

## Why

The controls answer one question, which period and for whom, and were spread over four places. A
tag in capitals read as a button. The coverage line and the labels repeated what the panel and
the tabs already say.
