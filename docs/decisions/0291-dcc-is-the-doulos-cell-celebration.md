# 2026-09-25 — DCC is the Doulos Cell Celebration

The specification used "DCC" throughout and never said what it stands for. Decision 0290 put
"Doulos Cell Celebration" on the Record screen at the owner's request, and
`architecture-guardian` raised that a screen was defining a ministry term the specification
did not.

## The ruling

**DCC is the Doulos Cell Celebration**, stated in Section 9. The owner confirmed it.

## Why

A term the church uses is defined once, in the section that governs it, rather than by a label
on one screen.
