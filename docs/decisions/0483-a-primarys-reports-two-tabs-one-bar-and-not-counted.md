# 2026-11-12 — A Primary's Reports: two Attendance tabs, one control bar, and *not counted*

Owner ruling, 2026-11-12, chosen from screenshots of a Full view leader's Reports on a phone and a
laptop, today beside the proposal, three questions one after another. It extends decision 0481 to
a Full view leader and changes words only for a removed DCC Sunday.

## The ruling

**1. A Full view leader's `Reports` has five tabs, *CG Attendance*, *DCC Attendance*, *# of Cell
Groups*, *# of Cell Leaders* and *# of People*, with no *Cell Group* / *DCC* switch**, as the
Senior Pastors' (decision 0481): one row from `lg` up, two to a row on a phone with the fifth
filling the third. The Admin and a Network Admin keep decision 0420's *Attendance* tab and its
switch, their Reports carrying more tabs.

**2. A Full view leader's *Weekly* / *Monthly* / *Yearly* switch sits in the control bar beside
the month**, on CG Attendance and DCC Attendance, rather than on a line of its own above the bar:
the period is one control. From `lg` the switch and the month share one line, with the note under
them in the same bar; on a phone they stack in the one bar.

**3. A Sunday removed from the DCC calendar is said to be *not counted*, never *no service***, for
every reader: "Sunday 14 June not counted" in the note and after *N Sundays counted*, "3 Sundays
counted this month." under *How often people came* (whose next sentence reads "Each row counts
the people who came that many times."), and *Not counted* with the Admin's reason on Record's DCC
list. Section 9 still requires a removal to be shown on every report covering it; only the words
change.

## Why

The owner wanted the Primaries' Reports as plain as the Senior Pastors' (decision 0481), and the
space the period controls took used: the switch and the month were two layers for one choice,
which design systems put in one toolbar. And DCC is held every Sunday: "no service" read as though
it had not been, while a removal is a decision about what is counted.

Considered and rejected: "removed from the DCC calendar", which the owner judged still implied
DCC might not happen; and changing the Senior Pastors' control bar, which also holds *Figures for*,
in the same change.

## What is owed

Nothing.
