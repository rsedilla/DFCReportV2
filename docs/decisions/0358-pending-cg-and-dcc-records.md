# 2026-10-17 — Pending CG Records and Pending DCC Records

Record's two Pending lists were named *Pending CG Reports* (decision 0332) and *Pending DCC
Reports* (decision 0336). The owner asked on 17 Oct for both to say *Records*, because they
are Record's lists: what they name is attendance still to be recorded, and *Reports* is the
name of another page.

## The ruling

**The two lists are named *Pending CG Records* and *Pending DCC Records*.** Only the words
change: what each lists, its count, its order and who sees it stay as decisions 0336 and 0340
set them, and so does every route.

## Why

A leader reads *Reports* as the Reports page. The lists sit under Record and lead to
recording, so they take Record's word.
