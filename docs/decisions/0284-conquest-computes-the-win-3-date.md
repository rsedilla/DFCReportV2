# 2026-09-24 — Conquest computes the Win 3 date

Section 27 has `cells`, `hierarchy` and `suynl` each compute their half of a goal and
`conquest` compose them. Win 3's date does not divide that way: the earliest instant at
which three of a person's direct disciples each held three lessons depends on the pastoral
edges and the lessons together.

The owner chose this answer from a drawing of the three options.

## The ruling

**`conquest` computes it.** `hierarchy` returns each direct discipling edge with its dates,
`suynl` returns the instant each person's third current lesson was filed, and `conquest`
finds the earliest instant at which edges to three different disciples are in force, each
at or after its disciple's third lesson. Nothing is stored.

Raise 12 leaders' date spans `cells` and `hierarchy` the same way and is not settled here.

## Why

It keeps the shape Section 27 already names, each module answering from its own tables and
`conquest` composing. Having `suynl` compute it would put a Conquest rule in `suynl`.

The cost is that the tab's counts at Whole Church read every discipling edge and every
third-lesson instant in the church, which Section 2's recorded scale keeps small.
