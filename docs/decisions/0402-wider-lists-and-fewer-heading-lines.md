# 2026-10-28 — Wider lists, and no heading line that repeats the page

Owner ruling, 2026-10-28, chosen from real screenshots of the pages today beside each proposal,
after the owner asked whether the app used a laptop's width and what other applications with a
sidebar do.

## The ruling

**1. A list or a table is up to 1280px wide, beside the sidebar.** Cells, People, Record, the
Reports tables, Family Tree, SUYNL and the other list pages were capped at 1024px, which left about
a third of a 1536px laptop empty to the right. They now widen to 1280px and stop there, so a very
wide monitor does not stretch the columns apart. They still start 40px from the sidebar: centring
them was shown and refused, because the page looked like it floated.

**2. One person's page stays at 1024px, and a form at 768px.** A person's page is read rather
than filled in and carries short tables of its own; a form is easier to fill in narrow. All three
widths start at the same place, so moving between pages moves only the right-hand edge. Phones and
small laptops are unchanged: below 1024px every page fills the screen as before.

**3. A heading carries no line that only repeats the page.** Six lines are removed:

- Record, for a Full view leader: "Record your Cell Group meetings and DCC Sundays here, and see
  who in your branch still has to." The tabs and the line inside the box say it.
- Cells: "Your Cells and this month's meetings." The headline total and the month say it.
- People: "Find someone by name or Member ID." The search box's label says it. Decision 0385
  named this line; it goes by this ruling.
- Awaiting Leader Assignment and Absent 3+ Months: each repeated its tab, whose box carries its
  own heading and explanation.
- Family Tree: "Your branch, generation by generation." The tree shows it.

A line stays where it says something the page does not: the SUYNL and Training counts and what
the names stand for, Reports' line naming what each report is, the Senior Pastors' and the
recording screens' Record lines, and *Add a person*'s "Search first".

Most products with a sidebar, and the design systems they publish, keep a heading's description
optional and use it only for what the title and the page do not already say; and they give lists
and tables the width beside the sidebar while keeping forms narrow.
