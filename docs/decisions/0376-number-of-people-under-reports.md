# 2026-10-20 — # of People under Reports, for everyone who has it

Decision 0364 put *# of People* under the `People` item for a Full view leader and the Admin, as
an exception to Section 19's home for figures, while the Senior Pastors kept theirs under
`Reports`. On 20 Oct the owner looked at the two side by side, in real screenshots, and chose to
end the exception.

## The ruling

1. **`Reports` carries *# of People* for every account whose screens have it**: the Senior
   Pastors (decision 0326, unchanged), a Full view leader, and the Admin. A Full view leader's
   `Reports` tabs read *CG Attendance Reports*, *DCC Attendance Reports*, *SUYNL Reports* and
   *# of People*; the Admin's read *Cell Groups*, *DCC*, *SUYNL*, *Training*, *Conquest* and
   *# of People*.
2. **The figure is unchanged.** A Full view leader reads their own branch for a month and opens
   a leader beneath them, at `GET /api/v1/reports/people/branch`, as decision 0364 set out; a
   whole-church reader reads the whole church and both roots' tables. It is at
   `/reports/number-of-people` for all of them.
3. **`People` carries *Add People* and, on Full view screens, *Family Tree*.** The old address
   `/people/number-of-people` forwards to Reports with its month and leader, so a saved link
   still opens the figure.
4. **On a phone a Full view leader's four tabs sit two by two**, equal; from `sm` they sit in one
   row sized to their words (decision 0361). The Admin's six sit three across on a phone and in
   one row from `sm`.

This replaces decision 0364's points 1 and 6. Its points 2 to 5 stand: what the figure counts,
the route and its guard, the Admin's figure, and `Branch` linked from nowhere.

## Why

Every other month-by-month figure is under `Reports`, and the Senior Pastors already found this
one there. Section 19 puts figures on the Dashboard and under `Reports`; with this ruling it has no
exception, and leaders helping each other find a figure in the same place. *Family Tree* stays
under `People`, where the people themselves are.
