# 2026-09-27 — Two pickers search one Network

Decision 0244 kept every person picker church-wide. Adding a member to a Cell and naming a new
pastoral leader then listed people the operation would refuse, because section 10 keeps a
Cell's members in its Network and section 5 refuses a cross-Network edge, so a leader found out
only after pressing Add or Move. The owner approved narrowing both on 2026-09-25 and chose this
version after seeing it running.

## The ruling

**1. Adding a member to a Cell searches the Cell's Network**, which section 10 makes its
leader's. **Naming a new pastoral leader searches the Network of the person being moved.**

**2. The narrowing follows the Cell or person acted on, never the searcher**, so a Senior
Pastor, who may act in both Networks (decision 0002), still reaches everybody the operation
allows.

**3. The API filters** (`GET /api/v1/people?network=`), so a page is never short of people who
match. It narrows rows and widens nothing; the capability, the scope and the per-person fields
are unchanged, and the operation's own refusals still stand.

**4. The picker says so**: "Showing the Men's Network only, because this Cell is in the Men's
Network." Where the Network cannot be read, the search stays church-wide.

**5. Adding a Person and naming who ran a Cell meeting stay church-wide**: decision 0274 lets
any current Person run a meeting.

## Why

A list should not offer a choice the save will refuse.
