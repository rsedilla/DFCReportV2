# 2026-10-27 — One button marks the rest Absent

Owner ruling, 2026-10-27, chosen from a side-by-side of three ways to record a Cell Group
meeting: today's, everyone starting Absent, and this one; and, from screenshots of the built
screen after review, that the button appears only once a member is marked.

## The ruling

**1. On the Cell Group meeting screen, a leader who has marked who came may mark everyone else
Absent with one button.** It reads "Mark the other *N* Absent", naming how many it will mark,
and "Mark the 1 left Absent" for one. It marks only members nobody has marked, and leaves every
mark already made.

**2. Nobody starts as Absent.** The screen still opens with every member unmarked, and Save
still waits until every member is marked (Section 13), so every Absent is one the leader chose,
by a mark or by the button.

**3. The button is shown only once at least one member is marked and while others are left,**
on a meeting that met, including a correction where members have no mark yet. It is never
offered before a member is marked: offered at the start, "Mark all *N* Absent" would be option
A again, one tap later, and its count would warn of nothing. A mark already stored, or kept on
the device and offered back (decision 0345), counts, being the leader's own. For a meeting nobody came to, the
leader marks one member Absent and then the rest. The DCC checklist does not have it: it saves
only the lines that are marked, so a mark nobody made would become a record.

**4. Nothing on the server changes.** A submission still names every member exactly once, present
or not. The button only fills in what the leader would otherwise tap one by one.

## Why

The owner found recording a large Cell slow: every member needs a mark, and most weeks most
of those who did not come are simply absent. Starting everyone as Absent (option A) was the
owner's first suggestion and was compared with this one. It is faster by one tap, and it saves
anybody the leader forgot to tick as Absent with nothing to say so: a member who came and was
missed would be recorded as missing, which lowers their attendance and every count that reads
it, and no figure could tell a slip from a real absence.

The button keeps most of the speed and keeps the Absent a decision. Its count is the last check:
a leader expecting two absences who reads "Mark the other 4 Absent" sees that two were missed.
It does not stop a leader who presses without reading, and is not claimed to.

The screen's choice of 2026-09-15, recorded in the screen's own description, refused "mark all
present", where one tap would record everybody as having come. A button for the rest, offered
only after the leader has marked somebody, is a different thing, and that refusal stands.
