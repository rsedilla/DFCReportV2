# 2026-11-05 — Attendance has Weekly, Monthly and Yearly, on one line, for every reader

On 2026-11-05 the owner asked for the Reports *Attendance* tab to be simpler: choosing *Cell
Group* or *DCC* should show Weekly, Monthly and Yearly, the same for a Full view leader, the
Senior Pastors and the Admin. Until then the Admin's lengths sat beside *Cell Group* / *DCC*
while a Full view leader's and the Senior Pastors' sat in a second box under it, and the three
were offered different lengths: Weekly, Monthly and *12 Months* for a Full view leader;
Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Year and *12 Months* for the Senior Pastors; Weekly, Monthly,
Quarterly and Year for the Admin. The owner chose from screenshots of the three screens today
and of the proposal, and asked that Yearly say it is the graph.

## The ruling

**1. Every reader's *Attendance* offers Weekly, Monthly and Yearly**, on one line beside *Cell
Group* / *DCC*, the month and *Figures for* staying in the panel under it. Choosing *Cell Group*
or *DCC* keeps the length chosen, Yearly included.

**2. Yearly is the last 12 months**, the graph that was *12 Months* (decisions 0326, 0377, 0380
and 0398), one point a month, and the Admin reads it on *Attendance* as the Senior Pastors do:
Whole Church and each root's branch, or one leader's. Its button carries a small graph, and one
line under the switch says "Yearly is a graph of the last 12 months, one point a month."
whichever length is chosen, so nobody has to open it to learn what it is.

**3. Quarterly and the calendar Year are withdrawn from every reader's screens**, and with them
the Year's month-by-month table (decision 0257) and the finished quarters and years of decision
0310. An address naming a quarter or a year opens the month. The report routes still answer a
quarter or a year (decisions 0293, 0294 and 0310); only the screens stop asking.

**4. The *#* tabs are unchanged** but for their button's words: the Senior Pastors' and a Full
view leader's keep Monthly and *Last 12 months* in their panel, its button now reading
*Yearly* as on *Attendance*. The Admin's *Counts* keeps its month alone.

**5. The Admin's graph takes the Senior Pastors' colours**, green for the Whole Church, blue and
pink for the two Network branches (decision 0327), which Section 23's one exception now names.

## Why

Three readers of one tab were offered three different sets of lengths in two different
layouts, and the owner wanted one. The graph was chosen as Yearly over the calendar year
because it answers the question a year is asked for, how attendance has moved, at a glance and
in one request, where the calendar year said little before its last months and read each month
separately. The quarter goes with it, since nothing on the owner's list kept it.
