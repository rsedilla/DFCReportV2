# 2026-11-03 — The Admin's three counts are one tab, Counts

The Admin's `Reports` had *Attendance*, *Encounter Readiness*, *Training* and *# of People*, and no
*# of Cell Groups* or *# of Cell Leaders*. Those two were made for the Senior Pastors (decision
0326) and given to a Full view leader (decision 0380), and the Admin's list was never widened,
though the API already let the Admin read both and their pages showed the whole church when
opened by address. On 2026-11-03 the owner asked whether something was missing, was shown the
Admin's tabs with the two added and with one *Counts* tab, on the real screen, and chose *Counts*
with its switch and the month on one row, for the Admin only.

## The ruling

**1. The Admin's tabs are *Attendance*, *Counts*, *Encounter Readiness* and *Training*.** *Counts*
replaces *# of People* and holds three figures: *Cell Groups*, *Cell Leaders* and *People*, the
Senior Pastors' *# of Cell Groups*, *# of Cell Leaders* and *# of People* for the whole church.

**2. A switch inside *Counts* chooses the figure**, as Attendance's *Cell Group* / *DCC* switch
does (decision 0420). Each figure keeps its own address, `/reports/number-of-cells`,
`/reports/number-of-cell-leaders` and `/reports/number-of-people`, and the month travels from one
to the next.

**3. The switch sits in the figure's control bar, beside the month, on one row.** On a phone the
bar wraps, the switch above the month.

**4. Nothing else changes.** The Senior Pastors and a Full view leader keep *# of Cell Groups*,
*# of Cell Leaders* and *# of People* as tabs of their own. The figures, the tables and who may
read them are unchanged.

## Why

Six tabs do not fit one row at the one tab size every tab row keeps (decision 0416), so adding
the two would have wrapped *Training* onto a second row. Folding the three counts into one tab
follows the pattern the owner chose for Attendance, and putting the switch beside the month gives
the figures the room the two rows took.
