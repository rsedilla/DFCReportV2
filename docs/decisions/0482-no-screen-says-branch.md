# 2026-11-12 — No screen says *branch*

Owner ruling, 2026-11-12, chosen from screenshots of the twelve-month graph on a phone and a laptop,
today beside the proposal, with a table of every other place the word appears. It changes words
only.

## The ruling

**No screen says *branch*.** The church does not use the word for the people a leader cares for,
so a screen uses the words it already has for them: *Your Disciples* as a heading (decision 0363),
and *in your care* in a sentence (decision 0436), with *[name]'s Disciples* or *in [name]'s care*
where a leader is opened. In particular:

- the twelve-month graph's heading is "DCC Attendance · Your Disciples" (or "· Manuel D.'s
  Disciples"), and its line a plain sentence, "How many people came to DCC each month, each
  person counted once.", the counts "How many Cell Groups were running at the end of each
  month." and the like, *# of People*'s saying "How many people there were at the end of each
  month." because the Senior Pastors read the same line;
- the Senior Pastors' key reads "Whole Church and each pastor's disciples";
- *CG Records Due* and *DCC Records Due* say "in your care" (decision 0477's line), and an emptied
  list for one leader "Nothing still to record for those in Ana Lim's care.";
- the My 12 table's extra line is "Others in your care", or "Others in [name]'s care";
- the account ticks, the move dialogs, the duplicate warnings, the People search, the restart
  dialog, the privacy notice and the Admin's audit log say "in their care", "under another
  leader", "the whole church" or "outside the searcher's care" as each sentence needs;
- the Cells to Approve card's *Branch* line is *Under*, naming the leader;
- the Senior Pastors' SUYNL rows read "Under neither pastor" and "Nobody here is counted.";
- the page that was headed *Branch* is headed *Family Tree*;
- the API's refusals that a screen shows say "in your care", "outside your care" and "the Primary
  over them".

The specification keeps *branch* as its own word for a leader's subtree, in definitions,
arguments and the names of rulings; it is not shown on a screen. Code identifiers are unchanged.

## Why

The owner said the church's G12 vocabulary does not call a Primary's people a branch. Usability
guidance asks a system to use its users' own words rather than its own (Nielsen Norman Group's
second heuristic), and both phrases used instead are already the application's.

Considered and rejected: changing the graph's heading alone, which would have left the word on
about fifteen other screens, two of them reworded the same day.

## What is owed

Nothing.
