# 2026-10-20 — The SUYNL report is Encounter Readiness, with no counts of its own

The SUYNL tab under `Reports` shows the next Encounter season and who is getting ready for it
(decisions 0296 and 0297), then Growth's three counts read only (decision 0292). A Full view
leader's tab read *SUYNL Reports* (decision 0361) and a whole-church reader's *SUYNL*, beside
`Growth`'s own *SUYNL*, where lessons are ticked. On 20 Oct the owner compared the two side by side
in real screenshots and chose to tell them apart.

## The ruling

1. **The tab reads *Encounter Readiness* for every reader that has it**, a Full view leader and a
   whole-church reader alike. Its address, `/reports/suynl`, and everything it shows above the
   cards are unchanged: the four steps to the next Encounter and the readiness table.
2. **It shows no count cards.** Not started, In progress and Graduated are on `Growth`'s SUYNL
   tab, unchanged, where they also filter the list; the page links there to tick lessons.
3. `Growth`'s *SUYNL* keeps its name: it is the place a lesson is recorded.

This replaces decision 0361's *SUYNL Reports* label and, for this one tab, decision 0292's count
cards under `Reports`. Training and Conquest keep theirs.

## Why

Two tabs named SUYNL read as one thing in two places, and the cards repeated figures one click
away. The report's heading already said what it was for, who is getting ready for the next
Encounter God Weekend, so the tab now says it too.
