# 2026-11-12 — Record's tabs say what to do without working it out

Owner ruling, 2026-11-12, chosen from screenshots of the installed iPhone app, today beside the
proposal, one tab at a time. It rewords three sentences decision 0432 set and the empty line
decision 0436 set, and changes no rule.

## The ruling

**1. *Update Records* states this week and its due date as dates.** Its line reads "Record your
own Cell Group meetings and DCC Sundays here. This week, **9–15 Nov**, is due by **12 noon on
Monday 23 Nov**.", worked out from today in Asia/Manila, in place of "Each week is due by 12 noon
on the second Monday after it." A week spanning two months names both ("30 Nov – 6 Dec"). A week
before the weekly rule (Section 13), which has no due date, keeps only the first sentence. A
Recording-only account reads the same words behind the ⓘ beside the welcome (decision 0454),
without "own".

**2. *Update Records*' empty list says what is recorded**: "All your Cell Group meetings are
recorded.", "All your DCC Sundays are recorded." or, under *Leaders Under Me*, "Your leaders' Cell
Group meetings are all recorded.", each followed by "Thank you for your faithfulness!", in place
of "All recorded." for all three.

**3. *CG Records Due* and *DCC Records Due* point at what each row shows**: "Cell Group meetings
in your branch that are not recorded yet. Tap **Record** where it shows; for the others, remind
the leader named to record them.", and the same with "DCC Sundays". A reader holding the list
church-wide reads *in the church*, as decision 0436 has it.

## Why

The owner reported leaders guessing. "The second Monday after it" asks the reader to count
Mondays, and many stop at the first. Everyday apps, from school to payroll, state the date rather
than the rule behind it, and each row already carries its own *Due* date (decision 0335), so the
rule need not be explained here. "Record the ones that are yours" asked the reader to work out
which rows are theirs, while *Record* is offered wherever the API lets them record, a leader
beneath them without an account included (decisions 0313 and 0368); the button and the leader's
name are what the reader can see. "All recorded." under the Cell Group half did not say what was.

Considered and rejected: keeping the rule after the date ("Every week is due at 12 noon on the
second Monday after it ends"), which the owner did not choose.

## What is owed

Nothing. The deadline itself is Section 13's and is unchanged.
