# 2026-10-05 — Show only Cells behind is Record's Cells behind, read every page

Record's *Cells behind* is Section 15's list of Cells with meetings still awaiting a record, and it
includes a closed Cell while its month is open (ruling of 2026-09-21). Its link *See every Cell
behind* opens Filed reports' *Coverage by Cell* with *Show only Cells behind* pressed, which listed
running Cells only, so the link opened on fewer Cells than the list it came from. The owner chose
this version from screenshots of both screens. Their Claude Design says nothing about it: its
coverage table is by leader and has no closed Cell.

## The ruling

**1. With *Show only Cells behind* pressed, Coverage by Cell is Section 15's Cells-behind list,
read every page**: the running Cells behind, then each closed Cell behind while its month is open,
with *Closed* and the date it closed beside its name. Behind is what decision 0267 defines, and a
closed Cell's meetings stop at its closure.

**2. Without the filter, the table lists running Cells only**, as every other count of Cells does
(Section 15).

**3. The count beneath the rows names the closed Cells separately**: *1 behind · 1 not behind, and
3 closed Cells still behind*.

**4. A failed read of the closed Cells never says that nothing is behind**; the failure is shown
instead.

## Why

One link should open on the Cells it names, and "behind" means one thing on every surface
(decision 0267). This is the one view outside Record where Section 15 lets running and closed Cells
be mixed, so it is the only one that does.
