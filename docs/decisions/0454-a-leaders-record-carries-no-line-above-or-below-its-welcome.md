# 2026-11-07 — Record carries no line above or below its welcome

The owner read `Record` in the installed app on a phone, marked the month above the welcome, the
sentence beside it and the *Whose* heading over the switch, and asked for the space back. On a
laptop or an iPad the same words could sit behind a tooltip. Every change below was chosen from
screenshots of the app at phone, iPad and laptop widths.

## The ruling

**`Record`, the Senior Pastors' apart, draws no line above or below its welcome.** The month
(*Record · November 2026*) and the Recording-only account's sentence, saying what the page is for
and when each week is due, are not drawn.

**From `sm` up, an ⓘ beside the welcome holds them.** It opens on a tap or a click, and closes on a
second one, on Escape or on a tap anywhere else. On a phone there is no ⓘ: the space is the list's,
and each row already says its own deadline.

**The *Whose* switch's heading is read by a screen reader and not drawn.** Its two choices say what
it is. **The reader's own work comes first**: *Own Cell* (or *Own 12* under DCC), then *Leaders
Under Me*, a leader's own Cell being their first responsibility (the owner's words). On a phone
each choice spans the row.

**The verse under the welcome (decision 0425) is as wide as the page**, lined up with the frame
and the table below it, and **on a phone it is justified with no word broken across a line**.

**The Senior Pastors' heading keeps its month and its line**, being outside what was asked.

## Why

A tooltip that waits on a hover opens on no phone or iPad, which have no hover, so the note is a
button the reader presses, the pattern accessibility guidance calls a toggletip. It is not the only
place the rule can be found, which that guidance also asks: every row carries its own deadline.

Justified text on a narrow screen either leaves wider gaps between words or breaks words across
lines. The owner was shown both and chose the gaps: a verse is not broken mid-word.

Considered and rejected: the two choices side by side on a phone, which saves a row but wraps
*Leaders Under Me* onto two lines.
