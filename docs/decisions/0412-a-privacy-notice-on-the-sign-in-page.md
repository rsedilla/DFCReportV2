# 2026-10-31 — A Privacy Notice, linked from the sign-in page

Owner ruling, approved on 2026-10-31 from screenshots of the sign-in page and the notice side by
side, after the owner looked at how Philippine churches and organisations label theirs.

## What was missing

DREAMS keeps the personal information of the church's members, minors among them, and nothing in
it said so. The Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) asks an organisation that keeps
personal information to say what it keeps, why, who sees it and how to reach it.

## The ruling

**1. The sign-in page carries a short notice** under "I have forgotten my password": DREAMS holds
the personal information of the members of Doulos For Christ World Harvest Ministry, children
among them, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012; use it only to care for the people you lead; and a
**Privacy Notice** link.

**2. The Privacy Notice is a page of its own, `/privacy`**, open to anyone, signed in or not. In
plain words it says what DREAMS keeps and does not keep, why, how children's records are kept, who
can see a record, how it is protected and by which three service providers (Hostinger, Brevo and
Google Drive), how long it is kept, the rights the Act gives, and whom to ask.

**3. Questions go to "Doulos For Christ", whose contact details are at doulosforchrist.org.** The
owner chose to name the church rather than a person, so the page does not change when the role
changes hands.

**4. It is dated "Last updated: October 21, 2026"**, the date the owner gave.

**5. The notice must stay true.** A change to what DREAMS keeps about a person, who can see it, or
who helps run it makes the page wrong, so the same change updates it.

## What it does not change

What DREAMS keeps, who may see it, or any route. It describes Sections 3, 7, 8 and 24 as they
stand; one sentence names the exception Section 3's duplicate check makes to "each leader sees only
their own branch". No record, route or migration changes.
