# 2026-11-02 — Fewer tabs: Needs Attention on Cells, Attendance on Reports

The Admin's Cells page had seven tabs and wrapped onto two rows, and Reports had five, wrapping
on a laptop and taking three rows on a phone. The owner asked how sites elsewhere handle too many
tabs. Those that keep a row of tabs keep it to one row, grouping by meaning (Google Workspace,
Microsoft 365, Shopify) or putting the rest under *More* (GitHub, Gmail, Jira). The owner chose
the grouping from side-by-side screenshots on laptop and phone, and for Reports chose a layout of
their own: the switch on attendance, the three counts kept as tabs.

## The ruling

**1. On Cells, the lists that ask for action share one tab, *Needs Attention*,** wherever more
than one of them is offered: *No CG Yet*, *Under 3 Members*, *Inactive 3+ Months* and *Time
Clashes*. A switch inside the tab chooses the list, each list keeps its own address and its count
where it has one, and the tab stands where the first of them stood. The Admin's tabs are
therefore *Active Cells*, *Needs Attention*, *Closed Cells* and *Cells to Approve*. An account
offered one of them alone, a Full view leader's *No CG Yet*, keeps it as its own tab.

**2. On Reports, *Cell Groups* and *DCC* are one tab, *Attendance*,** with a *Cell Group* / *DCC*
switch inside it, each keeping its own address. A Full view leader's and the Senior Pastors' tabs
are therefore *Attendance*, *# of Cell Groups*, *# of Cell Leaders* and *# of People*, and the
Admin's *Attendance*, *Encounter Readiness*, *Training* and *# of People*. The reports themselves
are unchanged.

**3. On a phone, an odd last tab fills its row**, on every row of tabs, rather than standing half
width beside a gap.

**4. A row holding one tab is not drawn**: Growth with *SUYNL* alone, on a Full view leader's and
a recording account's screens, shows its pane without one. This replaces the owner's choice of
2026-10-19 to show that tab in the frame. Record's one list for a recording account keeps its
tab, which names the list (decision 0323).
