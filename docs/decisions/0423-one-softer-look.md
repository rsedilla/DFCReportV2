# 2026-11-02 — One softer look on every screen

The owner found the screens plain: square boxes, black-outlined buttons in spaced capitals, and
a heavy green frame around every tab's pane. They asked for the best look the world's
well-made websites offer, as one standard for every account. On 2026-11-02 they were shown My
Cell in two styles, and then every Primary and every Admin page as it is and in the chosen
style, on a laptop and a phone, and chose it.

## The ruling

**1. Corners are rounded**: 8px on a button or a field, 12px on a card, a pane or a table.
This replaces the square corners kept since 2026-09-14.

**2. The page is a pale grey, and what sits on it is a white card**, with a light rule and a
faint shadow: a tab's pane, a frame, a control bar and a table.

**3. A button keeps its words as written, in Title Case**, rather than spaced capitals. One action on
a screen is filled in the accent; the others are white with their words in the accent. My
Cell's *Request New Cell* is the filled one.

**4. A tab row is underlined, not boxed.** The chosen tab's words are in the accent with an
accent bar beneath them; the others are grey. A count beside a tab or a switch is a small
pale green pill. This replaces decision 0416's filled tab.

**5. A table has a light header**: small grey capitals on the pale grey, and white rows
divided by a rule, with no alternating tint. This replaces decision 0416's solid header and
striped rows.

**6. A label is a pill with its words as written.** Every label of one kind keeps one look,
so none is told from another by colour; the words carry it (sections 13, 17 and 19). The
Senior Pastors' *Completed* stays pale red, the exception Section 19 already makes.

**7. The sidebar's items are written as words, not capitals**, on rounded rows, the current
one keeping its bar.

Sizes are unchanged: decision 0416's one tab size, button width, pill switch and search bar
stand.

## Why

A screen that reads calmly is one a leader opens again. The grey page and white cards group
what belongs together without a frame drawn round it, one filled button says what the screen
is for, and words in their own case read faster than capitals. Nothing here carries meaning
in colour, and the colours used are pairs `web/scripts/check-contrast.mjs` already checks in
both themes.
