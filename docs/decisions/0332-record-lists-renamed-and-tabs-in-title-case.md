# 2026-10-11 — Record's lists renamed, and every tab row in title case

Owner ruling, 2026-10-11, made from the Primaries' screens. It amends Section 19 and decision 0329.

## The ruling

**1. Record's four lists are renamed** everywhere they are chosen or headed: *Awaiting a record*
becomes *Update Record*, *Cells behind* becomes *Pending CG Reports*, *Needs a new leader* becomes
*Awaiting Leader Assignment*, and *Not in a Cell* becomes *Awaiting CG Assignment*. Filed reports'
filter reads *Show only pending CG reports*. Each list is the same list as before; nothing counted
changes. Where the old words describe a state rather than name a list, such as a meeting
"awaiting a record" or a person "Not in a Cell", they stay.

**2. The four buttons are one size, as wide as the longest label**, each with its count in a box
as before. Below a laptop's width they stay a grid of two by two. This replaces decision 0329's
"sized to their words" for this row, and the Senior Pastors' two Record tabs, which share it, are
equal too.

**3. Update Record's *Cell Group* and *DCC* buttons are equal width, and so are its *People I
oversee* and *Mine***, each pair as wide as its longer label rather than stretched across the row.

**4. Every tab row in the application writes its labels in title case rather than capitals**,
for example *CG Attendance*, *DCC Attendance*, *Recording Status* and *Filed Reports*. Section 19 names
the tabs the same way.

**5. The Senior Pastors' *# of Cells* tab, on Reports and in Trends, is named *# of Cell Groups***,
and its line reads "The Cell Groups running, month by month."

## Why

The owner chose the words the church uses for the work waiting to be done, and wanted the
buttons that sit together to look alike. The choices were made one at a time from drawings and
then checked on real screenshots of the running app.
