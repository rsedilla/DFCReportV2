# 2026-09-27 — Still to record links to Filed reports

The Network screen's Still to record card showed a branch's DCC records and Cell meetings
behind this month, and section 19 said the screen "hands nothing to Reports", so a leader who
saw a figure had no way to the rows behind it. The owner chose, from a drawing, to link each
figure.

## The ruling

**1. Each Still to record figure above zero is a link** to Filed reports, By leader, for the
focused person's branch and the month the card names: the DCC figure to DCC, the Cell figure
to Cell groups. A zero, or a figure not yet read, stays plain text.

**2. The link is always scoped to the focused person**, the reader included, so a
whole-church reader looking at their own branch does not land on the whole church.

**3. It is offered only to a reader holding `reports.view_subtree`**, the capability Filed
reports is read under; anyone else keeps the plain figures. Filed reports keeps its own guard.

**4. Nothing is counted differently.** The card counts the branch as it stands now (decision
0252) and Filed reports counts the month by section 20's rules, so the two can differ where
somebody changed leader during the month. That is stated rather than reconciled, as the
owner was told before choosing.

## Why

A number that says something is still to be recorded should lead to who owes it.
