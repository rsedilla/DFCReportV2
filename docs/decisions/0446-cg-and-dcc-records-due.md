# 2026-11-05 — *CG Records Due* and *DCC Records Due*

Record's two lists of what is still to record in an open week were named *Pending CG Records* and
*Pending DCC Records* (decision 0358). On 2026-11-05 the owner asked for plainer names that fit a
church rather than a business, first suggesting *No CG Records*. That was set aside because the
lists hold work that may still be recorded in time, and "no records" reads as a failure and, beside
its count, as "none". The owner then chose *CG Records Due* from screenshots of Record on a laptop
and a phone, beside today's names and *Due CG Reports*.

## The ruling

**Record's two lists are named *CG Records Due* and *DCC Records Due***, on their tabs, their
headings, the Admin's Home and wherever else a screen names them. What they hold, who sees them and
how they are counted are unchanged (decisions 0358, 0368 and 0370).

## Why

"Due" is a reminder that something is expected, which matches the rule (a week is due by 12 noon
on the second Monday after it) without marking anyone as failing. "Records" rather than "Reports"
keeps the page's own word, beside *Update Records* and *Recording Progress*, and keeps the lists
apart from the `Reports` item in the sidebar. Both names fit one line on a phone, where *Pending CG
Records* wrapped onto two.
