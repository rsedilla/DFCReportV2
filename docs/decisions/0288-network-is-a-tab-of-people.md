# 2026-09-24 — Network is a tab of People

The sidebar had six items, and two of them answered one question from two sides: `People`
finds a person, and `Network` shows who leads whom. A leader looking for somebody in their
care had two places to try.

The owner chose this answer from a clickable drawing of it.

## The ruling

**`People` and `Network` are one sidebar item, `People`, with two tabs, `People` and
`Network`.** Each tab is its own address, `/people` and `/network`, and each screen is as it
was. The sidebar is `Record · Reports · People · Cells · Growth`, one order for every account
as decision 0277 set.

## Why

It keeps both screens and every link to them, and takes one item off a bar that had six on a
320px phone. `Growth` already groups three screens the same way.
