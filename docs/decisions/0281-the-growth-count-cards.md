# 2026-09-24 — The Growth count cards

Section 28 puts counts at the head of the `SUYNL` and `Training` tabs and counts "those who
have reached that step" without saying what the steps are. The list endpoint has to return
them, so the cards had to be named before it could be written.

The owner chose this answer from a drawing of both tabs beside their Training design.

## The ruling

**SUYNL has three cards: Not started, In progress (one to nine lessons) and Graduated (ten of
ten).** Each current person the tab lists is on exactly one, so the three add up to everyone
listed.

**Training has a card per school — the Encounter, Life Class, SOL 1, SOL 2, SOL 3 — and one
for people with none yet.** The school cards overlap, because one person holds several
graduations.

**A card narrows the list to the people behind it**, as Section 28 already asks. The counts
are as of now and cover current people only (decisions 0278 and 0279).

## Why

The Training cards are the owner's design, less its accented tag, which Section 28 already
refuses. SUYNL had no design; three cards that partition the list are the ones a reader can
check against the list itself.
