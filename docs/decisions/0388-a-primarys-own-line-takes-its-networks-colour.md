# 2026-10-25 — A Primary's own line takes its Network's colour

Owner ruling, 2026-10-25, the day the softer Trends colours went live (pull request #374). The
owner asked that a Primary's own graph be coloured like Bishop Oriel's and Pastora
Geraldine's lines.

## The ruling

**A Full view leader's own *Last 12 months* (decision 0380) is drawn in the colour the Senior
Pastors' *Last 12 months* gives that leader's Network branch**: the Men's blue for a leader of the
Men's Network, the Women's pink, dashed, for a leader of the Women's. A leader with no Network is
drawn in ink, as every Full view leader's line was before.

**The Network is the leader's Network now**, sent on the line by
`GET /api/v1/reports/trends/branch`, which had sent none. It labels the line and counts nothing:
the figures, each month's own authorization and every gap are unchanged. A leader whose Network
changed after a month is drawn in today's colour for that month too; decisions 0082 and 0154
refuse a Network change while a person leads anyone or leads a Cell, so the case is narrow, and the Senior Pastors'
graph already colours one leader by today's table the same way.

## Why

Decision 0327 allowed colour to mark the Whole Church and the two Network branches on the Senior
Pastors' graph alone, as an exception to Section 23's rule that a leader is never encoded in
colour. A Primary's line is one of those Network branches, so it is drawn in the same colour on
both screens. The dash follows the line's Network, so the Senior Pastors' graph, whose chosen
leader carries none, draws a Women's Primary solid where their own screen draws them dashed. It
grades nothing, and the line is not told apart by colour alone:
it is the only line, named *Your branch*, with its figures one click away. The theme green was
rejected because on the Senior Pastors' graph green means the whole church.

## What this amends

Decision 0327's "for this graph alone", which now also reaches a Full view leader's own *Last 12
months*. Sections 17 and 23 are amended.

Decision 0388, indexed in [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) and amended into [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md)
Sections 17 and 23.
