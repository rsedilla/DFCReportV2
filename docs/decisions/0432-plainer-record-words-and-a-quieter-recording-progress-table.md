# 2026-11-03 — Plainer Record words, and a quieter Recording Progress table

On 2026-11-03 the owner asked for four sentences to be reworded and for the Recording Progress table
to be given a better look. They were shown each one on the real screens, the current version beside
a recommendation, and chose to build all five as recommended.

## The ruling

**1. Four sentences are reworded.**

- *Update Records* says "Please record your own Cell Group meetings and DCC Sundays. Each week is
  due by 12 noon on the second Monday after it."
- *Pending CG Records* says "These Cell Groups in your branch have meetings not yet recorded.
  Record the ones that are yours, and remind their leader about the rest."
- *Pending DCC Records* says "These leaders have DCC Sundays not yet recorded. Record the ones that
  are yours, and remind the others."
- *Absent 3+ Months* says "This list shows people who were never marked Present at a Cell meeting
  or DCC Sunday in August, September, and October 2026, but were marked Absent at least once each
  month. Only full months in a Cell Group or under a leader count, so new members are excluded."
  The year is said once where the three months share it, and on each month where they do not.

**2. The Recording Progress tables look quieter**, the Senior Pastors' and a Full view leader's
(decisions 0325 and 0334), and they alone. They are exceptions to the light table of decision
0423:

- the headings are in sentence case, without the shared header's tracked capitals;
- every other row is lightly shaded, and the rows are a little shorter;
- a figure where the row's leaders owed nothing is a grey dash rather than "0 of 0 leaders", and a
  screen reader says "None owed";
- *Nothing owed* is grey, and *N leaders still to record* is bold.

The rows, their order, their figures where something was owed, the status words and the *Others*
line are unchanged. The boxes above the tables still say "0 of 0 leaders".

## Why

The owner wanted plainer words a leader reads as a request. The deadline stays on *Update
Records*, because a leader who records their own work learns it there. The app's own names stay:
*branch* rather than *team* (decision 0318), and *DCC Sunday* rather than *Sunday service*.

On a table where most rows owe nothing, "0 of 0 leaders" repeated down two columns hid the rows
that need following up. A dash and a grey *Nothing owed* let those rows stand out without
ordering by a figure, which Section 19 forbids, and without colour carrying a status: the words
carry it, the grey and the bold only weight it.
