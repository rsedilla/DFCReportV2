# 2026-11-04 — A leader's people are "in your care"

The sentences a leader reads about their own people said "under you", "beneath them" and "you
oversee", which read as rank rather than as pastoring. On 2026-11-04 the owner asked for them to be
reworded in a warmer, ministry tone, starting with My People's count, which they wrote as "37 people
in your network. Pray for more workers in His harvest." "Network" already names the Men's and the
Women's Network in this application (Section 4), so "in your network" would read as one of those;
the owner chose "in your care" instead, from an audit of the sentences a leader sees.

## The ruling

**1. A sentence telling a leader about their own people says "in your care".** It replaces "under
you", "beneath them" and "you oversee" in these sentences:

- My People: "*N* people in your care", and under it "Pray for more workers in His harvest.", in place
  of the count of those directly under the reader and beneath them; with nobody, "No one in your care
  yet. Pray for the people God will bring."
- The Cells page, for a leader: "*N* Cell Groups in your care", and "*M* you lead · *N − M* led by your
  leaders · as of today".
- SUYNL: "*N* people in your care. Start Up Your New Life (SUYNL): ten lessons, taken one to one."
- Recording Progress, for a leader: "How your leaders are doing with their records, week by week or
  month by month."
- The empty lists: Record, "All recorded. Thank you for your faithfulness!"; Pending CG Records,
  "Every Cell Group in your care is up to date."; Pending DCC Records, "Every leader in your care is up
  to date on DCC."; No Leader Yet, "Everyone in your care has a leader."; No CG Yet, "Everyone in your
  care is in a Cell Group."; Absent 3+ Months, "No one in your care has been away three months. Praise
  God!"; My Cell, "You don't lead a Cell Group yet. Request one and it will be sent for approval."

**2. Decision 0317's labels are unchanged.** The name of the reader's whole scope on a switch, a
picker or a report heading stays *People you oversee*, or *Whole Church*; this ruling words the
sentences around it. A reader holding a list's capability church-wide, such as the Admin, reads
*in the church* where a leader reads *in your care*, except on My People, which is always the
reader's own people, as in "Every leader in the church is up to
date on DCC.", and the Cells page keeps its own whole-church words.

**3. Nothing a figure counts changes.** Every count and list is the one it was.

## Why

"In your care" says what a leader is to the people listed, which is the point of the work they record,
and it cannot be read as the Men's or the Women's Network. The other sentences a leader sees are
instructions on the recording screens, which explain a rule rather than encourage, and stay plain.
