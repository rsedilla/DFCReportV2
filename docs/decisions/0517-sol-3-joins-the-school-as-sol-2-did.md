# 2026-11-27 — SOL 3 joins the school as SOL 2 did, in one step, with one book and only the Admin ticking a pass from before it

Owner rulings of 2026-11-27 ("A, A, A"). Decision 0501 ruled SOL 1 to 3 (points 17 to 19), and
decisions 0514 and 0516 built SOL 1 and SOL 2. What 0501 ruled for SOL 3 stands unchanged and is
not restated here: ten weekly lessons and the same checklist grade, SOL 2 passed, the leadership of
an `ACTIVE` Cell now and twelve direct disciples now to enroll, a Guide who has passed SOL 3 and has
at least three of their own direct disciples each leading an `ACTIVE` Cell now, and a SOL 3
Coordinator ticked on a Full view account. This settles the three questions building it raised.

**1. Only the Admin ticks SOL 3 once its first class opens** (owner, "A, Admin only"), as for SOL
1 and SOL 2. Leaders tick SOL 3 on the Training tab as now until the first SOL 3 class opens, a
cancelled one not counting. From then on, only the Admin ticks or corrects a *Before the school*
SOL 3, with a reason, and a posted *Passed* files SOL 3 *From the school*. Once all three SOL
levels are the school's, a leader ticks nothing on the Training tab.

**2. The SOL 3 book is one book** (owner, "A, one book"), as SOL 2's is: the *Has the SOL 3 book*
the Registrar ticks at sign-up, with the date, is the book the student must read to pass, and the
sign-up form says it is the tuition, paid outside DREAMS.

**3. SOL 3 is built in one step** (owner, "A, one PR"). SOL 2 built everything a SOL level needs,
so SOL 3 adds its level, its Coordinator, its requirements and its Guide condition to it, and no
SOL 3 class can open before its register and final grades exist, there being no gap between the
two.

**It is built** (migration `0066_equipping_sol_3.sql`), from screenshots the owner approved on
2026-11-27. *Registration*, *Enrollment*, *Classes* and *Final Grades* carry a *SOL 3* pill. The
Register form reads *SOL 2 passed*, *Leads a Cell now* and *12 disciples now* from the records, the
twelve said as "needs 12 disciples under them in DREAMS" and never as a count, and has the Registrar
tick *Has the SOL 3 book*. The Admin ticks *SOL 3 Coordinator* on the Account view, offered and kept
only with Full view, which the database holds too, and the *Coordinators* list carries a SOL 3 row.
A SOL 3 class's register, quiz and book, final grades and change after posting are SOL 1's, and a
posted *Passed* files SOL 3 *From the school*. The Guide search for a SOL 3 class lists only a SOL 3
graduate with three of their own disciples each leading an `ACTIVE` Cell now, saying "Passed SOL 3,
with 3 Cell Leaders under them", and naming anybody else is refused. Once every SOL level is the
school's, a leader's Training tab reads "Every level now comes from the school. The Admin ticks one
for someone who finished before it."
