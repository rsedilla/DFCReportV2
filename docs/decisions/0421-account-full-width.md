# 2026-11-02 — Account is as wide as a list, and lists no permissions

The Account page was kept at 768px by decision 0410, being read from top to bottom, and it ended
with *What you can do*: every permission the account holds, in plain words, grouped by how far
each reaches. The owner found the list long and unneeded, and the page narrow on a laptop, and
chose this from side-by-side screenshots on laptop and phone.

## The ruling

**1. Account is as wide as a list (1280px) on a laptop**, with *Username*, *Your email* and *Your
password* side by side and *Sign out* below them. On a phone they stay one under another. This
replaces point 5 of decision 0410 for Account; *Where they sit* keeps 768px.

**2. Account no longer lists what the account may do.** The server checks every action, and the
list read as a set of settings to change. Its line under the heading now says who is signed in
and how they sign in.

**3. Account draws no tab row**: its one tab named the page itself, as decision 0420 says of
Growth. The page keeps its frame.
