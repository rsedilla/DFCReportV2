# 2026-10-19 — The recording screens' My Cell item is labelled Cells

Decision 0323 gave a Leader account without Full view the sidebar `Record · People · My Cell ·
SUYNL`, `My Cell` opening the reader's own Cells. On 19 Oct the owner asked for the item to read
`Cells`.

## The ruling

1. **The item is labelled `Cells`.** It still opens the same page, the reader's own Cells, and
   that page keeps its title, *My Cell*. Nothing else about the page changes.
2. **The person page's Full view hint follows it**: without Full view, a person "sees the
   recording screens: Record, People, Cells and SUYNL."
3. **Two accounts' `Cells` items open different pages, and no account has both.** A Full view
   leader's and the Admin's `Cells` opens the Cells module (Section 15); a Recording-only
   account's opens its own Cells. The label names what the item is about, which is the same for
   both, and the page says which it is.

Nothing changes about what any account may see or do.

## Why

Every other sidebar item is a plain noun naming a module, and *My Cell* was the one that read
as a page title. A leader on the recording screens who becomes a Full view leader now finds the
same word in the same place.
