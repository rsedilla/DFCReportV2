# 2026-09-22 — Any current Person may have run a Cell meeting, and the screen asks

Decision 0199 refused a `facilitated_by` naming nobody and left open what it may name beyond a
Person who exists: any Person, a member of that Cell, or someone in the leader's subtree. No
screen asked who ran a meeting, so the leader was always recorded. The owner's Claude Design
has no such field. The owner chose this answer from a drawing of it.

## The ruling

**Any current Person may be recorded as having run a Cell meeting**: one who exists and is
neither archived nor absorbed by a merge. That is what Section 10 asks of a Cell member, and the
submission refuses the rest as `INVARIANT_VIOLATION`.

**The recording screen asks "Who ran the meeting?"** on a first record of a meeting that met.
It defaults to the leader and sends nothing; "Someone else" opens the church-wide person picker.

## Why

Section 13's own examples are a disciple or an upline leader, and Section 10 lets a Cell hold
people outside its leader's tree. A narrower rule would refuse a visiting pastor. No report
counts a facilitator, so a wider rule costs nothing a figure depends on.

A correction does not change who ran a meeting, so the question is asked on a first record only.
How a wrong one is corrected, and at which date a facilitator must be current, are open in
`CLAUDE.md`. A named facilitator writes a `cell_meeting.facilitator_recorded` audit entry,
which Section 21 lists.
