# 2026-10-14 — Unsaved attendance marks are kept on the device

A leader marking a Cell meeting or a DCC Sunday on a phone lost every mark when they tapped a
link, reloaded, or the phone closed the tab in the background. Section 23 listed offline draft
storage on the device as deferred until required. The owner chose on 14 Oct to keep these marks
(option C, both halves).

## The ruling

**Leaving a Cell meeting or a DCC Sunday with marks not yet saved asks first**, whether by a
link inside the application or by closing or reloading the tab.

**The marks are also kept on the device**, and offered back with a note and a Discard the next
time the same screen opens for the same person. They are never sent until the leader saves.

**What is kept, and for how long.** Marks only: no note, reason or who ran a meeting. Each draft
belongs to the person signed in and is never offered to another. It is deleted when it is
saved or discarded, when somebody signs out or signs in on the device, and when the server ends
the session. It is not offered back after three weeks, and a mark made against a record that has
changed since is not offered back.

The rest of Section 23's deferred list stands.

## Why

A question before leaving cannot help when the phone closes the tab itself or the back button
is pressed, and a kept draft alone would let a leader walk away believing the marks were saved.
The limits are what a shared phone and the records of minors call for: the marks name nobody,
and they go with the session.
