# 2026-11-11 — A Network Admin's Cell powers reach every Cell of their Network

Owner ruling, 2026-11-11, two questions answered one at a time while a Network Admin's Cell
powers were built (decision 0469, point 4). It amends Section 7.

## The ruling

1. **A Cell led by a Network root, a Senior Pastor or an administrator is a Cell like any
   other.** A Network Admin adds and removes its ordinary members, changes its schedule and
   category, decides a handover of it, and closes it.
2. **Closing a Cell, or approving a request, may end or move the membership of a Person the
   Network Admin does not otherwise change.** A closure decides for every member, and an
   approval places the members a request names. Adding or removing such a Person as a member
   directly stays refused.

## Why

Section 7 says a Person holding `SENIOR_PASTOR`, `ADMIN` or `NETWORK_ADMIN`, and a Network
root, "is read and never changed", and lists edit, archive, restore and move. It did not say
whether a Cell such a Person leads, or a Cell they are a member of, is within a Network
Admin's Cell powers.

On the first, the owner had already ruled that a Network Admin records the meetings of a Cell
a root leads (decision 0469, point 5). Sending every new member of a root's Cell to the
whole-church Admin would undo the delegation the role exists for. The owner was told that
closing such a Cell, or approving its handover, ends that leadership without the application
asking the leader, and was offered keeping those two with the Admin.

On the second, the owner was shown both answers and that this one lets a closure do what a
direct removal may not. The owner chose it: the act is on the Cell, and a closure cannot
complete without a decision about every member (Section 10).

Considered and rejected: refusing a closure or an approval that would move such a Person,
which keeps the rule without exception and lets one member block an ordinary closure.

## What is owed

Nothing. The Cell powers were built this way, with tests of the direct refusals.
