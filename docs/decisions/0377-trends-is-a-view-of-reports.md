# 2026-10-20 — The Senior Pastors' Trends is a view of Reports

Decision 0326 gave the two Senior Pastors three sidebar items, `Record · Reports · Trends`.
`Reports` showed their five figures by period and `Trends` the same five over twelve months, under
the same five tabs. On 20 Oct the owner clicked through a mock of the two folded into one, on the
real screens, and chose it.

## The ruling

1. **The Senior Pastors' sidebar is `Record · Reports`.** There is no `Trends` item.
2. **Under `Reports`' five tabs, a switch reads *By period* and *Last 12 months*.** *By period* is
   the screen `Reports` was: Weekly to Year, the arrows, *Figures for*, the Whole Church boxes and
   the Primaries tables. *Last 12 months* is the screen `Trends` was: *Show* with every Primary, the
   graph, Line or Bars, the tick boxes and *Show the figures*. Switching keeps the tab.
3. **Nothing either view shows changes**, and *Last 12 months* keeps its address, `/trends`,
   where the sidebar now marks `Reports`. Decision 0327's colours on the graph stand (Section 23).
4. **Only the Senior Pastors' screens offer the switch.** Giving a Full view leader a twelve-month
   view of their own branch is a separate change, for which the API owes a branch-scoped figure.

This replaces decision 0326's point 4 as to where `Trends` sits; what it draws stands.

## Why

The two items were one set of figures in two places, and a pastor had to choose a menu before
choosing a figure. With the switch they choose the figure first and then how far back to look,
and a later tier given the graph gains a view of `Reports` rather than another sidebar item.
