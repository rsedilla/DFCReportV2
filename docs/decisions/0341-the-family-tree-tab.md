# 2026-10-13 — The Family Tree tab

Owner ruling, 2026-10-13, chosen one question at a time from drawings and real previews of a
Full view leader's screens on the demo. It amends Section 17.

## The ruling

**1. A Full view leader and an Admin have a third tab of People, `Family Tree`, after People
and Branch.** The Senior Pastors' screens and a Leader account without Full view do not have it
(decision 0323).

**2. It is an outline of the reader's branch as it stands now.** The reader comes first, opened
on their direct disciples; each line opens that person's direct disciples in place, to any
depth. Everyone is listed by name, whether or not they lead anybody. Each line reads its direct
disciples and everyone beneath, which Section 5 keeps apart, or "leads nobody yet". A name opens
the person's page.

**3. It reads the Branch screen's routes under `people.view_subtree`** and carries two of Branch's
headcounts and no other figure or action, so it shows nothing Branch does not. A reader holding no pastoral assignment
starts at the Network roots their scope reaches, as Branch does (decision 0268).

## Why

The owner wants each leader to see their branch at a glance, generation by generation, where
Branch shows one level at a time with figures and Move. A tab of its own keeps the two jobs
apart, and fits a phone. It is kept to Full view leaders, the Primaries and those given Full
view, rather than every leader with an account.
