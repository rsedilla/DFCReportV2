# 2026-10-17 — The Primaries' report tabs say Reports, sized to their words

Since decision 0360 a Full view leader's `Reports` has three tabs, CG Attendance, DCC Attendance
and SUYNL. They were stretched across the whole row as equal buttons (decisions 0292 and 0340),
three labels spread over the width meant for five. On 17 Oct the owner chose from real
screenshots to size them to their words and to name each one a report.

## The ruling

1. **A Full view leader's three `Reports` tabs read *CG Attendance Reports*, *DCC Attendance
   Reports* and *SUYNL Reports*.**
2. **They are sized to their words, in one row on the left**, in the tab style People, Growth and
   Cells use, rather than equal buttons filling the row. On a phone a label wraps inside its tab.
3. Only those three tabs change. Each report's own page, its address and what it shows are as
   before. A whole-church reader's five and the Senior Pastors' five keep their names and their
   equal widths (decisions 0292 and 0326).

## Why

Three tabs stretched across the row read as a frame rather than tabs. Sized to their words they
match the other tab rows. The owner wanted each tab to say plainly that it opens a report.
