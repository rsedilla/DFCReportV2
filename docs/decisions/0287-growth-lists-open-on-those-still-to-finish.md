# 2026-09-24 — Growth lists open on those still to finish

The `SUYNL` and `Training` lists showed everyone in name order, so people who had finished
took a row each and pushed those still working through the pages. With a hundred people in
someone's care, a leader would page past the finished ones to find who still needs lessons.

The owner chose this answer from a drawing of three options.

## The ruling

**The `SUYNL` list opens on the people short of ten lessons, and the `Training` list on those
short of all five schools**, still in name order. A line says how many who have finished are
left out, with a way to show everyone and back. A card, a search or "Only my disciples" shows
whoever it names, finished or not, and the counts still cover everyone the tab lists.

## Why

It keeps the people who need something in front of the leader without hiding anybody: the
line names what is left out, so nobody looks lost. Putting the finished last was considered
and not taken, because on a long list they would sit on the final pages with nothing on the
first saying they exist.
