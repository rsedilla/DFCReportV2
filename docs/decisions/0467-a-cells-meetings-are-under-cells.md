# 2026-11-10 — A Cell's meetings are under Cells, with a simpler top

A Cell's meeting screens sat under `Record` wherever they were reached from (ruling of
2026-09-14, decision 0245), so a leader who opened a Cell from `Cells` or *My Cell* (decision 0463) landed on
a page whose sidebar marked `Record`, under Record's name and tab, with a *Back to Cells* link,
a sentence explaining the page, *led by* in the subtitle, a *Members* button in a bar, and the
month picker above everything. On 2026-11-10 the owner was shown the page today beside the
proposal, on a laptop and a phone, and chose the proposal.

## The ruling

1. **A Cell's meeting screens are `Cells`'**, its list of meetings and the screen a meeting is
   recorded on, for every account with a `Cells` item: the sidebar marks `Cells`, which on the
   recording screens is the item that opens *My Cell* (decision 0323). **The Senior Pastors keep them under `Record`**, which
   is where they reach them, from Update Records, having no `Cells` item (decisions 0326 and
   0379). This replaces the 2026-09-14 ruling for every other account.
2. **The list of meetings has no section name or tab above it.** It opens on a small arrow back,
   naming where it goes: *Cells*, *My Cell* on the recording screens, or *Update Records* for
   the Senior Pastors. The sidebar already says which section it is in.
3. **The sentence explaining the page is behind an ⓘ from a tablet up, and is not shown on a
   phone**, as decision 0454 puts the leaders' explanations.
4. **The subtitle is the Cell ID and the leader's name**, without *led by*, and the day it closed
   for a closed Cell.
5. **The way to the members is a pill under the subtitle**, *👥 2 members ›*, or *Members ›* for a
   closed Cell, which has none. The *Members* button is removed.
6. **The month picker heads the box of meetings it changes**, the box keeping a heading for
   screen readers only.
7. **On a phone, the list's dates read *June 13***, the month then the day, without the weekday
   the Cell's name already gives, and without the time unless it differs from the schedule's.

It changes which sidebar item a page marks and how the page is laid out. It adds no route, reads
nothing it did not read before, and changes who may open, record or configure nothing:
*Change when it meets* and *Change category* are still offered only to an account holding
`cell.manage_configuration`.

## Why

A leader who reached their Cell from `Cells` or *My Cell* found the sidebar telling them they
were somewhere else, and the top of the page took most of a phone's screen before the first
meeting. The page now opens the way a detail page in a phone app does: back, the Cell, its
members, its meetings.

Considered and rejected: moving every account's meeting screens to `Cells`, which would leave
the Senior Pastors on a page whose sidebar marks nothing, since they have no `Cells` item.

Not part of it: the same treatment for the single meeting page and the Members page, which the
owner offered and has not agreed. The single meeting page now marks `Cells` too, by point 1,
and keeps its section name, tab and *Back to this Cell's meetings* link.
